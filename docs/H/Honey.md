# Honey

[[File:HoneyDrop.png|thumb|The honey icon.]]
{{Color|Honey}} is the main [[:Category:Currency|currency]] in [[Bee Swarm Simulator]].

== Overview ==
=== Usage ===
{{Color|Honey}} can be used to purchase many [[items]] such as [[tools]], [[bags]], and in-game [[:Category:Accessories|accessories]] such as [[:Category:Guards|guards]] and [[:Category:Gliding Tools|gliding tools]], and to play [[Memory Match]]. Honey can also be used to purchase products such as {{Cost|Ticket||4=}}, {{Cost|Basic Egg||4=}}, {{Cost|Royal Jelly||4=}}, {{Cost|Treat||4=}}, and [[Hive Slot|hive slots]]. In addition, Honey used along with {{Cost|Ticket||4=}}, could have been used to purchase [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Areas|Market Boosts]] from the [[Boost Market]], which is now removed. The price of many [[:Category:Consumables|consumables]] increases with each purchase, for example, {{Cost|Basic Egg||4=}}.

=== Obtaining Honey ===
Honey is usually obtained by converting [[Pollen]] into it. It can be converted at the [[Hive]] or (partly) instantly converted using an [[Instant Converter]] or with a {{Cost|Micro-Converter||4=1}}, catching a falling {{Cost|Coconut||4=1}} or Science Bear's falling [[Beesmas Lights|Beesmas Light]] (only during Beesmas), activating [[Passive Abilities#Coin Scatter|Coin Scatter]] and/or [[Passive Abilities#Diamond Drain|Diamond Drain]]. Honey can also be obtained by completing [[quests]], redeeming [[codes]], defeating [[mobs]], and collecting [[Sparkles]] and [[Goo]] from [[fields]]. [[Vicious Bee]]'s [[Ability Tokens#Impale|Impale]] ability spawns a honey token if it hits a [[mob]]. [[Festive Bee]]'s [[Ability Tokens#Festive Gift|festive gift]] ability always spawns Honey tokens. Some other [[bees]] have the ability to give a [[Ability Tokens#Honey Gift|Honey Gift]] or a [[Ability Tokens#Token Link|Token Link]]. Certain [[:Category:Hats|hats]], guards, and [[amulet]]s have the ability to [[Instant Conversion|instantly convert]] a percentage of pollen into honey. Popping [[bubble]]s, spawning a [[flame]], having field conversion such as [[Field Wind]]s, collecting a [[Ability Tokens#Bomb|Bomb token]] produced by a bee can result in instant conversion as well. The player can also get honey for participating in the [[Ant Challenge]]. Honey tokens can appear when the player collects pollen in a [[Fields|field]] and can be obtained from [[Honeystorms]]. The [[Mythic Meteor Shower]] awards Honey tokens after catching a meteor. The [[Honey Dispenser]] is also an option, however, it is only available to those who are in the [https://www.roblox.com/groups/3982592/Bee-Swarm-Simulator-Club#!/about Bee Swarm Simulator Club].

== [[System Page#Convert Rate|Convert Rate]] ==
Bees convert pollen into Honey at a set rate. For example, [[Basic Bee]] makes 80 honey in 4 seconds, translating into a conversion rate of 20 honey per second. [[Badges]], accessories, [[Robux Shop#Gamepasses|gamepasses]], {{Cost|Enzymes||4=1}}, and [[Buffs & Debuffs#From NPCs|Science Enhancement]] can increase the convert rate. The production rate stacks and adds up. There is currently no convert rate cap in the game.
