# Honeyday Candles

{{RemovedContent}}
[[File:Honeyday Candles.png|thumb|The Honeyday Candles after completing [[Gifted Riley Bee|Gifted Riley Bee's]] Beesmas Quest.]]
The '''Honeyday Candles''' are a Beesmas-exclusive machine which is unlocked after completing [[Gifted Riley Bee]]'s Honeyday Candles [[Quests|quest]].

When activated, 3 random level 10 red [[bees]] are summoned for 30 minutes, and a random [[Waxes|wax]] is spawned. They have a cooldown of 4 hours, and are located next to the [[Red HQ]]'s interior ladder.

When a player stands on top of the pad before completing the quest, a red text box appears on the top of the screen reading: <span style="background-color:#a61e19;color:white">The red bees are having trouble lighting the candles...</span>

Bees summoned by the Honeyday Candles can collect [[pollen]], attack [[mobs]], and convert honey for the player. They can also convert pollen at the hive now, using a random bee's hive slot.

The Honeyday Candles summon 3 bees from the following pool, each with increasing rarity:

*[[File:Fire Bee.png|40px]] [[Fire Bee]].
*[[File:Demon Bee.png|40px]] [[Demon Bee]].
*[[File:Spicy Bee.png|40px]] [[Spicy Bee]].

==Trivia==
*A {{Cost|Hard Wax|||0}} token used to be found hidden behind the candles.
* These are one of the four machines that grants the player temporary bees, the others being [[Onett's Lid Art]], [[Gummy Beacon]], and the [[Honey Wreath]] (after completing [[Honey Bee (NPC)|Honey Bee's]] Beesmas quest).
* There is a glitch when the player leaves, the bees generated would stay in place until they despawned.
**Even when the player joins the same server, the bees will not despawn.
*They were introduced in the [[Updates#2020.12.25|Beesmas 2020 event]] and returned in all Beesmas updates since.
*During Beesmas 2020, using these candles were one of the two only ways of getting {{Cost|Swirled Wax}}, the other way is by giving Gifted Riley Bee a {{Cost|Present|||0}} during that same event.
** For every Beesmas event succeeding Beesmas 2020, the Honeyday Candles would no longer guarantee a Swirled Wax.
*This is the only machine that can summon bees who produce [[Passive Abilities#Gathering Flames|flames]].
*Loot Luck does not affect Honeyday Candles drops.
*Summoned bees don't have to rest, even if you die.
