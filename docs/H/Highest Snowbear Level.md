The '''Highest Snowbear Level Leaderboard''' was, at the time, one of the [[leaderboards]] in the game. It was added on the [[Updates#2020.12.25|12/25/2020 update]] and returned on [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]] 2021,  2022 and 2024. It is next to the [[Spider Field]], [[Bamboo Field]], [[Gumdrop Shop]], [[Snowbear Summoner]], [[Basic Bee Gate]], and [[Panda Bear]]. To summon a [[Snowbear]], the player must complete Panda Bear's Beesmas [[Quests|quest]] to gain access to the summoner, allowing them to use the Snowbear Summoner. Each time the player defeats the Snowbear, its level increases by one. The leaderboard displays the players who have the highest top 100 Snowbear levels. This leaderboard does not have a white pad to stand on. 

== Trivia ==
* This is the second leaderboard to be removed, the first being the [[Most Commando Captures]] leaderboard.
** This is one of two leaderboards that were temporarily removed, the other being the [[Highest Robo Party Cake Rank]] leaderboard.
*** Both of these leaderboards reappear and reset each Beesmas.
