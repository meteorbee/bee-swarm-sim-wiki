*Enzymes}}The {{Color|Heat-Treated Planter}} is a reusable [[Planter]]. It grows in 12 in-game hours and stores around 10 billion pollen. It grants bonus {{Cost|Red Extract}} and {{Cost|Enzymes}}. It is purchasable in the [[Red HQ]]. The player also needs to complete 150 [[Gifted Riley Bee]] quests for it to be unlocked.

It grows 50% faster in fields with red flowers. Red bees are 50% more likely to sip [[Nectar]] from it, but blue bees are 50% less likely to sip from it. This planter grants 40% more Invigorating and Motivating Nectar. Harvesting a fully-grown Heat-Treated Planter has a 1/6 (16.66%) chance to spawn a [[Puffshroom]].

The Heat-Treated Planter drops extra {{Cost|Red Extract|}} in the [[Mushroom Field]], [[Strawberry Field]], [[Rose Field]], and the [[Pepper Patch]].

==Trivia == 
*This and the {{Cost|Hydroponic Planter|||0}} had models introduced in Beesmas 2021, but they weren't added as an obtainable item until Beesmas 2022. 
**This and the Hydroponic Planter were the newest planters to be added.
*This planter is the red counterpart of the Hydroponic Planter, and its white counterpart is the Petal Planter.
*This is one of the five reusable planters to reside outside of [[Dapper Bear's Shop]], the others being the {{Cost|Red Clay Planter|||0}}, {{Cost|Blue Clay Planter|||0}}, {{Cost|Petal Planter|||0}}, and Hydroponic Planter.
** This and the Red Clay Planter are the only planters to be sold from the Red HQ.
