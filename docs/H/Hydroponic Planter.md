The {{Color|Hydroponic Planter}} is a reusable [[planter]]. Alone it grows in 12 in-game hours and stores around 10 billion pollen, as well as granting bonus {{Cost|Blue Extract}} and {{Cost|Oil}}. It is purchasable in the [[Blue HQ]]. It requires the player to complete 150 [[Gifted Bucko Bee]] quests for it to be unlocked.

When planted in a field, it grows 50% faster in fields with blue flowers. Blue bees are 50% more likely to sip [[Nectar]] while red bees are 50% less likely to sip. This planter grants 40% more Comforting and Refreshing Nectar. Harvesting a fully-grown Hydroponic Planter has a 1/6 (16.66%) chance of spawning a [[Puffshroom]].

The Hydroponic Planter drops extra {{Cost|Blue Extract}} in the [[Blue Flower Field]], the [[Bamboo Field]], the [[Pine Tree Forest]], and the [[Stump Field]].
