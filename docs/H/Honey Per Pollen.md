# Honey Per Pollen

'''Honey Per Pollen''' is a [[Mechanics|mechanic]] that increases the amount of [[honey]] earned when converting [[pollen]]. For example, if a player has 10 pollen in their [[Bags|container]], with a 110% Honey Per Pollen multiplier, they would receive 11 honey if converted. 20 to 22, etc. To determine how much honey will be earned when converting pollen, multiply bag space ''B'', by Honey Per Pollen percentage.

Similar to many other boosts, (for example, [[Instant Conversion]]) the player can always check their current percentage of Honey Per Pollen on the [[System Page]]. It starts off at 100% and increases later on when the player gets more and more Honey Per Pollen boosts.

Honey Per Pollen, along with other boosts like Instant Conversion, [[goo]], and extra capacity, is one of the best ways to get maximum efficiency when producing honey. These boosts, when combined with each other, are extremely helpful overall. It can affect the player's condition when, for example, grinding or converting your [[Balloon|hive balloon]].

Keep in mind that there is a difference between Instant Conversion and Honey Per Pollen. While both help with honey production, Instant Conversion converts pollen collected in [[fields]] into honey instantly. However, Honey Per Pollen affects the amount of honey the player makes for every pollen.

== Other Honey per Pollen ==
As there are multiple method to converting pollen into honey, there are other variant of Honey per Pollen that applies from converting in one of those method, such as converting honey at hive and [[Instant Conversion]]. Similar to Honey per Pollen they start off at 100%.

Each Honey per Pollen variant stacks multiplicatively with Honey per Pollen, and other Honey per Pollen variant if possible. For example, with 110% Honey per Pollen and 150% Honey at Hive, the player is effectively having 165% Honey per Pollen only when converting pollen to honey at hive.

=== Honey from Instant Conversion ===
'''Honey from Instant Conversion''' is a Honey per Pollen that applies when pollen is converted by any [[Instant Conversion]], that is instant conversion itself, [[Instant Converter]], and anything that converts pollen from capacity except converting pollen at hive. Honey from Instant Conversion stat can't be found in [[System Page]] and the source is very limited.
