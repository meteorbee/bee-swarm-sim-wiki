# Hive Hub

<blockquote>''Not to be confused with the [[Hub Field]].''</blockquote>

The '''Hive Hub''' is a unique location added in the [[Updates#2024.01.12|1/12/2024]] update. It is inside the Hive Hub Portal, which is beyond the [[Ant Gate]]. It has several features, including a total of 36 hives for players to claim, the [[Public Sticker Board]], the [[Sticker-Seeker]], the [[Hub Field]], a [[Beequip]] Storage chest, and [[Sprout|Sticker Sprouts]] that spawns every 3 hours. The Hive Hub's purpose is to seek out opportunities to trade with other players and to craft the [[Sticker-Seeker]], a tool that specializes in collecting stickers and unlocks the [[Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine|Sticker-Seeker's quests]]. The Hive Hub also has a field, aptly named the Hub Field, which instantly converts all pollen collected into honey, as well as [[flowers]] regrowing very often, so there is no need for Sprinklers. [[Bees]] do not spawn in the Hive Hub to reduce lag. In the Summer Beesmas 2024 event, an obby made out of circular platforms was added. At the end of the obby, you jump up onto a hexagonal platform which has three tokens: a {{Cost|Smooth Dice|4=0}}, a {{Cost|Red Balloon|4=0}}, and a {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|4=0}}, as well as the Celestial Gift Box which is able to be opened after helping 12 NPC's complete their [[:Category:Beesmas Decorations|Beesmas decorations]].

== Tips ==
* Sharing {{Cost|Jelly Beans}} is possible here. This makes it easy to share Jelly Beans for quests, the sharing buff, or other purposes.
* Be aware that the Hive Hub isn't very well optimized (Bee Swarm Simulator wasn't made to handle this much data at once), so it's best to have a decent device and set your graphics to 1 in order to avoid crashing and major framedrops.

== Restricted Items ==
The following items are not usable in the Hive Hub:
* [[File:Sprinkler builder.png|25x25px]] [[Sprinklers]]
* {{Cost|Snowflake}}
* {{Cost|Stinger}} (This means that [[Passive Abilities#Star Saw|Star Saw]] cannot be activated here).
* {{Cost|Cloud Vial}}
* {{Cost|Field Dice}}, {{Cost|Smooth Dice}}, and {{Cost|Loaded Dice}}.
* {{Cost|Whirligig}}
* {{Cost|Night Bell}}
* Every type of [[Balloon]].
* Every type of [[Planter]].

== Trivia ==
*The Hive Hub is the only field in which your Bees don't spawn.
*Placing a [[planter]] will prompt, “Cannot use (type) Planter in Challenge” despite the hub not being a challenge area or field.
* The [[Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine]], [[Public Sticker Board|Sticker Board]], [[Gift Boxes]], & other Hive Hub structures can be seen behind the Bee Swarm Mountain, beyond the TP barrier at the edge of the base plate on the main map.
* It's possible to enter the Hive Hub without having 20 bees, since the 20 Bee Zone teleport barrier doesn't cover the left side of the area due to the teleport barrier never being increased after the area was expanded. Players can climb the wall to the left of the [[Ant Gate]], then following the wall to the left, they need to jump down. They should find themself behind the [[Stinger Shop]]. After that, the player just needs to jump while hugging the wall in front of them, allowing them to gain enough height to be able to climb over the fence, and once they are climbing it, they can either climb over it or fit in the middle hole of the fence.
**Alternatively, you can just join anybody who is in the Hive Hub directly from their profile without meeting the requirements.
*Before a natural Sticker Sprout spawns, the following message will be displayed: [🌱 A Sticker Sprout is about to spawn in (Public Server) Hive Hubs… 🌱 ]
**The Retro Swarm Challenge has the [Public Server] Part, although it technically isn’t a Private Server.
*All of the main machines/features of the Hive Hub seem to be behind the entrance portal.
