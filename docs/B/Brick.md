# Brick

{{Consumables|description=Used for purchases during the Retro Challenge|cooldown=N/A|image=Lego Brick.png}}
The {{Color|Brick|||0}} is used as the main currency of the [[Retro Swarm Challenge]]. It is used to buy many utilities to fend off many waves of slimes and zombies during the challenge.

==Ways to Obtain ==

*Farming in the Red, Blue, White, and Mixed Brick Field.
*Destroying [[Brick Bloom|Brick Blooms]] (Amount depends on the level).
*Slaying [[Slime|Slimes]] and [[Zombie|Zombies]] (Amount depends on the level).
*{{Cost|Brick|10}} can be purchased for honey near the hives.
*{{Cost|Brick|100}} can be purchased for honey on the left side of the monster's spawn.

===The methods below are no longer possible.===

*Completing [[Bubble Bee Man|BBM's Naughty List]] during Summer Beesmas 2024 rewarded {{Cost|Brick|1||}}.
*Giving [[Bubble Bee Man]] a present during Summer Beesmas 2024 rewarded {{Cost|Brick|2}}.
