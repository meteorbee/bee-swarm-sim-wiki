==Stats==
*x1.5 [[System Page#Blue Pollen|Blue Pollen]]
*x1.5 [[Capacity]]
*x1.25 [[Field Capacity|Blue Field Capacity]] 
*x1.25 [[Bubble#Bubble Pollen|Bubble Pollen]] 
*+30% [[System Page#Defense|Defense]]
*+15% [[System Page#Bee Ability Rate|Bee Ability Rate]]
*[[Passive Abilities#Bubble Bombs|+Passive: Bubble Bombs]]  
**Every 25th [[Ability Tokens#Bomb|bomb ability token]] spawns 25 [[Bubble]]s that last for 8 seconds. If a player touches them, they pop, collecting 3R/5W/10B pollen, increased by 15% per [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]] blue [[Bees|Bee]] type in the player's [[Hive]], from 29 nearby [[Flowers]] and causes them to grow. This passive has a 2 minute cooldown.

==Trivia==
*This, the [[Fire Mask]], [[Honey Mask]], [[Riley Guard]] and [[Bucko Guard]] all represent [[Bees/Epic|epic bees]].
*The Bubble Mask represents the [[Bubble Bee]], having an ability similar to it.
*Before the [[Updates#2019.04.05|4/5/2019 Update]], the Bubble Mask's stats were: 
**+25% [[System Page#Bee Gather Pollen|Bee Gather Pollen]]
** +25% [[System Page#Instant Conversion|Instant Conversion]]
**+30% Damage Reduction
**+11% Bee Ability Rate
**+115% Luck ([[System Page#Loot Luck|Loot Luck]])
**+25% [[System Page#Blue Bomb Pollen|Blue Bomb Pollen]]
**x1.15 [[System Page#Blue Pollen|Blue Pollen]].
*Before the [[Updates#2019.09.28|9/28/2019 Update]], the Bubble Mask didn't have any [[Passive Abilities|Passive Ability]]. Other stats were also changed.
**If the player owns the Bubble Mask, its passive is inherited by the [[Diamond Mask]].
*If a player were to [[Crafting|craft]] the ingredients to craft the item, it would take {{Cost|Honey|100,000,000}}, {{Cost|Pineapple|7,500}}, {{Cost|Blueberry|8,625}}, {{Cost|Strawberry|5,625}}, {{Cost|Sunflower Seed|1,250}}, {{Cost|Magic Bean|15}}, and {{Cost|Royal Jelly|1,125}}.
*The Bubbles from its Bubble Bombs passive have a unique sound effect when popped.
