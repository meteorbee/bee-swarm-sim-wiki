# Beesmas Feast

{{RemovedContent}}
[[File:Beesmas Feast2.png|alt=|thumb|170x170px|The Beesmas Feast's appearance after the player completes Polar Bear's Beesmas quest.]]The '''Beesmas Feast''' is a [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]]-exclusive machine which can be unlocked after completing [[Polar Bear|Polar Bear's]] Beesmas [[Quests|quest]]. 

It can be used every 90 minutes. When used, it grants the player [[bond]] for all of their [[bees]] and a variety of [[treats]]. It is positioned behind Polar Bear. The amount of bond and rewards are affected by the amount of [[Buffs & Debuffs#From NPCs|Polar Power]] the player currently has.

If a player walks onto the platform without completing his quest, it displays this message, <span style="background-color:#a61e19;color:white">"An empty plate... where’s the Beesmas Feast, Polar Bear?"</span>

==Trivia==

* A variety of foods can be seen on the Beesmas feast. These include:
** Bitterberries.
** Treats.
** A floret of broccoli.
** A carrot.
** A stew that seems to contain chives, onions, corn, peas, and meat.

*Polar Bear's feast design is reminiscent to Big Ol' Turkey Legs, a running gag within the [https://www.youtube.com/user/ThnxCya ThnxCya] community. 
**Polar Bear's dialogue after you complete his Beesmas 2020 quest directly referenced this, with the phrase "''A big ol' turkey and treat...''".
*[[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] stated on Discord that the Beesmas Feast was the Hostage Chicks from the [[Egg Hunt 2020]] event.
*The eating sound is a cut out section of the song "''[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SMWi7CLoZ2Q Nom Nom Nom Nom Nom Nom Nom]''", made by Parry Grip.
*The Beesmas Feast used to drop all the 4 treats but was changed in Beesmas 2022.
*It was introduced in [[Updates#12.25.2020|Beesmas 2020]], and has returned in every Beesmas since.
