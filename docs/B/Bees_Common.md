== Trivia ==
*The Common rarity and the [[Bees/Event|Event]] rarity are the only rarities of bees that can't be obtained by using a normal {{Cost|Royal Jelly|4=0}} or {{Cost|Star Jelly|4=0}}.
*Excluding [[Gifted Bee|gifted]] forms, this and [[Bees/Rare|Rare bees]] are the only rarity of bees that has a bee that can't spawn [[Ability Tokens|ability tokens]]. The rare bee that can't spawn ability tokens is [[Brave Bee]].
*The [[Hive Slot]] color for a '''Common Bee''' is brown, which may represent bronze.
*The Common rarity is the rarity with the least amount of bee types, having only 1 bee type.
*This rarity has only three ways of getting a bee of this rarity which are [[Egg#Basic Egg|Basic Eggs]], [[Egg#Star Egg|Star Eggs]], and the [[Royal Jelly#Specific Bee Jelly Variants|Basic Bee Jelly]].
