# Bee Bear's Catalog

{{RemovedContent}}
'''Bee Bear&#39;s Catalog''' is a limited-time shop that players could use to purchase various products during [[Updates#12.25.2020|Beesmas 2020]], 2021, 2022, Summer 2024, and Winter 2024.  It can be accessed through a side tab on the right side of the screen. Its description is: "Beesmas event shop! Purchase items for Gingerbread Bears and Snowflakes."

It would show the countdown until [[Beesmas]] ends, the countdown was located below the gingerbread icon used to open the shop.

The shop sells [[Egg|eggs]], bundles, Star and Atomic [[Treats]], [[Beequip|Beequips]], and [[Royal Jelly|Royal Jelly Variants]]. The player cannot purchase an item or bundle more than once.

Buying items from the Catalog was sometimes required in NPC quests like [[Bee Bear|Bee Bear's.]]

You could only buy items in this shop with {{Cost|Snowflake}} and {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear}}.

==Trivia == 

*Items from the Bee Bear&#39;s Catalog are all one-time purchases, meaning that the player can only buy them once and can't buy them again.
* In order to buy all the items from the catalog, the player would have needed: 
**{{Cost|Snowflake|29,460}} and {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|1,118}} during Beesmas 2020.
**{{Cost|Snowflake|55,571}} and {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|1,195}} during Beesmas 2021.
**{{Cost|Snowflake|107,635}} and {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|1,379}} during Beesmas 2022.
**{{Cost|Snowflake|111,729}} and {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|1,546}} during Beesmas 2024 of Summer.
**{{Cost|Snowflake|153,857}} and {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|1,619}} during Beesmas 2024 of Winter.
*Shortly after Beesmas Summer 2024 released, Onett reduced sticker requirements for some quests and made a few stickers purchasable in the catalog as well as making a few of them more common.  
**Since Onett didn't update every single server, some servers don't have the new sticker offers in the catalog.
**The next day, Onett updated the Hive Hubs to fix Bee Bear's 15th quest. Because all of these servers are updated to the latest version, you can easily go to the Hive Hubs if you don't want to keep looking for a server with the updated catalog.
*If the player bought something from the catalog, the system would say +[Item Name] (From [Item Name]). This was changed in Beesmas 2022 to simply say +[Item Name].
*This was the very first limited time shop or catalog in the game to exist.
*The 2020 Beesmas Pinecone Bundle used to have a scripting error, where it gave {{Cost|Glue|30}} instead of {{Cost|Enzymes|30}}.
*This was the only non-robux shop to have bundles sold in it.
*During Beesmas 2021, The Catalog's timer was extended with the release of Beesmas Part 2 to give the players more time to complete the new quests, since the Beesmas Part 2 update came late.
*Items bought in the Bee Bear&#39;s Catalog can exceed the items cap.
*Only Gingerbread Bears obtained in the same year as that year's Bee Bear Catalog can be used to buy items there. For example, leftover Gingerbread Bears from Beesmas 2020 cannot be used as currency in 2021's catalog, while those obtained in Beesmas 2021 can.   
**{{Cost|Gingerbread Bear}} from last year's Beesmas turn into {{Cost|Aged Gingerbread Bear}} when the next Beesmas event starts.
***{{Cost|Aged Gingerbread Bear}} do not change in the beesmas event following them becoming aged meaning they do not become two-year aged gingerbread bears
*This, the [[Gift Boxes|Mondo Gift Box]] and the [[leaderboards]] are the only non-robux ways to obtain a Gifted Mythic Egg.
*Its description has changed from "Winter" to "Beesmas" during [[Updates|Beesmas 2024]], most likely due to it releasing in the summer.
*During the summer of Beesmas 2024, the price on the Percussive Bundle of {{Cost|Snowflake|808}} (and {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|8}}) is a reference to the Roland TR-808 drum machine, commonly referred to simply as an "808". The 808 is the most popular drum machine in the history of music, being initially used in '80s new wave music, and since then, in nearly every rap or hip hop song ever made. Similarly, the 8 Gingerbread Bears are likely a reference to the Roland TR-08 Rhythm Composer, which is a digital re-release of the original drum machine, and/or it may refer to the Roland TR-8S, which also uses the same percussive sound.
*The format for the timer is DD:HH:SS (D for Days, H for Hours, and S for Seconds).
*The button is placed on the same place the Bear Bee Offer was placed during release, and has a similar appearance.
