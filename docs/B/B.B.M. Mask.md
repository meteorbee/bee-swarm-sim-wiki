}}The '''B.B.M. Mask''', or '''Bubble Bee Man Mask''', is a [[:Category:Hats|mask]] that was obtainable by completing [[Bubble Bee Man|Bubble Bee Man's]] [[Quests|quest.]], "Helping B.B.M." in Beesmas 2018. After completion of this quest, this hat automatically replaced any hat the player wore. Currently, players can reequip this mask by talking to BBM in the [[Bear Gate|30 Bee Zone]] (as long as the player does not own the [[Mondo B.B.M. Mask]]).

== Stats ==
* +75,000 [[Capacity]]
* +25 [[System Page#Convert Amount|Convert Amount]]
* +50% [[System Page#Honey from Tokens|Honey From Tokens]]
* +1 [[System Page#Blue Attack|Blue Bee Attack]]

== Trivia ==
* The B.B.M. Mask and the [[Mondo B.B.M. Mask]] were the first masks to be equipped by talking to an [[:Category:NPC|NPC]].
* The B.B.M. Mask is actually a [https://www.roblox.com/catalog/17735318/Bubble-Bee-Man Hat Accessory] on ROBLOX.
* This and the Mondo B.B.M. Mask are the only masks that cannot be purchased, and the only ones that do not have a description.
* After the [[Updates#2019.04.05|4/5/2019 Update]], this mask became unobtainable as the NPC that provided it, Bubble Bee Man, was removed from its former location behind the Global Top Battlers leaderboard. This and the Mondo B.B.M. Mask are the only unobtainable accessories in the game.
* The stats of the B.B.M. Mask have been changed three times.
* Wearing the B.B.M. Mask does not give access to [[Dapper Bear's Shop]].
