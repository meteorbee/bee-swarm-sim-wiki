# Beesmas Tree

{{RemovedContent}}
''<sup>Not to be confused with [[Beesmas Tree Hat]], a [[Beequip]] with a similar name.</sup>''
[[File:Beesmastree.png|thumb|The Beesmas Tree.]]
The '''Beesmas Tree''' is a feature that appeared during Beesmas 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, and 2024. The Beesmas Tree allows the player to view their collected [[ornaments]], who they are from, and what boosts they give. The tree is located in front of the [[Ant Gate]], near the [[Noob Shop]], and next to the hives.


==Beesmas 2024 (Winter)==
The Beesmas Tree made another return in 2024's Winter Beesmas, functioning the exact same way as during Summer Beesmas 2024. Conducting a gift exchange with an NPC would grant players an ornament that would grant special boosts on the Beesmas Tree.

The bottom row holds five ornaments. They are given, from left to right, from [[Black Bear]], [[Mother Bear]], [[Brown Bear]], [[Panda Bear]], and [[Science Bear]]. The second row of the tree holds another five ornaments. They are, from left to right, from [[Polar Bear]], [[Dapper Bear]], [[Onett]],  [[Spirit Bear]], and [[Robo Bear]]. The third row of the tree holds four ornaments. They are, from left to right, from [[Gifted Riley Bee]], [[Gifted Bucko Bee]], [[Stick Bug]], and [[Sun Bear]]. The ornaments on the 4th row of the Beesmas Tree are currently unknown, though it can be assumed they are Honey Bee and Gummy Bear. The ornament on the top of the tree is [[Bubble Bee Man]]'s ornament.

There is a {{Cost|Neonberry|4=0}} token under the Beesmas Tree and 25 {{I|Snowflake}}{{Color|Snowflake|Snowflakes}} token at the top of the trunk of the Beesmas Tree.

==Beesmas 2020==
{{RemovedContent}}
The Beesmas Tree returned in 2020, and it functioned the same. To put ornaments on the tree the player needed to collect and give [[present]]s to each corresponding [[:Category:NPC|NPC]]. In exchange, the player would receive their ornament along with a few special boosts.
[[File:Gingerbread bear under beesmastree.png|thumb|The Gingerbread Bear under the Beesmas Tree.]]
The bottom row held four ornaments. They were given, from left to right, from [[Black Bear]], [[Mother Bear]], [[Brown Bear]], and [[Panda Bear]]. The second row of the tree held another four ornaments. They were, from left to right, from [[Science Bear]], [[Polar Bear]], [[Onett]], and [[Spirit Bear]]. The third row of the tree held three ornaments. They were, from left to right, from [[Gifted Riley Bee]], [[Gifted Bucko Bee]], and [[Stick Bug]]. An additional three ornaments could also be added to the tree, and they were, from left to top, [[Honey Bee (NPC)]], [[Gummy Bear]], and [[Bubble Bee Man]].

There was a {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|4=0}} token at the bottom and a {{Cost|Neonberry|4=0}} token at the top of the trunk of the Beesmas Tree.

== Beesmas 2019==
{{RemovedContent}}
To put ornaments on the tree, the player had to complete the [[Quests|quest]] of the corresponding [[:Category:NPC|NPC]], then talk to them. The ornament would then automatically go onto the tree.

[[File:BeesmasTree_SomeOrnaments.png|thumb|Some ornaments on the Beesmas Tree.]]
The bottom row of the tree held four ornaments. They were given, in order from left to right, from [[Black Bear]], [[Mother Bear]], [[Brown Bear]], and [[Panda Bear]]. The second row of the tree held another four ornaments. They were, from left to right, from [[Science Bear]], [[Polar Bear]], [[Onett]], and [[Spirit Bear]]. The third row of the tree held three ornaments. They were, from left to right, from [[Gifted Riley Bee]], [[Gifted Bucko Bee]], and [[Stick Bug]]. The ornament placed on the highest row of the tree was from [[Bubble Bee Man]]. 

There was a {{Cost|Field Dice||4=0}}, {{Cost|Gumdrops|5|4=0}}, and {{Cost|Moon Charm|5}} located in the Beesmas Tree. On the top of its trunk, there were {{Cost|Bitterberry|10}}. To access the platform and the token on it, players had to glide down from on top of [[Noob Shop]] or drop down from the Ant Gate.
