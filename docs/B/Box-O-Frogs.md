# Box-O-Frogs

{{Color|Box-O-Frogs}} is an [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] that was added in the [[Updates#2019.12.25|Beesmas 2019 update]]. When used, three regular/gifted [[frog]]s will be summoned in any [[Fields|field]] the player is currently standing in, which last for three minutes, that can collect [[Ability Tokens|tokens]], produce [[Passive Abilities#Gathering Bubbles|bubbles]] around the field (if spawned in a field), and attack hostile [[mobs]].

It can also be used outside of a field, but the frogs summoned will aimlessly hop, which then won’t do anything unless one of the frogs is in the field. The frogs have infinite HP and cannot die.

==Ways to Obtain==
*Completing 111 [[Brown Bear]] quests rewards {{Cost|Box-O-Frogs|5}}.
*Completing 333 Brown Bear quests rewards {{Cost|Box-O-Frogs|3}}.
*Completing [[Black Bear]]’s "Bubble Trouble" quest rewards 1 Box-O-Frogs and other items.
*Completing Black Bear's "Boss Battles" quest rewards 3 Box-O-Frogs and other items.
*Donating the BBM From Below [[Sticker]] to the [[Sticker Stack]].
*A low chance to drop from [[Onett's Lid Art]].
*A low chance to drop from [[Stockings|Brown Bear's Stockings]].
*Completing Bee Bear's "Cheering Up Gloomy Cub (4/5): Material Things?" rewards {{Cost|Box-O-Frogs|3}} and other items.
*Completing Bubble Bee Man's "B.B.M's Naughty List" during Winter Beesmas 2024 rewards {{Cost|Box-O-Frogs|10}} and other items.
