# Blueberry

{{Consumables
|image=Blueberry.png
|description=Increases your bond with a bee by 25.
}}
A {{Color|Blueberry}} is a type of [[Treats|treat]] that can be fed to a [[Bees|bee]] to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a [[Bumble Bee|Bumble]], [[Cool Bee|Cool]], [[Bubble Bee|Bubble]], [[Frosty Bee|Frosty]], [[Bucko Bee|Bucko]], [[Diamond Bee|Diamond]], [[Music Bee|Music]], [[Tadpole Bee|Tadpole]], or [[Buoyant Bee|Buoyant]] Bee, the amount of [[bond]] they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed blueberries, though this only happens with a 0.0125% (1 in 8,000) chance for [[Bees/Rare|Rare bees]], 0.01% (1 in 10,000) chance for [[Bees/Epic|Epic bees]], 0.0083% (1 in 12,000) chance for [[Bees/Common|Common]] and [[Bees/Legendary|Legendary bees]], and 0.00417% (1 in 24,000) chance for [[Bees/Mythic|Mythic bees]] per Blueberry.

== Ways to Obtain ==
* From using the [[Blueberry Dispenser]] inside the [[Blue HQ]].
* As a reward from the [[Ant Challenge]].
* Redeeming certain valid [[codes]]:
** 1MLikes (Gives 1 blueberry + other stuff).
** 15MMembers (Gives 15 blueberries + other stuff).
* As a reward from [[Memory Match|memory matches]].

=== Drops ===
* Farming in the [[Blue Flower Field]], [[Bamboo Field]], [[Cactus Field]], [[Pine Tree Forest]], and the [[Stump Field]].
** Harvesting [[leaves]] in these fields may also yield blueberries.
* As a drop from [[Rhino Beetle]]s, [[Mantis]]es, and [[King Beetle]].
* As a drop from a [[Ability Tokens#Festive Gift|Festive Gift]] or [[Ability Tokens#Festive Mark|Festive Mark]].
* As a gift from your [[Cub Buddy|Cub Buddy.]]
* As a reward for completely growing any [[sprout]] except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
** Planting a sprout in the Blue Flower Field, Bamboo Field, Pine Tree Forest, or Stump Field yields 6x more blueberries than other fields.
* Harvesting [[sparkles]] (Higher chances in blue fields).
* As a drop from [[Puffshroom]]s.
* As a drop from planters in blue fields. 
** Non-blue fields that contain a significant portion of blue flowers can drop Blueberries (Clover Field, Cactus Field, Mountain Top Field).
** When in blue fields, {{Cost|Paper Planter|||0}} drops multiple Blueberry tokens in increments of 1, 3, 5, and 10.


=== Quests ===
* Gifted Bucko Bee may reward the player blueberries as a special reward.
* Spirit Bear gives blueberries on the following quest:
** Out-Questing Questions gives 1 blueberry.
