# Bitterberry

{{Consumables
|image=Bitterberry.png
|description=Increases bond with a bee by 100. Small chance to cause Mutations.
}}
[[File:54C3C074-D3F3-4DF1-A9D8-B31A5CB31B69.jpg|thumb|The text that appears in the chat when a bee gains a mutation, fed with bitterberry.]]
A {{Color|Bitterberry}} is a type of [[Treats|treat]] that, when fed to a [[Bees|bee]], raises its [[bond]] by 100 and has a 1/1000 chance to make it [[Mutation|mutated]]. The chance is boosted to 1/100 if the bee is [[Radiation|radioactive]]. These are not the favorite treat of any bee.

== Ways to Obtain ==
*  Redeeming certain valid [[codes]]:
** BeesBuzz123 (rewards 5 bitterberries + other stuff).
** 15MMembers (rewards 15 bitterberries + other stuff).
* As a stack reward for adding certain [[stickers]] to the [[Sticker Stack]]:
** {{Cost|Squashed Head Bear Sticker}} rewards 25 bitterberries.

=== Drops ===
* Farming in the [[Mushroom Field]], [[Spider Field]], [[Pineapple Patch]], [[Cactus Field]], [[Rose Field]], [[Mountain Top Field]], and [[Pepper Patch]].
** Harvesting [[leaves]] in these fields may also yield bitterberries.
* As a guaranteed drop from [[Commando Chick]], [[Mondo Chick]], and the [[Coconut Crab]].
* As a drop from [[ladybug]]s, [[spider]]s, [[scorpion]]s, [[Aphid#Rage Aphid|rage aphids]], [[Tunnel Bear]], and [[King Beetle]].
* As a gift from a [[Cub Buddy|cub buddy]].
* As a common drop from [[Mythic Meteor Shower|Mythic Meteors]].
* As a drop from a Debug [[Sprout]].
* As an uncommon drop from [[Puffshroom]]s.
* As a drop from the {{Cost|Pesticide Planter|||0}} and {{Cost|The Planter Of Plenty|||0}}.
** The {{Cost|Pesticide Planter|4=0}} has a chance of dropping a bonus 100 bitterberries when harvested at full growth in the Spider Field, Mountain Top Field, or Pepper Patch.

=== Quests ===
* Black Bear rewards bitterberries on the following quest:
** Sweet and Sour gives 25 bitterberries.
** Weed Wacker 3 gives 25 bitterberries.
* Brown Bear rewards bitterberies on the completion of these quests:
** Brown Bear's 20th quest rewards 25 bitterberries.
** Brown Bear's 40th quest rewards 50 bitterberries.
** Brown Bear's 135th quest rewards 50 bitterberries.
** Brown Bear's 180th quest rewards 50 bitterberries.
** Brown Bear's 210th quest rewards 50 bitterberries.
* Every 20th Brown Bear quest rewards a varying amount of bitterberries.
