# Black Balloon

The {{Color|Black Balloon}} is a [[:Category:Consumables|consumable]] [[Items#Misc. Items|item]] that was added in the [[Updates|12/26/2021 update]]. Once used, it creates a black balloon over the [[Fields|field]] the player is in. The balloon floats over the field for 5 minutes before floating to the hive balloon, storing the [[pollen]] collected underneath it. The pollen collected below is multiplied by 4, and it holds up to 25 times the player's capacity. Only {{Cost|Black Balloon|1}} can be summoned in a field per player.

Black balloons have a cap of 25 in the player's [[:Category:Inventory|inventory]]. Obtaining one while already having 25 will not add a Black Balloon to the player's inventory, unless obtained from robux packs, [[quests]], or [[codes]].

== Ways to Obtain ==
* As a very rare drop from [[Tunnel Bear]].
* As an exceptionally rare drop from a [[Spider]].
* Completing [[Dapper Bear|Dapper Bear's]] 12th quest rewards 1 black balloon and other items.
* Sticking the Black Star Sticker to the Sticker Stacker rewards one Black Balloon.
