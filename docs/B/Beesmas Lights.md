# Beesmas Lights

{{RemovedContent}}
[[File:RobloxScreenShot20210215 150122914 (2).png|thumb|Beesmas Lights, unlocked after completing [[Science Bear|Science Bear's]] Beesmas quest.]]
'''Beesmas Lights''' would occasionally appear in fields as the player collects pollen after completing [[Science Bear|Science Bear's]] Beesmas Lights [[Quests|quest]]. 

They would fall from the sky in a similar way to the [[Passive Abilities#Star Shower|Star Shower passive]], taking 2 seconds to fall. Whenever a Beesmas Light is caught, it would grant the [[Ability Tokens#Inspire|Inspire]] buff and converts based on the player's [[System Page#Convert Total|convert total]]. If the player didn't catch it, the light collects pollen instead. Beesmas Lights would spawn after collecting 25 Ability Tokens (with a 30 second cooldown), dropping 5 lights into the field. Beesmas Lights could spawn in any field, including the [[Ant Field]].

If the player approached the switch for the Beesmas Lights, located beside [[Science Bear]] and had not yet completed his quest, a text box will display reading the following text: <span style="background-color:#a61e19;color:white">"This [''sic''] Beesmas Lights aren't powering on..."</span>

== Trivia ==
* Players were required to catch a certain amount of Beesmas Lights for [[Bee Bear]] and [[Onett]]'s quests.<!-- Which year? -->
* The Beesmas Lights has the same function as the [[Passive Abilities#Star Shower|Star Shower passive]] with the difference of the fact that the star shower is more focused in a small area than Beesmas Lights. 
** This means that if the passive is disregarded, the Beesmas Lights are the only way to get the inspire buff without [[Gifted Bee|gifted bees]] or redeeming certain [[codes]].
*Catching a falling Beesmas Light has a 1 in 10,000 chance to reward a Red/Blue/Green/Yellow Falling Beesmas Light [[sticker]] (depending on the color of the light).
* Over the course of the removal of several Beesmas events, Beesmas Lights would still fall into fields a few days after Beesmas was over.
** [[Bubble Bee Man]]'s Beesmas quest dialogue also tends to take longer to be removed.
