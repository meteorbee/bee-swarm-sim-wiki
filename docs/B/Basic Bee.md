'''Basic Bee''' is a [[:Category:Colorless|Colorless]] [[Bees/Common|Common bee]]. It is usually the first bee to be discovered and hatched.

Basic Bee can be only hatched from a {{Cost|Basic Egg|||0}}, a {{Cost|Star Egg|||0}}, or obtained through the currently unobtainable Basic Bee Jelly. It cannot be obtained from normal {{Cost|Royal Jelly}} or {{Cost|Star Jelly}}.

Basic Bee's favorite type of treat is {{Cost|Sunflower Seed}}.

Basic Bee likes the [[Sunflower Field]], [[Clover Field]], and [[Mountain Top Field]]. It dislikes the [[Spider Field]].

==Stats==
*Collects 10 [[pollen]] in 4 seconds.
*Makes 80 [[honey]] in 4 seconds.
*🌟 [[Gifted Bee|Gifted Hive Bonus]]: x1.2 Pollen.

==Trivia==
*Basic Bee is the only type of Common bee.
*Due to the large number of appearances Basic Bee makes within the game and thumbnails, it is considered the main mascot of Bee Swarm Simulator.
*Basic Bee is one of three bees to have an official plushie on the [https://shop.beeswarm.com/ Official Bee Swarm Simulator Website], the others being Tabby Bee and Vicious Bee.
*The statistics for all other [[bees]] are described in terms of their difference from Basic Bee’s statistics. For example, +20 collection means that the bee it's describing has collected 20 more pollen than Basic Bee.
*Basic Bee and [[Brave Bee]] are the only bees without abilities. 
**However, if they are in their [[Gifted Bee|gifted]] forms, they still can produce [[Ability Tokens#Inspire|Inspire tokens]].
**And specific [[Beequip]]s grants abilities like [[Ability Tokens#Focus|Focus]] or [[Ability Tokens#Melody|Melody]] tokens.
**Because of this, during [[Bee Bear|Bee Bear's]] "Ability Token Tidings" quest [[Bee Bear]] refers to Basic Bee and [[Brave Bee]] as "Poor little merely mortal bees".
***Bee Bear also says they aren't magic due to this fact.
*Basic Bee and [[Bees/Event|Event Bees]] are the only types of bee that cannot be obtained from a normal royal jelly since Basic Bee is a Common Bee.
*The [[Codes|code]], "DontUseThisJelly" (expired) gave a [[Royal Jelly#Royal Jelly Variants|Basic Bee jelly]] that could be used on a bee to transform any bee into a Basic Bee.
*Basic Bee and [[Demo Bee]] share the same skin color pattern when they are not in their gifted forms.
*Basic Bee and [[Honey Bee]] possess the same colors but in a different pattern.
*Basic Bee's gifted bonus used to give +20% [[System Page#Pollen|Pollen]], but it later got buffed after the [[Updates#2019.04.05|4/5/2019 update]] into x1.2 Pollen, so it can now increase with any additional pollen multipliers that the player has.
*An image of a winking Basic Bee face appears at the end of the White Tunnel.
**This may refer to the currently valid code called "Wink".
**This also appeared in the thumbnail of the [[Updates#2018.06.02|6/2/2018 update.]]
*Basic Bee is the only kind of bee to appear in every different version of the thumbnail for the game.
*Basic Bee, along with Tabby Bee and [[Rascal Bee]], are all of [[Onett (Developer)|Onett's]] favorite bees. 
**This was revealed through Onett's dialogue.
*Basic Bee's face appears on the bee menu icon, found near the top left corner of the screen.
**Its face is also visible on {{Cost|Treat}}, {{Cost|Ticket}}, and the [[Beequip|beequip case]].
*The chances of getting a Gifted Basic Bee from sunflower seeds is 1 in 12,000.
** The same chances apply to Legendary Bees if you feed them their favorite treats.
*The chances of hatching a Gifted Basic Bee from a Basic Egg is 0.348% (1 out of 287.35632184).
*On very rare occasions, Basic Bee's face would disappear on most surfaces that use its texture.
*Basic Bee, [[Tadpole Bee]], Tabby Bee, [[Lion Bee]], Puppy Bee, and Bear Bee are the only bees to represent realistic animals.
** But only the [[Tadpole Bee]], Tabby Bee, [[Lion Bee]], Puppy Bee, and Bear Bee represent realistic animals if Basic Bee is not counted since all the bees in this game are bees.
