The {{Color|Blue Clay Planter}} is a reusable planter that was added in the 12/26/21 update. It grows in 6 in-game hours and stores around 3 million [[pollen]]. It is purchasable in the [[Blue HQ]].

It grows 25% faster in fields with Blue flowers, and gives 50% Bonus Blue pollen from harvests. Blue bees are 25% more likely to sip [[Nectar]] from this planter, but Red bees avoid sipping nectar from it. This planter grants 20% more Refreshing and Comforting Nectar. Harvesting a fully grown Blue Clay Planter has a 1/8 (12.5%) chance to spawn a [[Puffshroom]].

==Trivia==
* Its red counterpart is the {{Cost|Red Clay Planter|4=0}}.
* This is the only planter that drops {{Cost|Honeysuckle}} as a bonus item.
* This is one of the five reusable planters to reside outside of [[Dapper Bear's Shop]], the others being the {{Cost|Red Clay Planter|4=0}}, {{Cost|Petal Planter|4=0}}, {{Cost|Hydroponic Planter|4=0}}, and {{Cost|Heat-Treated Planter|4=0}}.
** This and the {{Cost|Hydroponic Planter|4=0}} are the only planters to be sold from the Blue HQ.
* In the inventory's description, it doesn't say that it drops {{Cost|Honeysuckle}} as a bonus drop.
