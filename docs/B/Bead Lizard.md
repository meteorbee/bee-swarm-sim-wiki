# Bead Lizard

{{Beequip}}

A {{Color|Bead Lizard}} is a [[Bond|level]] 9 [[Beequip]]. It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [[Bees/Common|common]] or [[Bees/Rare|rare]] [[bees]].

==Ways to Obtain== 
*As a very rare drop from the {{Cost|Tacky Planter|4=0}}.
*As a very rare drop from the {{Cost|Pesticide Planter|4=0}}.
*As a very rare drop from the {{Cost|Petal Planter|4=0}}.
*As a product available for purchase in [[Dapper Bear's Shop]].
