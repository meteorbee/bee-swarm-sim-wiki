# Reindeer Antlers

{{Unobtainable}}

The {{Color|Reindeer Antlers}} is a [[Bond|level]] 8 [[Beesmas]] [[Beequip]]. It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [[Puppy Bee]].

==Trivia== 
*The {{Cost|Reindeer Antlers|4=1}} and the {{Cost|Festive Wreath|4=1}} share many similarities: 
**The two are the only Beequips to have models visible on the bee that they are equipped to.
**Before the [[Updates#2024.01.12|2024/01/12 update]]'s removal of permanent Beequips, the two were the only permanent Beequips in the game. 
***This is the first permanent Beequip added to the game, followed by the Festive Wreath.
***These were the only Beequips to have a set potential, with this Beequip always having max potential and the {{Cost|Festive Wreath|4=1}} always having a potential of 3 stars (6 points).
***They were the only Beequips that cannot be discarded.
***{{Cost|Caustic Wax}} were unable to be used on these two Beequips. However, this is no longer the case.
*A model of a Puppy Bee with {{Cost|Reindeer Antlers|}} can be found outside the map, next to a model of a [[Festive Bee]] with the Festive Wreath.
*This Beequip is likely a reference to Dr. Seuss’ children's book “How the Grinch Stole Christmas”, where the Grinch dresses his dog as a reindeer.
*This is the one of three Beequips to give a unique ability.
**The other two are the {{Cost|Snowglobe|4=1}} and {{Cost|Festive Wreath|4=1}}.
