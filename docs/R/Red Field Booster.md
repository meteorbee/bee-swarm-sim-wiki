# Red Field Booster

[[File:RedBooster.jpg|thumb|220x220px|Red Field Booster|The Red Field Booster inside of the Red HQ.]]

The '''Red Field Booster''' is a [[:Category:Machines|machine]] that provides a [[Field Boost]] to a random [[:Category:Red|red]] [[field]] for 15 minutes. The machine is located in the [[Red HQ]]. In order to use it, the player needs to have discovered 5 different red [[Bees|bee]] types. It has a cooldown of 45 minutes.

The following audio plays when the Red Field Booster has been activated:[[File:Fieldbooster.ogg]]

== Boosts ==
*[[Mushroom Field]]: +300% [[Pollen]]
*[[Strawberry Field]]: +100% Pollen
*[[Rose Field]]: +100% Pollen
*[[Pepper Patch]]: +100% Pollen (only if player has 35+ Bees)

== Trivia ==
* This is one of the three field boosters in the game, along with the [[Field Booster]] and the [[Blue Field Booster]].
** The Red Field Booster has the same design as the Field Booster and the Blue Field Booster, the only difference being the colors.
* The Red Field Booster, unlike the Blue Field Booster, is located near the entrance of the [[Red HQ]], while the Blue Field Booster is located on the second floor in the [[Blue HQ]].
* In an update, the Strawberry Field boost was nerfed from 200% to 100%.
* Prior to the [[Updates#2024.05.23|5/23/2024 update]], this booster was not able to boost the Pepper Patch.
