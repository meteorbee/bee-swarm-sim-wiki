# Robo Party

{{RemovedContent}}
[[File:Cake.PNG|thumb|Robo Bear's Party Cake, used to summon a Robo Party.]]
The '''Robo Party''' is a server-wide event where everyone in it has to defeat all the Party Robots scattered around the [[fields]]. To start a Robo Party, the player has to use a [[Robo Bear's Party Cake]], which is accessed by completing [[Robo Bear]]'s Beesmas Quest. The player can only start a Robo Party once every 3 hours. The Robo Party has a time limit of 3 minutes before the party is over. If all Party Robots are defeated, all participants will be rewarded with [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Other|Robo Party Blessing]], and the Player who used the Party Cake will be rewarded with items and can summon a Robo Party with a higher rank. If not all of the party mobs were killed, the player's rank for the Robo Party gets lowered. The Robo Party can also be summoned by [[Onett (Developer)|Onett]]. 

If all the Party Robots are defeated, the player who used the Party Cake will get an amount of Robo Party Blessing equal to the rank that was finished (Completing Rank 5 would reward the player with 5 stacks of Robo Party Blessing). 

__TOC__

==Mobs==
The Robo Party has a ranking system, the higher the rank, the more mobs will appear, along with their level and health increasing. However, the Party Robot's drops and the challenge's rewards will slowly improve. There are four types of Mobs that are spawned during the event: Party Mechsquitoes, Party Cogmowers, Party Cogturrets, and Party Mega Mechsquitoes. Party Cogturrets will spawn on Rank 5, while Party Mega Mechsquitoes will spawn on Rank 15.<tabview>

==First-Time Rewards ==
First-time rewards are the rewards that are given after a player completes a Robo Party rank for the first time. The rewards are always the same for every player who beats that rank for the first time. 

Each rank completed for the first time rewards the player with an amount of {{Cost|Ticket|}} equal to the level of the rank completed (Completing Rank 5 would reward {{Cost|Ticket|5}}).  {{Cost|Stinger|}} and a type of [[Balloon]] are guaranteed for every rank completed for the first time. At least one [[Drives|Drive]] is guaranteed for every rank completed for the first time starting at Rank 19.

== Strategies for Robo Parties ==
*Using [[Stinger|Stingers]] will give x1.5 attack to your bees for 30 seconds. This can be very useful when killing high-level mobs like Party Cogturrets or Party Mega Mechsquitoes.
*If the amount of mobs are too overwhelming, use [[Passive Abilities|Star Saw]] (if you have it). Star Saw circles around you for 30 seconds after activation, and while it's difficult to hit Party Mechsquitoes and Party Mega Mechsquitoes (owing to their flying hitboxes), Party Cogmowers and Party Cogturrets can easily be hit with it, allowing you to quickly take down these threats if the Bees are preoccupied with Party Mechsquitoes and Party Mega Mechsquitoes.
*[[Vicious Bee]] and [[Windy Bee]] provide excellent crowd control, especially since most mobs often come in pairs or packs (other than Party Mega Mechsquitos). Digital Bee can also stun many mobs at once and allow them to take extra damage via Mind Hack.
*Party Cogturrets will always shoot straight at where they are facing so you can avoid their attacks simply by not being in front of them.
*Party Cogmowers don't aim for the player, allowing players to easily sidestep them.
*Stand near the Party Mobs, this will allow your bees to lock on to the Party Mobs and quickly deal damage.
*Party Mechsquitos and Party Mega Mechsquitos aim for where the player is, not where the player will be. Because of this, moving around in circles will often result in them missing their attack.
*Always keep an eye on your health. While doing Robo Parties, it is easy to be too focused on killing the Party Robots, and not keep track of how much damage you've taken.
**If you so happen to die, you can look around for fields with Party Robots when you respawn until your bees wake up.

==Trivia==
*The number on the Party Cake changes based on how how old the game is on that year
*The Party Cogturret is the only Party Robot without a Party Hat.
*It is not possible to have 2 Robo Parties happening at the same time.
*The Robo Party Blessing Icon is similar to Gifted [[Buoyant Bee|Buoyant Bee's]] Surprise Party ability token.   
**Coincidentally, the Party Hat spawned upon the ability being activated is similar to the one most of the Party Mobs wear.
*Every Party Mob behaves similar to [[Golden Cogmower]]s, spawning in random/specific fields, rather than either swarming the player or spawning in the field they're currently in.
