'''Robo Bear''' is an [[:Category:NPC|NPC]] released in Beesmas 2022 update. It is located in the 15 Bee Zone, found next to the Red HQ and at the other end of the [[Rose Field]]. The main feature of Robo Bear is initiating the [[Robo Bear Challenge]]. Before gaining access to Robo Bear, the player will be first given a quest to assemble the missing parts of Robo Bear being Red Drive, White Drive, Blue Drive, and Glitched Drive. The drives can be obtained in the following quests:
* [[Science Bear]]: Repairing the White Drive
* [[Dapper Bear]]: Bartering for the Blue Drive
* [[Panda Bear]]: Ready For the Red Drive?
* [[Science Bear]]: Corrupting the Glitched Drive
When all requirements are met, the player can then access to talking to Robo Bear and initiate the Robo Bear Challenge at the cost of 1 Robo Pass, obtainable through the dispensers in the 30 bee zone.

During Beesmas 2022, players were able to complete the quest by purchasing a white drive in [[Bee Bear's Catalog]], completing Science Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest for a red drive, completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas 2022 quest for a blue drive, and completing [[Bee Bear|Bee Bear's]] “Decorating For Drives” quest for a glitched drive.

==Trivia==
*Robo Bear is the second NPC in the game that can initiate a challenge, the first being [[Stick Bug]]. 
*Robo Bear uses the [https://www.roblox.com/bundles/82/Robot-Animation-Pack Robot Animation Pack].
*Robo Bear is one of the few quest givers who own a shop, the others being [[Mother Bear]], [[Dapper Bear]], [[Gummy Bear]], and [[Bee Bear]].
*Robo Bear's original face decal was made on 2/24/2019, eventually releasing on 12/14/2022 for [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]].
*Robo Bear is the only bear to publicly have its design changed from before release.
