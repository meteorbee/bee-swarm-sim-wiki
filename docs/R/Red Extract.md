# Red Extract

{{Consumables

|description = Grants x1.25 Red Pollen for 10 minutes.
|cooldown = 1 second

{{Color|Red Extract}} is an [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] that was added in the [[Updates#2018.11.25|11/25/2018 update]]. The purpose of it is to use it as a material to craft [[:Category:Accessories|accessories]], or for a 10-minute boost that grants x1.25 [[System Page#Red Pollen|Red Pollen]]. The [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Items|buff]] cannot stack and will only reset its timer if you used a '''Red Extract''' when there is another one active. Using a {{Cost|Purple Potion|4=0}} or {{Cost|Super Smoothie|4=1}} will override the buff.
[[File:Red extract token bss.png|thumb|A red extract token.]]

== Ways to Obtain ==
* [[Crafting]] it via [[Blender]] for:
** {{Cost|Strawberry|50}}
** {{Cost|Royal Jelly|10}}
* Matching a pair of red extracts in [[Memory Match#Mega Memory Match|Mega Memory Match]] (worth 1 red extract) or [[Memory Match#Extreme Memory Match|Extreme Memory Match]] (worth 3 red extracts).
* As a reward from the [[Wind Shrine]].
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain [[Stickers]] to the [[Sticker Stack]]:
** {{Cost|Flying Rad Bee Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.
** {{Cost|Round Rascal Bee Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.
** {{Cost|Flying Festive Bee Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.
** {{Cost|Little Scorpion Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.
** {{Cost|Red X Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.
** {{Cost|AFK Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.
** {{Cost|Small Flame Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.
** {{Cost|Shrugging Heart Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.
** {{Cost|Red Doodle Person Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.
** {{Cost|Scythe Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.
** {{Cost|Dark Scythe Sticker|||0}} rewards 1 red extract.

* As a reward from the [[Retro Swarm Challenge]].  

=== Drops ===
* As a drop from certain [[mobs]]
** [[Ladybug]] (Rare)
** [[Scorpion]] (Rare)
** [[King Beetle]]
** [[Aphid#Rage Aphid|Rage Aphid]]
* Farming in the [[Mushroom Field]], [[Strawberry Field]], [[Rose Field]], or the [[Pepper Patch]] can rarely drop red extracts.
** A rare drop from [[leaves]] in the Mushroom Field, Strawberry Field, or the Rose Field.
* As a rare drop from [[sprout]]s (the odds increase depending on the rarity of the sprout and if the sprout is located in red fields).
* As a drop from a [[Ability Tokens#Festive Gift|Festive Gift]] (if [[Festive Bee]] is [[Gifted Bee|gifted]]) or [[Ability Tokens#Festive Mark|Festive Mark]].
* As a rare gift from a [[Cub Buddy|cub buddy]].
* As a guaranteed drop from the certain [[planters]]:
** {{Cost|Red Clay Planter|||0}}
** {{Cost|Heat-Treated Planter|||0}} (Drops extra red extracts in red fields.)
*** Planting the {{Cost|Heat-Treated Planter|||0}} in the Mushroom Field, then the Strawberry Field, then the Rose Field, then the Pepper Patch results in an additional {{Cost|Red Extract|100}} (unknown cooldown).
*** Additionally, it has a small chance to drop {{Cost|Red Extract|100}} upon harvesting in any field.
** Planters harvested in red fields have a chance to drop red extracts.

=== Quests ===
* Completing any of [[Gifted Riley Bee|Gifted Riley Bee's]] [[quests]] will always reward one Red Extract.
* [[Black Bear]] gives Red Extracts on the following quests:
** Get Red-y gives {{Cost|Red Extract|1}} and other items.
** Rocky Red Mountain gives {{Cost|Red Extract|1}} and other items.
** Capsaicin Collector gives {{Cost|Red Extract|1}} and other items.
** Black Bear's repeatable quests occasionally give Red Extracts.
* [[Science Bear]] gives red extracts on the following quests:
** Meticulously Crafted gives {{Cost|Red Extract|1}} and other items.
** Mushroom Measurement Monotony gives {{Cost|Red Extract|3}} and other items.
