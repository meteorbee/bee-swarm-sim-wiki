# Robo Bear's Party Cake

{{RemovedContent}}
The '''Robo Bear's Party Cake''' is a [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]]-exclusive machine that was added in the second part of Beesmas 2022. It is accessible after completing [[Robo Bear]]'s Party Cake Quest and initiates a special challenge every 3 hours.

==Appearance==
Before completing Robo Bear's quest, the party cake is only 3 metal trusses, stacked to make a pyramid, with an unlit olive green "5" candle on the top. The number changes every year to show how many years the game's been up. 

After the quest is finished, the "number" candle is lit with neon lights and a blue outline. The cake layers are olive green with different colored frames, the bottom layer blue, the middle yellow, and the top purple. Candles and sparklers are placed on the corners, and Neon Sprinkles are placed around the front of each layer.

==Uses==
After completing Robo Bear's quest, players will be able to start a [[Robo Party]] every 3 hours. The Robo Party spawns Party Mobs around the map for players to defeat, and in turn they can get rewards, including Robo Party Blessing. The Robo Party can spawn "Party" versions of the Mechsquito, Cogmower, Cogturret and Mega Mechsquito. Once a Robo Party has begun, a set number (that changes with the rank of the party) of Party Mobs spawn in random fields, and the players in the server have to defeat them all within 3 minutes. If they are successful, the player that summoned the party has their party cake increased by 1 rank and the players receive rewards, including Robo Party Blessing. If they do not complete it within the timer, the player that summoned it has their party cake decreased by 1 rank. Party mobs also have a chance to drop Robo Party Blessing tokens, with the chance increasing with the level of the Mob and the type of mob that is defeated.

==Rewards==
Each rank completed for the first time rewards the player with an amount of Tickets equal to the level of the rank completed (Completing Rank 5 would reward 5 Tickets). Stingers and a type of [[Balloon]] are guaranteed for every rank completed for the first time. At least one [[Drives|Drive]] is guaranteed for every rank completed for the first time starting at Rank 19.
