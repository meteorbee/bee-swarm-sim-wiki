The {{Color|Red Clay Planter}} is a reusable [[planter]] added in the 12/26/21 update. It grows in 6 hours and stores 3 million [[Pollen]]. It grants bonus [[Red Extract]]s and [[Waxes]]. It can be purchased in the [[Red HQ]].

The planter grows 25% faster in fields with red flowers (4.5 hours ) and it gives 50% bonus red pollen from harvests. Red bees are 25% more likely to sip [[Nectar]] from it, but Blue bees avoid sipping nectar from it. This planter grants 20% more Invigorating and Satisfying Nectar. Harvesting a fully grown Red Clay Planter has a 1/8 (12.5%) chance to spawn a [[Puffshroom]].

==Trivia==
* Its blue counterpart is the {{Cost|Blue Clay Planter|||0}}.
* This is one of the five reusable planters to reside outside of [[Dapper Bear's Shop]], the others being the Blue Clay Planter, {{Cost|Petal Planter|||0}}, {{Cost|Hydroponic Planter|||0}}, and {{Cost|Heat-Treated Planter|||0}}.
** This and the Heat-Treated Planter are the only planters to be sold from the Red HQ.
* At some point the description in the inventory used to state that it grows in 4 hours, but it would actually grow in 6 hours ever since it was added.
