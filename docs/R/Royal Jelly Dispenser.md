# Royal Jelly Dispenser

<blockquote>''<small>Not to be confused with the [[Free Royal Jelly Dispenser]], which does not cost anything, or the [[Royal Jelly Shop]].</small>''</blockquote>
[[File:Royal Jelly Dispenser-0.png|thumb|240x240px|The Royal Jelly Ticket Dispenser beside the Clover Field.]]
The '''Royal Jelly Dispenser''' is a [[dispenser]] that is located near the [[Clover Field]] and the [[Noob Shop]]. It requires {{Cost|Ticket|6}} to be exchanged for {{Cost|Royal Jelly|1}} and 10× [[Ability Tokens#Haste|Haste]].  

The dispenser has a cooldown of 3 seconds.

== Trivia ==

* In terms of honey, the Royal Jelly Dispenser is very overpriced due to 1 royal jelly costing 1 million honey at the royal jelly shop, although if a player were to purchase 6 tickets from the mountain top [[Ticket Shop]], it would cost more than a million honey.
*The Royal Jelly Ticket Dispenser is one of three dispensers that require tickets to use, the others being the [[Ant Pass Dispenser]] and the [[Robo Pass Dispenser]].
**Coincidentally, all three dispensers have a counterpart that gives their item for free, albeit with a much longer cooldown in between usage.
**The Royal Jelly Dispenser's free counterpart, however, requires [[Gifted Bee|gifted bees]] in order to become utilized.
*The Royal Jelly Dispenser, the [[Honey Dispenser]], the [[Strawberry Dispenser]], the [[Blueberry Dispenser]], the [[Glue Dispenser]], and the [[Samovar]] are the only dispensers that are required to be used for a [[Quests|quest]].
