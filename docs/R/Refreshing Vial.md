# Refreshing Vial

{{Consumables|description=Grants 4 hours of Refreshing Nectar. (Boosts: Red Pollen, Blue Convert Rate, Bee Ability Pollen)|cooldown=1 Second|cap=10|image=Refreshingvial.png}}

The {{Color|Refreshing Vial}} is a consumable [[Nectar Vial|nectar vial]] [[Items|item]] that was added in the [[Updates#2022.12.26|12/26/2022 update]]. Once used, it grants the player 4 hours of the [[Nectar|Refreshing Nectar]] [[Buffs & Debuffs|buff]] instantly. It can also be used to craft other [[:Category:Accessories|accessories]].

== Ways to obtain ==
* Completing [[Dapper Bear|Dapper Bear's]] 7th and 14th quests.
* Using the [[Nectar Condenser]] to condense 12 hours of Refreshing Nectar.
* Adding the Refreshing Nectar Icon to the [[Sticker Stack]] grants 1 Refreshing Vial.
