The '''Red Balloon''' is a [[:Category:Consumables|consumable]] [[Items#Misc. Items|item]] that was added in the [[Updates|12/26/2021 update]]. Once used, it creates a red [[balloon]] over the [[field]] the player is in. The balloon floats over the field, storing [[pollen]] collected underneath it. The pollen collected increases by 10%, with red pollen increasing by 20%. It holds up to 5x the player's capacity, and floats over the field for 3 minutes before floating to the [[Balloon#Hive Balloon|hive balloon]].

Red balloons have a cap of 25 in the player's [[:Category:Inventory|inventory]], and 1 per player in the field.

== Ways to Obtain ==

* As a exceptionally rare drop from a [[scorpion]].
* As a rare reward from the [[Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine]].
* Completing [[Dapper Bear]]'s 6th quest gives {{Cost|Red Balloon|1}} and other items.
* A Red Balloon token can be found on top of the [[Hive Hub]] after completing the parkour.
* Redeeming certain valid codes:
**15MMembers (gives {{Cost|Red Balloon|1}} and other stuff).
