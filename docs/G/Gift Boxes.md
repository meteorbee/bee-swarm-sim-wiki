# Gift Boxes

{{RemovedContent}}
''<sup>Not to be confused with [[Present]]s and [[Ornament Presents]].</sup>''

'''Gift Boxes''' are presents that appear during each Beesmas update, which are located around the map. To open them, the player needs to help a certain number of [[:Category:NPC|NPCs]] complete their [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]] decorations by completing their [[quests]]. There are 13 gift boxes this year.

==Trivia==  
*Originally, the Mondo Gift Box required 12 NPCs.      
**When the second update had arrived, it changed to 14 NPCs.
**In Beesmas 2021, the Mondo Gift Box required 14 NPCs, then due to the occurrence of [[Dapper Bear]] being added, it changed to 15 NPCs. Everything that required 6-11 NPCs were made to require 1 more than before, and the Festive Gift Box was added, requiring 13 NPCs. This happened after the Midnight Gift Box was added.
**In Beesmas 2022, the requirements of every gift box that required above 7 NPCs had their requirement increase by 1. This happened after Chestnut Gift Box was added in part 2 of Beesmas, where Spirit Bear, Gummy Bear, Bubble Bee Man, Robo Bear, and [[Honey Bee (NPC)|Honey Bee]] got new Beesmas quests.
* The Midnight Gift Box and the Chestnut Gift Box were the only gift boxes to be added in the middle of Beesmas.   
**The Midnight Gift Box was the first to accomplish this.
*The Mondo Gift Box was the last gift box to get removed from the game in Beesmas 2020, because [[Bubble Bee Man|B.B.M's]] quest was extended by 1 week.
*It was '''not''' necessary to have 35 bees to access the Pristine Gift Box. Instead, the player could go to the roof of the [[Red HQ]], where they could jump onto the ledge with the box. However, taking just a few steps beyond that would bring them back to the spawn point.
*There were 10 gift boxes in total during Beesmas 2020, 12 gift boxes in 2021 and 2022, and there are 13 gift boxes this year. There has never been a gift box for helping one NPC.
:*Up to the 12/25/2024 update, there was never a gift box for helping two NPCs, until the Elite Gift Box was changed from 3 NPCs to 2 NPCs. There was never a gift box for helping three NPCs since.
*Originally, the Elite Gift Box was next to [[Panda Bear]].
