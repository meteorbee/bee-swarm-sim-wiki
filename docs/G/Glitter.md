# Glitter

{{Color|Glitter}} is a craftable [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] that was added in the [[Updates#2018.11.25|11/25/2018 update]]. It grants a +100% [[pollen]] boost in the [[field]] the player is standing in for 15 minutes upon activation. If used in a field with a [[planter]], the planter gains a 25% growth rate boost until it is harvested. Glitter has a 15 minute and 5 second cooldown between uses. This is to prevent the user from stacking the item's buff for one field.

It can be used in all fields except the [[Ant Field]].  Attempting to do so will result in the following message: {{Notification|Red|You can't boost a challenge field}}

== Ways to Obtain ==
* [[Crafting]] via [[Blender]] for:
** {{Cost|Moon Charm|25}}
** {{Cost|Magic Bean|1}}
* Playing any type of [[Memory Match]] (Increments of 1 or 5).
* As a reward from donating to the [[Wind Shrine]].
* From a [[Ability Tokens#Festive Gift|Festive Gift]] or [[Ability Tokens#Festive%20Mark|Festive Mark]].
* Purchasing the [[Bee Bear's Catalog|Brilliant Beesmas Bundle]] from Bee Bear's Catalog.
* As a stack reward for adding certain [[Stickers]] to the [[Sticker Stack]]:
** Sideways Spirit Bear rewards 1 glitter.
** Shining Halo rewards 1 glitter.
** Glowing Smile rewards 1 glitter.
** Every Star Sign sticker rewards 1 glitter.

*** Planting the {{Cost|Petal Planter|||0}} in any white field may yield a bonus {{Cost|Glitter|10}} (Unknown cooldown).

== Trivia ==
* This is one of four consumable items to grant a [[Field Boost|field boost]], the others being [[Field Dice]], [[Smooth Dice]], and [[Loaded Dice]]
** It also is the one of the four consumables that shows glitter particles on the field when the said field is boosted.
** This is the only consumable to grant a Field Boost of the player's choice.
* Spirit Bear mentions in one of her quest dialogues, Spirit's Starter, that glitter was once so rare that 1 pouch of it costed 5 million honey.
