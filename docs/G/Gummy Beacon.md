# Gummy Beacon

{{RemovedContent}}
[[File:Gummy Beacon.png|alt=The Gummy Beacon|thumb|The Gummy Beacon after the player has turned in Gummy Bear's Beesmas quest.]]
The '''Gummy Beacon''' is a [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]] 2020+ machine that can be unlocked after completing [[Gummy Bear|Gummy Bear's]] Beesmas [[Quests|quest]]. It is located on top of the [[Noob Shop]], on the corner closest to the [[Royal Jelly Dispenser]] next to the [[Clover Field]], in front of the [[Star Hall]]. If the player attempts to activate it without completing Gummy Bear's Beesmas quest, it reads: ''"The satellite dish isn't working, but it still emits bad vibes..."'' Completing his quest allows the player to activate a Gummy Siege.

== Gummy Siege ==
Upon activating, a platform will appear with [[Gummy Bear]] and [[Gummy Bee]] in front of the Gummy Beacon, and the [[Passive Abilities#Gummy Morph|Gummy Morph]] tune will play. Then, it will summon three level 8-20 Gummy Bees for five minutes. Gummy Bear will begin to shoot [[goo]] puddles at random fields similar to the [[:Category:Gummy Invasion|Gummy Invasion]] event. The targeted field will also spawn {{Cost|Gumdrops}} tokens. Gummy Bear will pick five random fields (excluding the [[Ant Field]] and [[Hub Field]]), and shoots goo for one minute per field, making the Gummy Siege 5 minutes long. 

If a player stands on the Gummy Beacon who hasn't completed the Gummy Bear's Goo Year Beacon quest, the message will say: "This Satellite dish isn't working, but it still emits bad vibes...".
[[File:Static-assets-upload566652386421731089.png|thumb|Three random leveled Gummy Bees being summoned.]]

When it starts, two server-wide messages will appear saying the following lines: 
{{Notification|Goo|Gummy Bear: "Artillery has arrived, Gummy Soldier {Username}."|15}}
{{Notification|Goo|Gummy Bear: "Beesmas ends now. Commence GOO YEAR celebrations!"|13.5}}

As Gummy Bear targets fields, more server-wide messages will appear showing which field is being targeted:

{{Notification|Goo|Gummy Bear: "Sight set on {Field Name}!"}}

== Trivia ==
* This is the second and currently the only way of making Gummy Bear appear in the mountain, the first being using the [[Gummy Cannon]].
** Although Gummy Bear is shown during the siege, he can still be seen in his lair.
** This is also the only way that the Gummy Cannon can appear.
* This is one of the 4 machines that summons temporary bees, the others being the [[Honeyday Candles]], [[Onett's Lid Art]], and Honey Bee's [[Honey Wreath]].
* This is the only way to have more than 1 Gummy Bee, though it is only for a limited amount of time.
* The Gummy Bees given by the Gummy Beacon can help player activate the [[Passive Abilities#Gummy Morph|Gummy Morph]] very quickly by spawning tokens that contribute towards the passive.
* This and Onett's Lid Art are the only ways to have multiple [[Bees/Event|Event Bees]]. However, they only last for a limited amount of time.
* This, Onett's Lid Art and the [[Naughty List]] are Beesmas machines that have the longest cooldowns in the game, at 8 hours.
* The Gummy Bees from the Gummy Siege are not affected by the [[Passive Abilities#Gummy Morph|Gummy Morph]] Passive from the [[Gummy Mask]], however their [[Ability Tokens|abilities]] will still contribute to activating the passive.
