The '''Glider''' is a [[:Category:Gliding Tools|gliding tool]] that can be purchased at the [[Mountain Top Shop]], it is the upgraded version of the [[Parachute]].

It allows the player to glide much quicker and change direction faster than the [[Parachute]]. To activate it, jump, then jump again while in mid-air. If the player goes airborne through some other method (a cannon, for example), double jump; to disengage it, jump once more or make contact with ground. The gliding feature is hardcoded to the player, and they will glide even during a lag spike; although, the Glider doesn't immediately appear.

The primary use of the Glider is to glide to places on the map that would otherwise be inaccessible. It can be used to collect some of the {{Cost|Royal Jelly|4=0}} and {{Cost|Ticket|4=0}} tokens around the map and can be used to perform several shortcuts. The Glider, along with the [[Parachute]], are the only ways to obtain the {{Cost|Diamond Egg|4=0}} token below the platform in the [[Bear Gate|30 Bee Zone]]. It is also used to reach the [[Star Hall]] located on top of the [[Noob Shop]]. The Glider can also be used to dodge [[mobs]], since most of them don't have a high detection range in the air. Combined with the cannons (especially with the [[Red Cannon]]), it is one of the most efficient ways to travel around the map.

The Glider's gliding speed is not affected by boosts that enhance the player’s movement speed, such as [[Ability Tokens#Haste|Haste]], Haste+, [[Ability Tokens#Bear Morph|Bear Morph]] or [[Oil]].

==Trivia==
*The Glider and the [[Parachute]] are the only gliding tools in the game, excluding the unobtainable Debug Glider.
*With the Glider, it is possible to glide outside of the map.
**You can also exit out of the map via a [[Wild Windy Bee]] blowing at you with a gust of wind.
*If the player enables shift-lock, they can slightly strafe to one side while using the [[:Category:Gliding Tools|gliding tools]].
*The Glider is located on top of the [[Mountain Top Shop]] as a decoration, similar to how a Parachute model is located on top of the [[Pro Shop]].
*The Glider is the second cheapest item in the Mountain Top Shop, after the first [[Hive Slot|hive slot]].
**It was the third cheapest item in the shop before the {{Cost|Eviction|4=0}} was removed.
*The Glider along with the [[Parachute]] can be used to move during a game of [[Memory Match]].
