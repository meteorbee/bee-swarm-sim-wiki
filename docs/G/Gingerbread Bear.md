</blockquote>A {{Color|Gingerbread Bear}} is a unique type of [[Treats|treat]] that was added in the [[Updates#2020.12.25|12/25/2020 update]]. It is obtainable by checking [[Mother Bear|Mother Bear's]] [[Gingerbread House]], defeating certain [[mobs]] and bosses, opening [[Gift Boxes]], completing [[Bee Bear|Bee Bear's]] quests, giving {{Cost|Present}} to certain [[Quest Givers|NPCs]] around the map, completing NPCs Beesmas quests, using [[Onett's Lid Art]], and buying certain [[Robux Shop|Robux Packs]].

==Overview==
Gingerbread bears have 2 functions. The player could save them up and spend it on [[Bee Bear's Catalog]] during the Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2024 events, or feed them to [[bees]]. In addition to granting [[bond]], it has a 1% chance to make them [[Gifted Bee|gifted]]. They can also be saved up until the next Beesmas event, where they turn into {{Cost|Aged Gingerbread Bear}}.

The formula of turning a bee gifted:

<math>(1 - 0.99^x) \times 100\%</math> (𝑥 is the number of the {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear}} fed to a bee)

==Trivia==
*{{Cost|Gingerbread Bear||4=1}}, along with {{Cost|Snowflake}}, are the only currencies that can be used in Bee Bear's Catalog.  
**However, unlike {{Cost|Snowflake}}, {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear||4=1}} are not removed from the player's inventory when Beesmas ends and are still obtainable and usable.
*This, along with the {{Cost|Star Treat|||0}} and {{Cost|Aged Gingerbread Bear}}, are the only items in the game that can turn [[Bees/Event|Event bees]] gifted.
*This and the {{Cost|Festive Bean|||0}} are the only event items that are infinitely renewable outside of an event.
*Feeding {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|100}} to a bee does not guarantee to turn it gifted. The chance of it becoming gifted is around 63%.
