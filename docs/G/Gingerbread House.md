# Gingerbread House

{{RemovedContent}}
[[File:Gingerbread House.png|thumb|180px|The Gingerbread House after being assembled by completing [[Mother Bear]]'s Beesmas quest.]]The '''Gingerbread House''' is a [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]] exclusive machine which can be assembled after completing [[Mother Bear]]'s Beesmas quest.

It sits between [[Mother Bear]] and the [[Treat Shop]]. When activated, the house gives {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|1}} for every 2 playtime hours, which can be built up over time.

If the player attempts to use the Gingerbread House before it is completed, it will display text that reads: <span style="background-color:#a61e19;color:white">This Gingerbread House is far from complete...</span>

==Overview==
The Gingerbread House has gumdrops for decorations at the corners, a {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|||0}} in the middle, and a candy cane acting as a possible chimney.

Its only use is to produce a Gingerbread Bear every 2 '''in-game''' hours. If the player is offline, it does not generate any.

Upon completing the quest, the Gingerbread House can be used immediately for one Gingerbread Bear. <span style="color: red">'''The Gingerbread House <u>will not</u> accumulate Gingerbread Bears until after it is used for the first time.'''</span>

==Trivia==

*The Gingerbread House stores multiple gingerbread bears at the same time, meaning that multiple gingerbread bears can be collected in one go after 4 or more hours.
*Even though the cooldown to collect reset once it was collected, the timer for generating gingerbread bears does not. For example, if a player with 3 hours in-game checks the house, they will only get 1 gingerbread bear and the cooldown will reset to 2 hours. The timer saves that extra hour, so the player will get 2 gingerbread bears if they collect after 3 more hours (6 hours total). 
* A player can get {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|12}} a day if they are online for 24 hours. This means they can make the 500 gingerbreads required for the super smoothie pack in 32 days (31 days and 16 hours to be exact) just from the Gingerbread House, so long as they are online 24/7.
* The Gingerbread House was introduced in [[Updates#2020.12.25|Beesmas 2020]], and has returned on each Beesmas since.
* Quest givers such as [[Bee Bear]] require the player to interact with the Gingerbread House a varying amount of times.
