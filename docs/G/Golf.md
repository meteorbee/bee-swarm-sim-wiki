# Golf

{{RemovedContent}}
'''Golf''' is a system that was introduced with the [[Updates#2024.08.01|2024.08.01 update]] as part of Bee Swarm Simulator's experience in "[[The Games]]" event and removed in the [[Updates#2025.04.30|2025.04.30 update]].

Golf was used throughout [[Sun_Bear|Sun Bear's]] 2024 questline, his quests required the player to hit golf balls into holes, and to activate Targets. Golf currently has no use in any [[quests]].

Golf only appears in the [[Dandelion Field]], [[Blue Flower Field]] and [[Sunflower Field]].

== Golf Balls ==
{{Mob

|location=The Dandelion Field, Blue Flower Field, and Sunflower Field.

'''Golf balls''' have a pollen meter, which can be filled, similar to [[Puffshroom|Puffshrooms]]. When filled, the player can putt the ball, by collecting [[pollen]] near the ball when it's pollen meter is full. The further away the player stands, the farther and higher the ball will go. A hit golf ball collects [[pollen]] from [[flowers]] underneath it, however, they do not interact with [[mobs]] or [[bees]] but they do have collision with the environment and Pink Fences. Golf balls can also hit targets, giving the player some rewards.

=== Spawning ===
Golf Balls can only spawn in the [[Dandelion Field]], [[Blue Flower Field]] and [[Sunflower Field]]. There can only be two Golf Balls in a field at any time. A new Golf Ball spawns after they are putted into a hole or sink into mud.

== Holes ==
{{Infobox item
|title=Hole
|effects=Putting a golf ball into a hole spawns 7 Honey tokens.
|source=Appears in the Dandelion, Blue Flower and Sunflower Fields.
|image=GolfFlagDandelionField.png|imagecaption=}}
'''Holes''' are located in the [[Dandelion Field]], [[Blue Flower Field]] and [[Sunflower Field]]. Putting the golf ball into a hole rewards a ring of {{Cost|Honey|7}} tokens around the hole, which scales by the amount of points and the level of the golf ball. After putting a golf ball into a Hole, a new golf ball with a different level spawns.

== Mud ==
{{Infobox item
|title=Mud
|effects=Makes Golf Balls that stop on it respawn.|source=Appears in the Dandelion, Blue Flower and Sunflower Fields.
|image=Mud.png|imagecaption=}}
'''Mud''' is found on [[flowers]] in the [[Dandelion Field]], [[Blue Flower Field]] and [[Sunflower Field]]. Mud tints all flowers underneath it brown. If a golf ball comes to a complete stop over mud, it sinks and a new golf ball spawns.

This is the same mud as the pattern [[Stick Bug]] turns into during the [[Stick Bug Challenge]].

== Pink Fences ==
''<small>Note: This mechanic has not been given an official name, but will be referred to as Pink Fences on this wiki.</small>''
{{Infobox item
|title=Pink Fence
|effects=Blocks Golf Balls shot at it.
|source=Appears in the Blue Flower Field and Sunflower Field.
|image=PinkFenceBlueFlowerField.png|imagecaption=}}
'''Pink Fences''' are a set of transparent pink fences that can be found in the [[Blue Flower Field]] and [[Sunflower Field]]. The fences block Golf Balls shot at it.

They can only hit moving Golf Balls, and do not affect players, [[mobs]], or any other mechanics.
