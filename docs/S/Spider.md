The '''Spider''' is a level 4 [[Mobs|mob]] that can only be found in the [[Spider Field]].  

It takes 30 minutes for the spider to respawn after being defeated (25 minutes 30 seconds with a [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]] [[Vicious Bee]]). Defeating it gives 10 [[Battle Points]] and {{Cost|Honey|1000|4=}} spread across as 5 {{Cost|Honey||4=}} tokens. Various other rewards are also possible with their probability increasing with [[Loot Luck]].

== Strategies ==
* One way to keep idle while defeating the spider is to stay on the first block in the [[Obstacle Courses#Bamboo Field Obby|Bamboo Obby]] - the spider will try to lunge towards the player, but it will be stopped by the block. The player can also stand near the edge of the [[Vicious Bee Egg Claim]].
* The [[Glitches#Frozen Field Defenders|Frozen Field Defenders Glitch]] can be used to kill the spider easily, just like any other normal mob.
* Another method is to charge at the spider, then jump and glide over the spider, though this will require the [[Parachute]] or [[Glider]].

== Trivia ==
* This, the [[Mantis]] and the [[Werewolf]] are the only mobs that can attack players outside of their designated fields.
* There is a {{Cost|Ticket||4=1}} token located within its residence (which is the Spider Cave).
* The Spider gives more {{Cost|Honey||4=}} and gives better rewards than [[Mantis|Mantises]] and [[Scorpion]]s despite them being in higher-tier fields.
* The Spider is tied with the [[Werewolf]] as the regular mobs with the least amount of spawn locations, as they both only have one.
* Unlike real-life spiders, who have eight legs and six/eight eyes, the spider only has six legs and four eyes. It also appears to have a head and thorax, despite spiders and other arachnids having a cephalothorax. This is joked about in the Forward Facing Spider [[Stickers|sticker]]'s description, which reads: ''"Not enough legs to technically be a spider."''
* Two creatures similar to the spider in appearance called [[Cave Monster]]s are found inside the [[Werewolf's Cave]]. However, they have vastly different stats.
* Spiders have a faint red glow under their eyes, but it’s mostly visible at night.
