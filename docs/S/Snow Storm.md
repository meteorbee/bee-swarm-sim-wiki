# Snow Storm

{{RemovedContent}}
[[File:Snowstorm.png|right|frameless|200x200px|An active Snow Storm.|alt=An active snow storm.]]
A '''Snow Storm''' is a server-wide event that can be summoned by using the [[Snow Machine]] or randomly in the game. It is similar to a [[Honeystorm]], but spawns {{Cost|Snowflake||}} instead. A Snow Storm lasts for 20 seconds.

When a player uses the Snow Machine to summon a Snow Storm, a server-wide announcement states: {{Notification||❄️ {Username} has activated the Snow Machine! ❄️|18}}

{{Cost|Snowflake}} will start to fall rapidly from the sky on every field. There is no audio at the beginning of a Snow Storm, unlike a Honeystorm or [[Mythic Meteor Shower]].

When a Snow Storm is randomly summoned, the message states: {{Notification||❄️ A Snow Storm has begun... ❄️}}

==Trivia ==
*Occasionally, [[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] would spawn a Snow Storm, usually under an alias. This is similar to how he globally summons Mythic Meteor Showers, [[Day/Night Cycle#Night|nighttime]], special [[sprout]]s, and [[mobs]].
*Like Honeystorms and mythic meteor showers, Snow Storms can light up the [[Werewolf's Cave]] enough to see the [[Cave Monster]]s and the {{Cost|Gold Egg|4=1}} at the end of the cave. 
**Unlike Honeystorms, Snow Storms don't spawn snowflake tokens in the [[Ant Challenge|Ant Field]].
* During Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022, and 2024, giving [[Spirit Bear]] a {{Cost|Present|4=1}} would trigger a Snow Storm after her dialogue finishes and the [[Ornaments|ornament]] was given. 
**Finishing Spirit Bear’s Beesmas quest would also summon a Snow Storm.
* The {{Cost|Snowflake||}} keep falling for a few seconds after a Snow Storm ends because it takes time for the {{Cost|Snowflake||}} to reach the ground.
*If a Snow Storm is summoned during [[Day/Night Cycle#Night|nighttime]], the white fog will stay even after the Snow Storm has ended until nighttime ends.
