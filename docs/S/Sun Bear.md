'''Sun Bear''' is a [[:Category:Traveling Bears|traveling bear]] who appeared during special events. He is one of the four temporary [[Quest Givers]], the others being [[Bee Bear]], [[Gummy Bear]], and [[Bubble Bee Man]].

He made five appearances. His first visit was from April 27, 2018, to May 13, 2018. He gave 12 quests with rewards including the [[Belt Bag]] and a final reward of a [[Mondo Belt Bag]]. His second visit was from September 10, 2018, to October 8, 2018. He gave 14 quests with rewards including the [[Basic Boots]], the [[Hiking Boots]], and the [[Beekeeper's Boots]]. His third visit was from September 28, 2019, to October 28, 2019. He gave 10 quests themed around [[Bean Bugs]], with a final reward of 250 [[Ticket]]s. His fourth visit was from April 6, 2020 to May 21, 2020, for the [[Egg Hunt 2020|Roblox Egg Hunt 2020]]. His fifth visit was in August 1, 2024 for the [[The Games|Roblox "The Games"]] event, later the bear stayed in the game for sometime and at October 29, 2024, he received a new quest line. He left from his fifth visit on April 30, 2025, the same day 2024 Winter Beesmas ended'''.'''

== Trivia==
*He was the only traveling bear to not introduce his own [[Bees/Event|event bee]].
*In the quest ''''Focus Feature'''', Sun Bear said to defeat a [[Werewolf]] instead of 3 [[Spider]]s.
*The [[Badges#Ability Badge|Ability Badge]] is represented by him in the game's description.
* After his spring visit, the belt bags can be bought in [[Pro Shop]] ([[Belt Bag]]) and the [[Mountain Top Shop]] ([[Mondo Belt Bag]]).
*During his spring visit, there was a [[Ticket]] behind Sun Bear’s RV. When Sun Bear went away, the ticket stayed there for a little time, but now it's gone.
*Sun Bear uses the [https://web.roblox.com/catalog/734338331/Knight-Animation-Package Knight Animation Package].
*Sun Bear says that only [[Rage Bee|Rage]], [[Exhausted Bee|Exhausted]], and [[Music Bee|Music]] Bees spawn [[Ability Tokens#Token Link|Token Link]], but [[Shocked Bee]] does as well. This was fixed a few days later.
*In his second visit ([[Updates#2018.09.10|9/10/2018 Update]]) and his third visit ([[Updates#2019.09.28|9/28/2019 Update]]), his RV had a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Phoenix Flag of Phoenix] bumper sticker on the back.
*During his first visit, Sun Bear was shown to be wearing the [[Mondo Belt Bag]], which he gave at the very end of his quests. During his second visit, he was shown to be wearing both the Mondo Belt Bag and [[Beekeeper's Boots]], which he gave at the end of his quests.
*Sun Bear gives {{Cost|Ticket|48}} throughout all his quests with the ticket token that was behind the RV.
*During the quest ''''Sun Bear's Scavenger Hunt'''', he says 3 more quests are needed to be completed to get the [[Beekeeper's Boots]], when it is actually 4 quests. However, [[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] could have done this on purpose, because Sun Bear was completely surprised by [[Rogue Vicious Bee|Vicious Bee]] in his dialogue, and gives you a quest around this.
*He was the first [[:Category:Traveling Bears|traveling bear]] added to the game, with [[Gummy Bear]] being the second and [[Bee Bear]] being the third.
*The item prices of his rewards after his visit are: [[Basic Boots]] are 4,400, [[Hiking Boots]] are 2,200,000, Beekeeper's Boots are 15,000,000, [[Belt Bag]] is 440,000, and [[Mondo Belt Bag]] is 12,400,000 [[Honey]].
*He was the only [[:Category:Traveling Bears|traveling bear]] who never gave an egg on his last quest.
*He and [[Bubble Bee Man]] were the only [[:Category:NPC|NPCs]] to give an [[:Category:Accessories|Accessory]].
*It was hinted that Sun Bear is from Arizona, this is from the [[Codes|Code]] "'''Arizona'''", and the City of Arizona flag that was on his RV the second and the third visit.
*During the quests in his third visit, it is revealed that the reason he had [[Bean Bugs]] was that he was taking them to Orlando for an unknown reason.
*In the quest ''''Total Focus'''', Sun Bear said to collect 200 [[Ability Tokens#Focus|Focus Tokens]] before you received the quest, but he didn't say to collect 200 Focus Tokens after you received the quest.
* For the ''''Total Focus'''' quest, after you finish the dialogue with Sun Bear, a [[Memory Match]] game will start automatically for free.
*He stated only [[Basic Bee]] doesn't generate tokens, but [[Brave Bee]] doesn't as well, even though both of them can generate [[Ability Tokens#Inspire|Inspire tokens]] when they are [[Gifted Bee|gifted]].
*To complete every quest on his third visit, you must collect 3835 tokens from [[Bean Bugs]].
* It was possible for his trapped Bean Bug to jump out of its cage.
**It was also possible for the player to go in and out of the [[Bean Bugs|Bean Bug's]] cage.
*Onett has stated that Sun Bear isn't welcome on the mountain anymore for accidentally releasing [[Bean Bugs]] and the [[chicks]]. It seems as if Sun Bear was unbanned in 2024 with his visit during [[The Games]].
*In “Boot Lootin” Sun Bear mentions that he has been to a desert before.
*In “Light Up The Night” Sun Bear mentions that he prefers [[Day/Night Cycle|Nighttime]] to Daytime, as he is able to relax.
*Sun Bear likes [[Mantis|mantises]]. Referring to them as ‘cool’ in his quest “Show Me The Honey”.
*Sun Bear mentions in his quest “Vicious Bee Begone” that he has had an encounter with a [[Rogue Vicious Bee]] while going to visit [[Brown Bear]].
*In his quest “Total Focus” Sun Bear mentions that he transports ‘exotic things’ around in his RV as a job.
