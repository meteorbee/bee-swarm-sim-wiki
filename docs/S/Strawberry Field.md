# Strawberry Field

{{Field|width = 22|height = 26|image = StrawberryFieldBeesmas2021.png|fieldcolor = Red|bees = 5}}The '''Strawberry Field''' is a [[field]] located past the [[Basic Bee Gate]], to the left of the [[Spider Field]], and beside the [[Vicious Bee Egg Claim]]. This field is protected by 2 [[ladybug]]s; one of which is level 2 and the other level 3.

It is a 22x26 field, containing 572 [[flowers]]. The flowers are red and white, in sizes of single, double, and triple.

This field can spawn [[honey]], [[Strawberry]], [[Ability Tokens#Boost|Red Boost]], [[Red Extract]], [[Bitterberry]], [[Ticket]] and [[Royal Jelly]] tokens while collecting [[pollen]].

[[Bees]] that like the Strawberry Field are [[Fire Bee]], [[Riley Bee]], and [[Shy Bee]]. Bees that dislike the Strawberry Field are [[Cool Bee]], [[Bubble Bee]], [[Bucko Bee]], and [[Windy Bee]].

This field grants Refreshing [[Nectar]] when a [[planter]] is placed here.

== Obtaining Boosts ==
* Using the [[Red Field Booster]] may give a Strawberry [[Field Boost]]. When it does, it will give +100% Strawberry Field pollen for 15 minutes. [[File:Strawberry Field Icon.png|thumb|75px|The Strawberry Field boost icon.]]
* Using [[glitter]] in the Strawberry Field will add 100% Strawberry Field pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if the boost isn't already maxed out.
* Using [[Field Dice|field dice]], [[Smooth Dice|smooth dice]], or [[Loaded Dice|loaded dice]] may add 100% - 300% Strawberry Field pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out and also what type of dice is used.
*Strawberry [[Field Wind]]s can be obtained through the [[Wind Shrine]] or [[codes]]. They have a base of +20% instant Strawberry Field conversion, and give +5% instant Strawberry Field conversion and +15% Strawberry Field pollen for each stack. Strawberry Field Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.
*The field can be boosted by the [[King Beetle Amulet]]. If it has a Strawberry Field Boost, it can boost the field by +1% up to a max of +100% pollen.

==Trivia==

*This is the third smallest field in the game, outranked by the [[Stump Field]] and [[Pumpkin Patch]].
*There is a strawberry token behind the biggest strawberry in the top left of the field.
*This is one of six fields that [[fireflies]] can travel to during [[Day/Night Cycle|nighttime]], with the others being [[Bamboo Field]], [[Cactus Field]], [[Pineapple Patch]], [[Rose Field]], and Spider Field.
*[[Mother Bear]] states that finding strawberry tokens are much more common in this field than in any other field.
*According to [[Spirit Bear]], the Strawberry in the field were planted from seeds imported from Sweden. She also states that [[sprout]]s in this field spawn the most strawberry tokens, 8 times more than normal sprouts.
*If you run towards the strawberry on the corner fast enough, you may glitch inside of the strawberry. If this happens you will have to reset your character, rejoin the game, or use a {{Cost|Whirligig|||0}}.
*This the only red field that gives [[Nectar|Refreshing Nectar]].
