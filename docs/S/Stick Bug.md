'''Stick Bug''' is a [[:Category:NPC|NPC]] found on the tallest mushroom in the [[Mushroom Field]], next to the [[Top Stick Bug Fighters]] Leaderboard. It is accessible by using the [[Red Cannon]]/[[Yellow Cannon]]/[[Blue Cannon]] with the [[Parachute]]/[[Glider]], or using the [[Beekeeper's Boots]] and jumping from the second mushroom to the one with the {{Cost|Royal Jelly|4=0}}. Players will need a {{Cost|Translator|4=0}} to talk to Stick Bug, where they can then ask it to start the [[Stick Bug Challenge]]. Stick Bug then appears as a boss [[Mobs|mob]] and always spawns in the [[Sunflower Field]] first. Upon defeat, it flees to a random field from this list:
* [[Sunflower Field]]
* [[Clover Field]]
* [[Strawberry Field]]
* [[Bamboo Field]]
* [[Pineapple Patch]]
* [[Cactus Field]]
* [[Pumpkin Patch]]
* [[Mountain Top Field]]

The player can start the Stick Bug Challenge for free every 1 and a half days (1d, 12hr),or by initiating it anytime for 50 [[Ticket]]s, or by giving Stick Bug a [[Present]] during the 2018, 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2024 Beesmas Event.

When the challenge ends, the player will be awarded an [[Stick Bug Amulet|amulet]] depending on their score (''More information in the [[Stick Bug Amulet]] page'').

==Trivia==
* If the player gave Stick Bug a Present, started its Egg Hunt quest, started its 2019 Beesmas Quest, or finished any of its Beesmas quests (except for 2019), the Stick Bug Challenge would automatically start.
* Stick Bug, [[Shadow Bear]], the [[Cub Buddy|Cub Buddies]], [[Bee Bear]] and [[Spirit Bear]] are the only NPCs that dance.
* There is a [[Glitches#Stick Bug Falls Down|glitch]] where Stick Bug may fall over from its regular location on top of the tallest mushroom. If this occurs, it will either be in the [[Dandelion Field]], the Mushroom Field, or somewhere in between.
** Another glitch is on the [[Brave Bee Gate]] ramp, where walking to the bottom of the ramp and on the corner of the Bamboo Field will cause Stick Bug to briefly get stuck there when it tries to follow the player.
* In Beesmas 2019, Stick Bug gave the most honey when given a present ({{Cost|Honey|33,333,333}}), the next being [[Panda Bear]] at only {{Cost|Honey|5,000}}.
* If Stick Bug moves to the Mountain Top Field, it has a chance of falling and getting stuck in the Pineapple Patch or Pumpkin Patch.
* Stick Bug and Wild Windy Bee are the only mobs that are impossible to kill, as depleting all of their HP will only level them up and regain their HP.
* Stick Bug waves when the challenge ends.
* Stick Bug is one of the three NPCs that requires a translator to interact with, the others being [[Gifted Riley Bee]] and [[Gifted Bucko Bee]].
* Stick Bug's boss fight theme became part of the popular bait-and-switch meme "Get stickbugged lol", which spread far from the BSS Community.
** Stick Bug is aware of this meme, indicated by his Beesmas 2020 dialogue for receiving a present.
* In [[Spirit Bear|Spirit Bear's]] dialogue, she states that Stick Bug scared her as a cub, which indicates that Stick Bug has been around for a long time.
* Stick Bug's Beesmas 2022 Quest originally gave {{Cost|Caustic Wax|1}}, no {{Cost|Swirled Wax}}, no {{Cost|Ticket Planter}}, and {{Cost|Invigorating Vial|1}}. This was soon buffed to {{Cost|Caustic Wax|3}}, {{Cost|Swirled Wax|1}}, {{Cost|Ticket Planter|1}}, and {{Cost|Invigorating Vial|2}}.
* As stated by [[Spirit Bear]], Stick Bug loves [[Pineapple|pineapples]].
* Stick Bug is the only NPC, who needs a [[translator]], but he doesnt give permanent quests. 
* During [[Updates#2024.07.17|Beesmas 2024]], [[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] would start a Festive [[Stick Bug Challenge]] at random every few days.
* During the [[Updates#2019.04.17|Egg Hunt 2019 Event Update]], Stick Bug would plant an Epic, Legendary, and Supreme Sprout after finishing its [[Egg Hunt 2019|Egg Hunt]] quest.
** This makes Stick Bug the only NPC able to spawn sprouts.
