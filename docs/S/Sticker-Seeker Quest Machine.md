# Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine

<blockquote>''This article is about the quest giver. For the tool, see [[Sticker-Seeker]].''</blockquote>
[[File:StickerQuest Giver.png|thumb|209px|The Sticker Quest Giver and the Sticker-Seeker leaderboard.]]
The '''Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine''' is a [[Quest Givers|quest giver]] in the [[Hive Hub]]. To interact with it, the player must own and have the [[Sticker-Seeker]] equipped. {{Hover|The player must also have a total of 15 bees hatched to interact with the machine.|Note that the player can enter the Hive Hub without any bees by joining a friend, but the machine itself requires the player to have 15 bees to be interactable.}}

It has infinite quests, but only has confirmed milestone rewards until quest 600. 

== Quests ==
Obtaining and turning in [[Quests]] from this quest giver require the player to have the Sticker-Seeker equipped. Usually, 3 requirements are given, but starting at rank 100, there are always 4. 

For the requirements listed below, let ''X'' represent the player's rank, or the number of quests completed plus one (with lowercase x representing the player's rank - 1),  and ''Z'' be the number of Stickers from whatever requirement the player has to obtain. == Ranks ==
A player's rank is the amount of Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine's quests they have completed. Ranks are also the way the [[Top Sticker-Seekers]] leaderboard is measured, and increases every time a player completes a quest. The higher the rank, the better the rewards. For example, a player is more likely to get Sticker Planters when they have achieved further ranks than players with a Rank of 5. Quest requirement amounts also scale to the ranks.

== Rewards ==
Every quest reward always includes {{Cost|Honey}}, a {{Cost|Ticket|||0}}, {{Cost|Royal Jelly}}, and a random [[Sticker]], as well as many other possible rewards, including {{Cost|Sticker Planter}} (in which you are guarenteed one every 111 quests and a random chance to get an one on normal quest) or [[Balloon]]s. Both are determined by the Rank, or number of quests the player has completed. Every once in a while, the player reaches a Rank milestone, which rewards an [[Egg]], like a {{Cost|Silver Egg|||0}} at the 25th Rank completion. It has a very low chance to give a basic color hive, but it is possible in any rank.

== Trivia ==
* It is the only [[Quest Givers|Quest Giver]] that does not have any dialogue and does not notify to talk to the Machine again after quest completion.
* The Sticker Quest Giver is the fourth randomized [[Quest Givers|Quest Giver]] to give [[egg]]s at certain milestones, the other three being [[Gifted Riley Bee]], [[Gifted Bucko Bee]], and [[Brown Bear]].
* The quests it gives always includes the requirements "Collect {amount} of Pollen using [[Sticker-Seeker]]," and "Find 2-7 Seeker-Stickers hidden in {zones}."
* Sometimes, a quest requirement can get so long you can’t see part of it.
** Progressing in the requirement a bit may fix this problem.
* When having the Sticker-Seeker equipped and standing on the pad with the machine in front of it, normally there is a message saying “Your Sticker-Seeker quest is in progress…” (if you have not yet completed the quest). However, after standing on the pad for a few seconds, it changes to the next egg reward, and at what rank you will obtain it.
* The Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine has 8 Stickers on it.
