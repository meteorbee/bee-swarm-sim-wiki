It takes 20 minutes for a scorpion to respawn after being defeated (17 minutes with [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]] [[Vicious Bee]]). Defeating it guarantees 5 [[Battle Points]] and 1000 [[Honey]]. Various other rewards are possible with their probability increasing with [[Loot Luck]]. It deals 25 damage to the player (before accounting [[System Page#Defense|Defense]]).
