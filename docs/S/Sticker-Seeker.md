The '''Sticker-Seeker''' is a [[Tools|tool]] that was released on the 1/12/2024 update and can be crafted in the [[Hub Field Shop]].
{| class="article-table"
!Crafting Ingredients
|-
|{{Cost|Honey|7,000,000}}<br>{{Cost|Glue|1}}<br>{{Cost|Oil|1}}<br>{{Cost|Soft Wax|5}}<br>{{Cost|Neonberry|5}}<br>{{Cost|Micro-Converter|10}}
|}

The '''Sticker-Seeker''' collects 10 pollen from 13 patches in 1 second. It has a chance to find [[Sticker]]s in fields and unlocks the Sticker-Seeker quest. When you equip the Sticker-Seeker, you have the ability to find [[Sticker#Seeker Stickers|Seeker-Stickers]]. Once you click on a Seeker-Sticker, there will be a message on the bottom-right hand corner saying, "🔎You found a Seeker-Sticker! ({amount} to go)" 

The Sticker-Seeker has a chance to dig up a sticker in the field you are in. Once you dig it up, like the Seeker-Sticker, it will say, "Oh! Your '''Sticker-Seeker''' dug something weird up." The sticker appears as a rainbow token on a random flower on the field you're in.

==Trivia== 
*The Sticker-Seeker is the only tool you can use to spot and obtain Seeker-Stickers as a quest requirement in the [[Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine]] 
*Not counting the Seeker-Stickers that appear when equipping this tool, the Sticker-Seeker is the only tool in the game with no associated tool sticker. This is ironic considering that the secondary function of this tool revolves around locating stickers.
*This tool bears a striking resemblance to a [[Wikipedia:Magnifying glass|magnifying glass]].
*This is the tied with the [[Pulsar]] and the [[Gummyballer]] for the slowest tool in the game (in terms of how long the cooldown lasts).
