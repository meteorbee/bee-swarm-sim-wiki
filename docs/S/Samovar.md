# Samovar

{{RemovedContent}}
[[File:Completedsamovar.png|thumb|272x272px|The Samovar when completed.]]
The '''Samovar''' is a [[:Category:Machines|machine]] that was added in [[Beesmas]] 2021 and has returned in Beesmas 2022 and 2024. This machine can be unlocked by completing [[Dapper Bear]]'s Beesmas quest, and when activated, it rewards a random [[Nectar]] type and [[items]] near the cup. The Samovar has a cooldown of 6 hours.

If a player stands on the activation platform with Samovar without completing Dapper Bear's Beesmas 2021/2022 quest, the following message will appear: "''This strange thing is looking a little rusty...''"

== Drops ==
''It is important to note that the Samovar lists how many times it was used '''previously''', not how many uses it is on currently.<br />For example: If it says it has been used 24 times, then you are on the 25th use.''

Every time the Samovar is used, the amount of Nectar received will increase by 5 minutes, capping at 4 hours 12 minutes. {{Cost|Honeysuckle|}} increases by 1 each use, stopping at {{Cost|Honeysuckle|30}}.

The Samovar has a fixed cycle of rewards: {{Cost|Oil|||0}} -> {{Cost|Enzymes}} -> {{Cost|Gumdrops}} -> {{Cost|Glitter|||0}} -> {{Cost|Ticket Planter|||0}}. On the 25th use, the Ticket Planter is replaced with a {{Cost|Turpentine|||0}} as a 1 time reward (there aren't any further milestones at 50, 100, etc.). It will continue to cycle through the first 5 rewards afterward.

Guaranteed:
* {{Cost|Honeysuckle|||0}} (Starts from 3, increases by 1 each use and caps at 30).
* Random [[Nectar]] buff (Starts from 1 Hour, increases by 5 minutes each use and caps at 4 hours (4 hours and 12 minutes if you have the [[Ornaments|Potpourri]] ornament)).

Bonus drops:
* {{Cost|Oil|1}}
* {{Cost|Enzymes|1}}
* {{Cost|Gumdrops|25}}
* {{Cost|Glitter|1}}
* {{Cost|Ticket Planter|1}}
* {{Cost|Turpentine|1}} (25th use only).

== Trivia ==
* This and the [[Robo Bear's Party Cake|Party Cake]] are currently the only Beesmas decorations not added in the Beesmas 2020 event.
* It would take 36 uses to get the max amount of nectar from this machine.
* The nectar collected from the Samovar will contribute to quests requiring a specific type of nectar.
*The samovar would be used 284 times if you used it every 6 hours from the beginning of beesmas to the end of beesmas.
