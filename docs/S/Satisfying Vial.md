# Satisfying Vial

{{Consumables|image=Satisfying_vial.png|cooldown=1 Second|cap=10|description=Grants 4 hours of Satisfying Nectar. (Boosts: White Pollen, Red Convert Rate, Honey at Hive)}}

The {{Color|Satisfying Vial}} is a consumable [[Nectar Vial|nectar vial]] [[Items|item]] that was added in the [[Updates#2022.12.26|12/26/2022 update]]. Once used, it grants the player 4 hours of the [[Nectar|Satisfying Nectar buff]] instantly. It can also be used to craft other [[:Category:Accessories|accessories]] and [[tools]].

== Ways to Obtain ==
* Completing [[Dapper Bear|Dapper Bear's]] 5th, 11th and 15th quests grants 1 Satisfying Vial and other items.
* Using the [[Nectar Condenser]] to condense 12 hours of Satisfying Nectar.
* Adding the Satisfying Nectar Icon to the [[Sticker Stack]] grants 1 Satisfying Vial.
