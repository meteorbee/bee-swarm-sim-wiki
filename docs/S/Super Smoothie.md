# Super Smoothie

{{Consumables|image = Super Smoothie.png|description = Grants the improved boosts of many consumable items combined for 20 minutes!|cooldown = 20 minutes|}}
The {{Color|Super Smoothie}} is a [[Crafting|craftable]] [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] added in the [[Updates#2020.06.06|6/6/2020 update]]. When used, it grants the following for 20 minutes:
*x1.5 [[System Page#Capacity Multiplier|Capacity]].
*x1.6 [[System Page#Red Pollen|Red Pollen]].
*x1.6 [[System Page#Blue Pollen|Blue Pollen]].
*x1.6 [[System Page#White Pollen|White Pollen]].
*x1.4 [[System Page#Bee Gather Pollen|Bee Gather Pollen]].
*x1.4 [[System Page#Pollen From Tools|Pollen From Tools]].
*x1.5 [[System Page#Convert Rate|Convert Rate]].
*x1.5 [[System Page#Hive Convert Rate|Convert Rate At Hive]].
* +20% [[System Page#Instant Conversion|Instant Conversion]].
*+1% [[System Page#Super-Crit Chance|Super-Crit Chance]].
*+6% [[System Page#Critical Chance|Critical Chance]].
*x1.25 [[Stats#Speed|Bee Movespeed]].
*x1.25 [[System Page#Movespeed|Player Movespeed]].
{{Cost|Red Extract}}, {{Cost|Blue Extract}}, {{Cost|Glue}}, {{Cost|Oil}}, {{Cost|Tropical Drink}}, {{Cost|Purple Potion}}, or {{Cost|Enzymes}} buffs cannot be active simultaneously along with {{Cost|Super Smoothie|4=1}}. If any of these buffs are active, when a {{Cost|Super Smoothie|4=1}} is used, they will be overridden.

== Ways to Obtain==
* [[Crafting]] via [[Blender]] for:
** {{Cost|Neonberry|3}}
** {{Cost|Star Jelly|3}}
** {{Cost|Purple Potion|3}}
** {{Cost|Tropical Drink|6}}

** {{Cost|Super Smoothie|50|4=}} are included in the 50 Super Smoothies Pack
** {{Cost|Super Smoothie|10|4=}} are included in the Triumphant Turp Pack
* As an extremely rare drop from defeating certain [[mobs]], such as the [[Coconut Crab]] or [[Tunnel Bear]].
* As an extremely rare drop from [[Mythic Meteor Shower]]s.
* As a reward from the [[Robo Bear Challenge]].
* As a very rare drop from [[planter]]s.
** {{Cost|Plastic Planter}} have a rare chance of dropping {{Cost|Super Smoothie|1|4=1}} in any field, 36 days cooldown.
** {{Cost|Paper Planter}} have a very rare chance of dropping {{Cost|Super Smoothie|1|4=1}} in any field, 36 days cooldown.
** {{Cost|The Planter Of Plenty|4=1}} drops 10 Super Smoothies when harvested from the [[Bamboo Field]], 36 days cooldown.
** {{Cost|Candy Planter}} have a low chance to drop {{Cost|Super Smoothie|1-3|4=1}}.
* As a reward from certain [[quests]]: 
** [[Dapper Bear]]'s "More Than Myth" (#15) gives {{Cost|Super Smoothie|1|4=1}} and other items.
** [[Science Bear]]'s "Corrupting The Glitched Drive" gives {{Cost|Super Smoothie|1|4=1}} and other items.

==Trivia==

* This is one of the four [[:Category:Consumables|consumable]] items that increases capacity, with the others being {{Cost|Marshmallow Bee}}, {{Cost|Purple Potion}} and {{Cost|Snowflake}}.
*{{Cost|Super Smoothie||4=}} were once misspelled as "Super Smoothy".
*The {{Cost|Super Smoothie||4=1}} is the consumable that gives the highest number of different buffs in the game.

**{{Cost|Magic Bean|9}} or {{Cost|Ticket|180}}

** {{Cost|Moon Charm|225}}

** {{Cost|Magic Bean|9}} or {{Cost|Ticket|180}}
** {{Cost|Gumdrops|450}} or {{Cost|Ticket|150}}
* The {{Cost|Super Smoothie||4=1}} is one of the items that cannot be donated to the [[Wind Shrine]]. 
** The {{Cost|Super Smoothie||4=1}} is also the only craftable item that cannot be donated to the [[Wind Shrine]].
* [[Bee Bear]] calls this the "ultimate boost granting beverage" in his 2021 Beesmas quest, "Quality Cousin Time".
** The only other mention of a smoothie is by [[Polar Bear]] and his "Thick Smoothie" quest.
* The {{Cost|Super Smoothie||4=1}} used to have +7% [[Critical Hits|Critical Chance]] and no [[Critical Hits|Super-Crit Chance]].
