# Sprout

''<sup>Were you looking for [[Magic Bean]]?</sup>''

[[File:Sprouttransparent.png|thumb|The sprout model.]]

A '''sprout''' is a plant that spawns in the center of a [[Fields|field]]. Sprouts emit a bright yellow beacon that reaches the sky, and have a counter at the base of it representing a [[pollen]] amount. When players collect pollen, or when a [[Clouds|cloud]] is on the field, the number counts down and it gradually grows taller. Once the pollen counter reaches 0, it sends out shockwave and scatters various tokens throughout the field. If no interaction with a sprout happens for more than 5 minutes, it will despawn.

== Spawning ==
Sprouts can spawn randomly around the map and can also be planted using {{Cost|Magic Bean}}. It can also be summoned by using the [[Special Sprout Summoner]] near the [[Red HQ]], by anyone who has discovered all eight [[Bees/Legendary|Legendary Bee]] types, once every sixteen hours. 

When a sprout spawns, it will broadcast a server-wide message: <br>
{{Notification|Honey|🌱A (Rarity) Sprout has appeared...🌱}} 

If a player has planted a sprout, the message would read:<br>
{{Notification|Honey|🌱 {Username} has planted a (Rarity) Sprout...🌱}} 

If Sticker Sprouts are about to spawn in [[Hive Hub]], the message will read:<br>
{{Notification|Sticker|🌱 Sticker Sprouts are about to spawn in the Hive Hub...🌱}}

When the Sticker Sprout has spawned in the Hive Hub, the message will read: <br>
{{Notification|Sticker|🌱 A Sticker Sprout has spawned in the Hive Hub...🌱}}

Sprouts cannot spawn or be planted in the [[Ant Field]].

== Variants ==
There are ten different variants of sprouts:
*Sprout (Green) (Common)
*Rare Sprout (Silver) (Rare)
*Epic Sprout (Gold) (Very Rare)
*Legendary Sprout (Teal) (Extremely Rare)
*Supreme Sprout (Luminescent green) (Unfathomably Rare)
*Moon Sprout (Only available during nighttime (common), luminescent blue during both night and day)
*Gummy Sprout (Translucent pink) (Very Rare)
*Sticker Sprout (Spawns only every 3 hours in the [[Hub Field]], or a extremely rare chance when planting a magic bean in the Hub Field) (Rainbow, colors shifting)
*Festive Sprout (Planted by {{Cost|Festive Bean}} or [[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] (White and Red stripes)
*Debug Sprout (Can only be planted by [[Onett]]) (Black)
The amount of pollen needed for the sprout for it to be harvested depends on the rarity of the sprout and the field it's in.

== Drops ==
=== Location Specific Drops ===
Depending on where the sprout is planted, the drops spawned can vary in amounts or may drop certain items.
*A sprout in a [[:Category:Red|red]] field yields 4 times more {{Cost|Strawberry}} than in any other field. No {{Cost|Blueberry}} will spawn.
**A sprout in the [[Strawberry Field]] will yield twice as many strawberries than sprouts in other red fields.
*A sprout in a [[:Category:Blue|Blue]] field yields 6 times more blueberries than in any other field. No strawberries will spawn.
*A sprout in the [[Sunflower Field]] yields 7 times more {{Cost|Sunflower Seed}} than in any other field.
*A sprout in the [[Pineapple Patch]] yields 7 times more {{Cost|Pineapple}} than in any other field.
*A sprout in the [[Windy Bee Gate|35 Bee Zone]] yields 20% more rewards than sprouts in other areas. It also requires significantly more pollen to pop.
*A sprout in the [[Coconut Field]] yields {{Cost|Coconut}} and {{Cost|Tropical Drink}} in addition to other treats.
*Other fields may produce more regular {{Cost|Treat}} than any other treats depending on the field.<!-- needs documentation -->

==Tips==
* Various items, abilities, and equipment can boost player movespeed, helping to collect tokens more efficiently before they despawn.
**Using {{Cost|Oil}} grants 1.2x player movespeed.
**Equipping the [[Hasty Guard]] grants 1.1x player movespeed.
**Having [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Ability Tokens|haste]] can increase the player's [[System Page#Movespeed|movespeed]] up to 2x.
***[[Ability Tokens#Haste|Haste producing bees]] are the primary source of this buff.
***The Honey, Treat, Strawberry, and Blueberry [[Dispensers]] give 5 stacks of Haste.
***The [[Royal Jelly Dispenser]] gives 10 stacks of Haste.
**If the player and has either the [[Coconut Clogs]] or the [[Gummy Boots]] equipped, they can have a coconut fall on them and grant the player a short burst of [[Passive Abilities#Coconut Haste|Coconut Haste Surge]].
**[[Bear Bee|Bear Bee's]] {{Cost|Bear Morph}} [[Ability Tokens|ability]] can significantly increase player movespeed as well.
**The Free Royal Jelly Dispenser gives a stack of Haste+.
*[[Tadpole Bee]] and the {{Cost|Box-O-Frogs}} can summon [[frog]]s that collect sprout tokens.
*The [[Passive Abilities#Star Saw|Star Saw]] can collect massive amounts of sprout tokens.
*[[Windy Bee|Windy Bee's]] {{Cost|Tornado}} can also collect massive amounts of sprout tokens.
*{{Cost|Cub Buddy}} can also collect sprout tokens.

==Trivia==
*If a {{Cost|Festive Bean|||0}} is planted by a player, the {{Cost|Festive Blessing}} and {{Cost|Beesmas Cheer}} buffs  are only obtainable by the player that planted it.  
**If the sprout is planted by Onett, anyone can collect the tokens.
*Gummy Sprouts are the only sprouts that are translucent.
*Most sprout notifications are displayed in gold, but a few have unique displays:
**Gummy Sprouts have a light purple notification.
**Supreme, Moon, and Debug Sprouts have gray notifications.
**Festive Sprouts have a red notification.
**Sticker Sprouts have a rainbow notification.
*Many of the treat drop statistics are mentioned in Spirit Bear's dialogue.
*Sprouts were originally called "Seedlings" when they were released and then the name got changed in the [[Updates#2019.12.23|12/23/2019 update]]. Its original message was "🌱A Seedling has sprouted...🌱"
*The Supreme Sprout previously required half the [[pollen]] that a Legendary Sprout would need. It now requires 50% more pollen than a Legendary Sprout.

*Supreme Sprouts were formerly named "Mythical Sprouts" before the 12/23/2019 update.
*If the player turned in [[Stick Bug#Egg Hunt 2019 Quest|Stick Bug's Egg Hunt 2019 Quest]], Stick Bug spawned an Epic, Legendary, and a Supreme Sprout.
*The "[[Special Sprout Summoner]]" was formerly called "Sprout Summoner", and it would plant any type of sprout randomly except for Debug, Festive and Sticker Sprouts.
*The Debug Sprout is the only sprout that does not drop honey tokens.
*If a sprout spawns before a player joins the server, the sprout and the sprout's pollen counter will be invisible to that player, however it can still be popped by the player. 
*On mobile, there is a glitch where the sprouts in the server are invisible and can't be popped by the player.
*Sticker Sprouts are the only sprouts that grow on their own (without clouds). It loses about 1M pollen per second, even if no one is collecting in the Hub Field.
*If the player who spawned the sprout leaves before the sprout has popped, the sprout will not spawn anything.
*The debug sprout is the only type of sprout that cannot be planted by a player (besides Onett, who can plant them at will.)
*Sprouts are mentioned by [[Sun Bear]] to only exist on the Mountain. As he mentions “I've been all around the world, and I've never seen ANYTHING like those before.”
*If the player is lagging, they may see the pollen number on the sprout become negative for a brief moment when they pop it.
*Supreme Sprouts and Moon Sprouts are the only sprouts that glow.
