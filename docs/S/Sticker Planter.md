It is guaranteed to spawn a rare+ [[Puffshroom]]. Only {{Cost|Sticker Planter|1}} can be active at a time. It has a cap of 25.

==Ways To Obtain==
*As a drop from [[Sprouts|Sticker Sprouts]].
*By purchasing it in the [[Robux Shop]] for 100 Robux. however, it requires having 20 bees to gain access to purchasing it and has a limit of 10 per player.
*
*Completing [[Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine|Sticker-Seeker]] quests.    
**Completing a Rank that has 3 digits with all numbers repeated will grant ((Quests Completed)/111) Sticker Planters: 
***Completing 111 Quests Guarantees a Sticker Planter.
***Completing 222 Quests Guarantees 2 Sticker Planters.
***Completing 333 Quests Guarantees 3 Sticker Planters.
***Completing 444 Quests Guarantees 4 Sticker Planters.
***Completing 555 Quests Guarantees 5 Sticker Planters.
***Completing 666 Quests Guarantees 6 Sticker Planters.
***Completing 777 Quests Guarantees 7 Sticker Planters.
***Completing 888 Quests Guarantees 8 Sticker Planters.
***Completing 999 Quests Guarantees 9 Sticker Planters.
*From purchasing the Planter Sampler Pack from [[Bee Bear's Catalog]].

==Trivia ==
*The Sticker Planter continuously alternates between different colors, which can be tuned down in the [[Settings]].
*Out of the four single-use planters, the Sticker Planter is the only one of them to have a cap of 25.
*This, the {{Cost|Ticket Planter|4=1}} and the{{Cost|Paper Planter|4=1}} share the same base models.
**They also share the same outline for their decals.
*It is tied with the Ticket Planter for the second-best Nectar multiplier in the game, behind the [[Festive Planter]] at x3.
*It is one of four single-use planters in the game, the others being the Festive Planter, the Ticket Planter and the Paper Planter.
*This and the Ticket Planter are the only planters that will always spawn a [[Puffshroom]] at full growth.
**Additionally, this is the only planter to always spawn at least a Rare Puffshroom.
