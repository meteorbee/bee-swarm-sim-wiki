{{Color|Spirit Petal|Spirit Petals}} are a special [[crafting]] material that can be used as one of the ingredients to craft the [[Petal Wand]] and the [[Petal Belt]]. It can also be donated to the [[Wind Shrine]] to allow the player a chance of obtaining a [[Windy Bee]] (or a {{Cost|Star Treat|||0}}, if player already had [[Windy Bee]]) when donating {{Cost|Cloud Vial}}. Note that players are only able to donate one {{Cost|Spirit Petal||4=1}} with {{Cost|Cloud Vial}} to the Wind Shrine to get a chance to obtain a Windy Bee, as the other two are strictly to be used for crafting the [[Petal Belt]] and [[Petal Wand]].

There are {{Cost|Spirit Petal|3}} that are given every 10th [[Spirit Bear#Quests (30)|Spirit Bear quest]], first one being Sway Away, second being Tickle The Wind, and third being Spring Out Of The Mountain.

==Trivia==
*This is one of the two unique items that can only be obtained from completing a [[:Category:Bears|bear]]’s quests, the other being {{Cost|Translator}} from [[Science Bear]].
*If a player attempts to donate another {{Cost|Spirit Petal||4=1}} to the Wind Shrine after already donating one, Spirit Bear will shout "Only one, dear!" upon rejection.
**If the player tries to donate 2 or more {{Cost|Spirit Petal||4=}}, only one will be donated and the rest will be given back to the player.
