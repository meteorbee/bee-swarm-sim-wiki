# Slingshot

[[File:Slingshot2.png|thumb|The Slingshot.]]
The '''Slingshot''' is a [[:Category:Transport|transportation]] device available to the player once they have discovered 8 different [[Bees|bee]] types. It is located next to the [[Clover Field]] and usually quickly transports the player past the [[Brave Bee Gate]] and right in front of the [[Pro Shop]], it may undershoot and cause the player to land in front of [[Panda Bear]].

== Trivia ==
* If the player has 8 different bee types but doesn't have 10 bees in their [[hive]] yet, they will be always be teleported back to the spawn pad if they try to pass the Brave Bee Gate. This used to be a method to pass the Brave Bee Gate.
[[File:Slingpand.png|thumb|270x270px|A player being launched towards Panda Bear.]]
* The cause of the different destinations is described by [[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] as "noob scripting". To increase the chance of launching to the Pro Shop (the intended destination), jump onto the pad; to increase the chance of launching to Panda Bear (the "it's a feature, not a bug" destination), walk onto the pad instead.
* This is the only non-cannon transport device that launches the player.
* There is a smaller version used as a weapon in the [[Retro Swarm Challenge]].
