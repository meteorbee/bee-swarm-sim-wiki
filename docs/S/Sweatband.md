# Sweatband

{{Beequip}}

The {{Color|Sweatband}} is a [[Bond|level]] 5 [[Beequip]]. It has an equip limit of three. There is no color requirement.

{{BeequipStatsTable}}
==Ways to Obtain==
*As a very rare drop from harvesting the {{Cost|Tacky Planter|||0}}, {{Cost|Candy Planter|||0}}, and {{Cost|Petal Planter|||0}}. ({{Cost|Tacky Planter|||0}} and {{Cost|Petal Planter|||0}} have a higher chance to drop in [[Clover Field]])
*Buying it from [[Dapper Bear's Shop]].
*Completing Robo Bear's Winter 2024 Beesmas quest gives 1 Sweatband and other items.

*It is one of the first beequips added that is obtainable outside of Beesmas.
*This beequip was released on the [[Updates|2022/04/01 update]].
*This is currently one of the only non-event beequips that does not give an extra ability token on its stats.
