# Swirled Wax

{{Consumables
|image=Swirled Wax.png
|description=A spiraled hunk of beeswax. Used to redetermine the stats of a Bee's Beequip.
}}
{{Color|Swirled Wax}} is an [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] and a type of [[Waxes|wax]] that can be used on a [[Beequip]] to reroll its stats. Beequips with higher potential have a greater chance of having better stats when rerolled. Swirled Wax has [[Beequip#Stats, Waxes, Chances|Upgrade Value]] of 0.

When used on a Beequip, it will change its stats. If the Beequip has waxes used on it, it will alter the enhancements as well.

When a Swirled Wax is applied to a Beequip, a message pops up saying:

{{Notification||The Wax altered the Beequip's stats}}

Sometimes, the stats won't show any changes when you use a swirled wax; most of the time, you only need to rejoin to fix it.

=== Drops ===
*As a rare drop from a Epic Puffshroom. An uncommon drop from a Legendary Puffshroom, and a common drop from a Mythic Puffshroom.

*3 swirled wax are guaranteed to drop when [[Snowbear]] is defeated at level 13.

**Planting a {{Cost|Candy Planter|||0}} in the [[Cactus Field]] guarantees one every month.
**Planting a {{Cost|Blue Clay Planter|||0}} in the [[Spider Field]] guarantees one every month.

The cooldown time seems to be 7 days. However, Swirled Wax from Pine Tree Forest is guaranteed with a 7 day cooldown.
**Planting a {{Cost|Tacky Planter|||0}} in the [[Pumpkin Patch]] guarantees one every month.
**Planting a {{Cost|Pesticide Planter|||0}} in the [[Strawberry Field]] guarantees one every month.
**Planting a {{Cost|Heat-Treated Planter|||0}} in the [[Blue Flower Field]] guarantees one every month.
**Planting a {{Cost|Hydroponic Planter|||0}} in the [[Mushroom Field]] guarantees one every month.
**Planting a {{Cost|Petal Planter|||0}} in the [[Strawberry Field]] guarantees one every month.
**{{Cost|The Planter Of Plenty|||0}} has a high chance of dropping one Swirled Wax in any field.
**{{Cost|The Planter Of Plenty|||0}} may drop 5 Swirled Waxes with one every month.

==Trivia==
*This is the first wax to be added to the game. It was added in Beesmas 2020 while the rest were added in Beesmas 2021.
*This wax, {{Cost|Soft Wax|||0}} and {{Cost|Debug Wax|4=0}} are the only waxes guaranteed to change a Beequip's stats.
*Before the 12/26/21 update, this item had no favor when donated to the [[Wind Shrine]].
*Swirled Wax used to have a limit of 5, but the limit was removed in the [[Updates#2021.12.26|12/26/21 update]].
*Before the 12/26/21 update, Swirled Wax did not have a wick in its icon design.
*This is the only wax that was not included in the Waxy Bundle.
