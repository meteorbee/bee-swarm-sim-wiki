# Micro-Converter

{{Consumables
|image = Mconv.png
|description = Instantly converts all Pollen in your bag to Honey.
|cooldown = 5 seconds
|cap = 15
}}The {{Color|Micro-Converter}} is an [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] added in the [[Updates#2019.04.05|4/5/2019 update]]. When used, it instantly converts all [[pollen]] in the player's [[Bags|bag]] into [[honey]]. It acts as an [[Instant Converter]] without the need for [[ticket]]s and the journey to the hive, a significantly shorter cooldown, and is portable.

The amount of honey the player receives from a micro-converter is not affected by their [[System Page#Convert Rate|Convert Rate]], although it is affected by [[System Page#Honey Per Pollen|Honey Per Pollen]].

The player can have a maximum of 15 micro-converters in their inventory at a time. Obtaining more than 15 will not add any micro-converters to their inventory unless purchased from the [[Robux Shop]], obtained from [[Quests]], or adding certain [[stickers]] to your [[Sticker Stack]].

The following audio plays when a micro-converter is used: [[File:Mconv.ogg]]

== Ways To Obtain ==
* Rarely dropped when defeating [[mobs]], such as a [[Rhino Beetle|rhino beetle]], [[mantis]], [[werewolf]], [[Tunnel Bear]] and [[aphid]], or guaranteed drop from [[Coconut Crab]] and [[Mondo Chick]].
* Completing some of [[Black Bear#Quests to Star Jelly (15)|Black Bear's Star Jelly quests]] and [[Black Bear#Quests to Mythic Egg#Mythic Egg quests|Black Bear]]'s Mythic Egg quests (15 in total).
* Completing some of [[Spirit Bear#Quests (30)|Spirit Bear's quests]].
*Completing some of [[Bee Bear|Bee Bear's]] quests.
*Completing [[Dapper Bear|Dapper Bear's]] 4th 9th, and 13th quest.
* Completing 10, 30, 70, 155, 170, 215, and 270 [[Brown Bear]] quests will reward an additional 5 micro-converters, and completing 80 will reward an additional 10 micro-converters.
* A rare gift from [[Ability Tokens#Festive Gift|Festive Gifts]].
* As a rare drop from [[Ability Tokens#Festive Mark|Festive Mark]].
* As a reward for donating to the [[Wind Shrine]].
* Matching a pair of micro-converters in [[Memory Match]]es (available in increments of 1, 2, 6, or 5 depending on which Memory Match).
* Rarely through a [[Cub Buddy]] gift in increments of 1 or 5.
* A token behind [[Black Bear]] in between the [[Red HQ]] and the [[Windy Bee Gate]].
* As a drop from a [[Blue Clay Planter]].
** Planting it in the [[Spider Field]] makes it drop at least 10 Micro-Converters.
* As a reward from [[Robo Bear Challenge]].
* As a reward from [[Retro Swarm Challenge]]
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain [[stickers]] to the [[Sticker Stack]]:
** The Alert Icon rewards 3 micro-converters.
** The Simple Skyscraper rewards 3 micro-converters.
** The Lightning rewards 3 micro-converters.
** The Grey Diamond Logo rewards 3 micro-converters.
** The Magnet rewards 3 micro-converters.
** The Spark Staff rewards 3 micro-converters.
