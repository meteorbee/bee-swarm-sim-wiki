# Monthly Top Honeymakers

The '''Monthly Top Honeymakers''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game and is only viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the [[System Page]]. This leaderboard shows how much [[honey]] players have collected over the span of 1 month. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on the leaderboards for lengths of time (hackers/exploiters will be removed). The leaderboard resets on the 1st day of every month at 12:00 AM, CST.

== Trivia ==

* This is the only leaderboard in the game to reset every month, unlike the daily leaderboards which reset every day and the all-time leaderboards which doesn't reset.
