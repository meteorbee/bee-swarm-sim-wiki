The {{Color|Motivating Vial}} is a consumable [[Nectar Vial|nectar vial]] [[Items|item]] that was added in the [[Updates#2022.12.26|12/26/2022 update]]. Once used, it grants the player 4 hours of the [[Nectar|Motivating Nectar]] [[Buffs & Debuffs|buff]] instantly. It can also be used to craft other [[:Category:Accessories|accessories]].

== Ways to Obtain ==
* Using the [[Nectar Condenser]] to condense 12 hours of Motivating Nectar.
* Adding the Motivating Nectar Icon to the [[Sticker Stack]] grants 1 Motivating Vial.

=== Quests ===
* Completing [[Dapper Bear|Dapper Bear's]] 8th quest.
