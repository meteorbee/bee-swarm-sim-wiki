The {{Color|Marshmallow Bee}} is an [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] that was added during the [[Egg Hunt 2019]] event. It has a capacity limit of 25, which can only be exceeded by [[Codes|code]] and [[Quests|quest]] rewards. Collecting 3 Marshmallow Bees and handing them in to the [[Egg Hunt Info]] gave the player the [[Flight of the Bumble Egg]] and a [[Badges#Event Badges .5BCurrently Unobtainable.5D|badge]] during the Egg Hunt 2019 Event.

When used, it grants the following [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Items|buffs]] for 30 minutes:
*+50% [[System Page#White Pollen|White Pollen]].
*+50% [[System Page#Capacity Multiplier|Capacity]].
*+250% [[System Page#Convert Rate|Convert Rate]].

== Trivia ==
*This is the only item to use the word "eat" instead of "use" in the pop-up when double clicked in the inventory.
*This is one of the four [[:Category:Consumables|consumables]] that increase capacity, the others being the {{Cost|Purple Potion|4=0}}, {{Cost|Super Smoothie|4=0}} and {{Cost|Snowflake}}.
*There are sugar crystals on the Marshmallow Bee, something most real life marshmallows don't have. This may have taken influence from/be a reference to the brand [[Wikipedia:Peeps|Peeps]], as they are also animal-shaped marshmallows coated in sugar, and are popular during Easter.
*Before the [[Updates#2020.04.06|April 6th, 2020 update]], there was no capacity limit on how many Marshmallow Bees could be stored in the player's inventory. This was likely so because the player could only obtain a limited amount of Marshmallow Bees prior to this update.
*Marshmallow bees are the few consumable buffs that won't override a {{Cost|Super Smoothie|4=0}} buff. The others being {{Cost|Snowflake}}, {{Cost|Stinger}}, {{Cost|Jelly Beans}} and {{Cost|Glitter}}.
*Before the [[Updates#2022.12.26|December 26th, 2022 update]], [[Digital Bee]] and Marshmallow Bee were the only bees that the player couldn't have in their hive.
**Since Digital Bee has now been added in as an obtainable bee for the player's hive, this only applies to Marshmallow Bee now.
