# Festive Wreath

{{Beequip}}
{{Unobtainable}}
The {{Color|Festive Wreath}} is a [[Bond|level]] 10 [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]] [[Beequip]]. It has an equip limit of one. It has a red color requirement. It can only be equipped to [[Festive Bee]].

==Trivia== 
*The {{Cost|Festive Wreath|4=1}} and the {{Cost|Reindeer Antlers|4=1}} share many similarities: 
**The two are the only beequips to have models visible on the bee that they are equipped to.
**Before the [[Updates#2024.01.12|2024/01/12 update]]'s removal of permanent beequips, the two were the only permanent beequips in the game. 
***This was the second permanent beequip added to the game, preceded by the Reindeer Antlers.
***They were the only beequips that cannot be discarded.
***{{Cost|Caustic Wax}} were unable to be used on these two beequips. However, this is no longer the case.
*A model of a Festive Bee with the Festive Wreath can be found outside the map, next to a model of a [[Puppy Bee]] with Reindeer Antlers.
*This is the one of three beequips to give a unique ability.
**The other two are {{Cost|Snowglobe|4=1}} and {{Cost|Reindeer Antlers|4=1}}.
*The model of the Festive Wreath on Festive Bee is the [https://www.roblox.com/catalog/191097654/Deluxe-Christmas-Wreath Deluxe Christmas Wreath] Roblox accessory.
