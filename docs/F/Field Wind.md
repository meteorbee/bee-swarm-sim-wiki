# Field Wind

[[File:Screenshot 2022-03-26 120621.png|thumb|A field wind showing it's length, field boost and conversion.]]
A '''Field Wind''' is a stacking buff that grants a bonus to [[pollen]] and [[Instant Conversion|instant conversion]] to a specific [[Fields|field]]. Each stack of a field wind increases pollen gained from its field by a set amount depending on the field, and also gives 3% instant conversion to the field starting with a base of 25%. Field winds last for 30 minutes. Field winds are obtained by donating certain [[items]] to the [[Wind Shrine]] or using various [[codes]]. Field wind tokens from the Wind Shrine have a lifespan of 30 seconds.

With each application, the field winds stacks add up, and the duration resets to 30 minutes. If there's another code that gives a boost for that same field, this can be used to extend or reset the timer for another 30 minutes. The max stack for field winds is x15. Field winds can stack up to x15 if the item donated is rare enough and the player has enough [[Wind Shrine#Favor|favor]].

Field winds can also be replenished with codes, for example, if the Wind Shrine and a code both grant field winds, it restarts the 30 minutes field winds timer.

Unlike its [[Field Boost|field boost]] counterpart, fields boosted with winds do not give off [[sparkles]]. However, the icons used to identify field winds are the same as field boosts, except they have a white background.

Lower level fields tend to have more field pollen than other fields.

Specific items donated to the Wind Shrine can give specific winds. For example, donating Pineapples makes it more likely to give Pineapple Field Winds.
