==Trivia==
*At this time, the max possible amount of Festive Planters a player can own is 100.
*This is the only planter that can drop Magic Beans.
*This planter gives the best Nectar multiplier in the game (3x), overtaking the [[Ticket Planter]] and [[Sticker Planter]] that give a x2 nectar multiplier.
*This is one of the four planters that aren't reusable. Others being the [[Ticket Planter]], [[Sticker Planter]] and the [[Paper Planter]].
**Out of these four, the Festive Planter is the only one that is non-renewable.
