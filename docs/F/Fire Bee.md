'''Fire Bee''' is a [[:Category:Red|Red]] [[Bees/Epic|Epic bee]].

Fire Bee's favorite type of treat is {{Cost|Pineapple}}.

Fire Bee likes the [[Mushroom Field]], and [[Strawberry Field]]. It dislikes the [[Pine Tree Forest]].

== Stats ==
* Collects 10 [[Pollen]] in 4 seconds.
* Makes 80 [[Honey]] in 4 seconds.
* -20% [[Stats#Speed|Movespeed]], +25% [[Energy]], +3 [[Stats#Attack|Attack]].
* 🌟 [[Gifted Bee|Gifted Hive Bonus]]: x1.25 [[Flame#Flame Pollen|Flame Pollen]].

=== Abilities ===
* '''[[Ability Tokens#Bomb|[Red Bomb+]]]''' Collects 10 pollen from 29 surrounding red [[Flowers]] (+10% pollen per Level). Combo with other bombs to increase power.
* '''[[Passive Abilities#Gathering Flames|[Passive:]] [[Flame#Gathering Flames|<nowiki>Gathering Flames]</nowiki>]]''' 35% chance (50% if Gifted) to spawn a [[Flame]] when gathering. Flames last 4 seconds. Each second they deal 10 damage (+25% of the bee's [[Bee Attack|base attack]]) to nearby enemies and collect [5 Red / 3 White / 2 Blue] pollen (+10% per bee level) from 9 flowers. Standing near a flame grants Flame Heat, which increases [[System Page#Red Pollen|Red Pollen]] (between x1.2 and x2) and [[Bee Attack]] (between +5% to +25%).

==Trivia==
*The [[Fire Mask]], which can be purchased in the [[Red HQ]], has a special design, name, and stats dedicated to Fire Bee.
*Fire Bee can be summoned by [[Spicy Bee]] with its [[Ability Tokens|Inferno]] ability, making it the only bee summon-able by [[Ability Tokens]].
**This bee could also be summoned by using the [[Honeyday Candles]].
*Fire Bee is the red counterpart of [[Bubble Bee]].
*This bee was one of the bees to have a specific jelly in the [[Bee Bear's Catalog]]. The jelly was given as part of the Cozy Bundle.
