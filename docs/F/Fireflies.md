# Fireflies

[[File:Fireflies_StrawberryField.png|thumb|251px|Fireflies in a crescent in the [[Strawberry Field]].]]
[[File:FirefliesFlying.png|thumb|A depiction of what the firefly would look like as a bee.]]
'''Fireflies''' are passive [[mobs]] that only appear during [[Day/Night Cycle|Nighttime]]. Individually, they produce [[sparkles]] on the [[flowers]] that they land on. Fireflies band together to generate various [[items]] for the player.

As soon as Nighttime begins, a group of eight fireflies will spawn above the [[Star Hall]]. These fireflies will then fly to a random [[field]]s. Fireflies can fly to the [[Spider Field]], [[Bamboo Field]], [[Strawberry Field]], [[Pineapple Patch]], [[Cactus Field]], or the [[Rose Field]]. Once they reach the designated field, they will land randomly in a circle, facing towards the center. Fireflies also spawn [[Sparkles]] on the current flower they are on.

The player can chase a firefly from its position by walking close to it, forcing it to fly up. They will fly up if a player is within a 3-flower radius. If a firefly is nudged away, the flower the firefly sat on will generate sparkles. == Trivia == 
*The face of a firefly has the same face as the [[Gummy Bee]], but the color of the face is black and not cyan.
*[[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] has confirmed that the chance of obtaining a {{Cost|Star Treat|||0}} from fireflies is 1 in a million.
*Fireflies, [[Frog]]s, [[Chicks#Chick|Chicks]], [[Chicks#Hostage Chick|Hostage Chicks]], [[Chicks#Spotted Chick|Spotted Chicks]], and [[Bean Bugs]] are the only Passive Mobs in the game.  
**Fireflies were the first passive mob introduced to the game.
**Fireflies are also the only passive mobs to spawn exclusively at night.
*If a player uses a {{Cost|Night Bell|||0}} when a group of fireflies are leaving, another group will replace them. In an unknown update, the old group will come back instead of a new one.
*Despite the fact there are eight fireflies, the [[Sticker|Glowing Smile Sticker]] only shows seven "fireflies".
