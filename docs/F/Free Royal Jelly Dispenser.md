# Free Royal Jelly Dispenser

<blockquote>''Not to be confused with the [[Royal Jelly Dispenser]] which requires tickets or the [[Royal Jelly Shop]].''</blockquote>
[[File:Royaljelly.png|thumb|The Free Royal Jelly Dispenser.]]

The '''Free Royal Jelly Dispenser''' is a [[dispenser]] that is located in the [[Star Hall]]. It requires at least 1 [[Gifted Bee|gifted bee type]] to redeem {{Cost|Royal Jelly|||0}} and [[Ability Tokens#Haste|Haste+]]. The number of royal jellies the player receives is based on how many [[Gifted Bee|gifted bees]] they currently have in their [[hive]]. For example, if the player has 15 gifted bees, but two of them are the same gifted bee, they will still get {{Cost|Royal Jelly|15}}.

This dispenser has a cooldown of 22 hours. Currently, players can get a maximum of 50 royal jellies per use with 50 gifted bees.

== Trivia ==

* Unlike the Royal Jelly Dispenser which gives a stack of 10x Haste, the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser gives Haste+.
* Using this is one of the two only ways to get the Haste+ [[Buffs & Debuffs|Buff]], the other being certain [[codes]].
* Unlike the Royal Jelly Dispenser, which is limited to one Royal Jelly per use, the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser can give up to 50 Royal Jellies.
* Unlike how the [[Free Ant Pass Dispenser]] or the [[Free Robo Pass Dispenser]] are directly next to their {{Cost|Ticket|||0}} counterparts, the Free Royal Jelly Dispenser is not directly next to its ticket counterpart, the Royal Jelly Dispenser.
* This, the Free Robo Pass Dispenser, and the [[Glue Dispenser]] are the only dispensers with a 22-hour cooldown, the longest in the game.
