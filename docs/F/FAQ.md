# FAQ

As with practically anything, some questions come up over and over as more people discover the game.

==Game FAQs==

<h3 class="faq">How do I cut the vines by the [[Wealth Clock]]?</h3>

You need to have either the [[Clippers]], [[Scissors]], or [[Scythe]] equipped and then walk up to the pad in front of the vine and press "E" to cut the vines. You can then enter the area behind the vines with whatever tool you want.

<h3 class="faq">How do I get a [[Spirit Petal|spirit petal]]?</h3>

For every 10 [[Spirit Bear]] quests, Spirit Bear will give you a spirit petal. There are 30 quests in all, meaning you can get 3 spirit petals.

<h3 class="faq">How do I get a [[Windy Bee]]?</h3>

There are two ways. One way was to purchase it from the Robux Shop for 800 Robux before Beesmas 2019 began. The other way is to get a spirit petal from Spirit Bear's quests, donating it to the [[Wind Shrine]], and then donating [[Cloud Vial|cloud vials]] to (hopefully) obtain a Windy Bee (Note: Some items can increase the chance of you getting Windy Bee, and it will most likely take you a long time to get it).

You do not need to donate a spirit petal to start increasing your chance of getting Windy Bee, you increase the chance anytime you donate an item to the Wind Shrine. You just have to donate a spirit petal to ''be able''' '''''to have the chance to get Windy Bee.

<h3 class="faq">Who should I give my [[translator]] to?</h3>
It is commonly recommended to give your translator to either [[Gifted Riley Bee]] or [[Gifted Bucko Bee]]. [[Stick Bug]] should be last, because the [[:Category:Quest Bees|quest bees]] have good rewards and other players/[[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] can summon Stick Bug. Depending on whether you're a better red [[pollen]] [[:Category:Tools|collector]] or blue pollen collector, your first translator should go to the color you specialize in. Give it to Gifted Riley Bee if you are better in red, and Gifted Bucko Bee in blue. This is so you can start doing the easier quests for you and get rewards. If you're balanced in both red and blue pollen, give the translator to Gifted Riley Bee, as its quests may reward [[Stinger|stingers]], an [[Items|item]] that is very useful for acquiring [[Vicious Bee]] and the [[Demon Mask]].

Your second translator should go to the other bee that you ''didn't'' choose. For example, if you gave your first translator to Gifted Riley Bee, you would be recommended to give it to Gifted Bucko Bee. This is so you can get started on the other quests for more rewards.

As mentioned above, your last translator should go to Stick Bug. Stick Bug should be last, because it isn't as rewarding as the two bees. The [[Stick Bug Challenge]] periodically starts because of Onett, and other players can summon it as well if they gave a translator to Stick Bug, so giving the translator to Stick Bug will not make a big difference in terms of gameplay.

<h3 class="faq">How do I get a translator?</h3>
Finish [[Science Bear]]'s new quests.

<h3 class="faq">How do I start the Stick Bug Challenge?</h3>

First, get a translator, then go talk to the Stick Bug on top of the mushroom. (You'll need a [[parachute|Parachute]] or [[glider|Glider]], but if you don't have either of those, you're probably not at a high enough level to challenge Stick Bug.)

<h3 class="faq">Which [[Bees/Event|Event bee]] should I get first?</h3>

The general consensus is to get [[Tabby Bee]] first, because the sooner you start collecting [[Tabby Love]], the sooner your Tabby Bee will reach the maximum level. [[Photon Bee]] is usually agreed to be the next priority. After that, it becomes a matter of how many bees you have, what colors they are, and which quests are you on, so there is no one-size-fits-all guideline.

<h3 class="faq">Should I get the [[Scythe]] or the [[Bubble Wand]]?</h3>

This is a matter of opinion. In terms of absolute numbers based on the pollen collector alone, the Bubble Wand has a ''slight'' edge, but that's not the whole story. The right answer for you will depend on multiple factors, such as: Do you have more red bees or more blue bees? Do you prefer collecting pollen in a line or in a circle? Are you on a quest for a large quantity of red pollen? Some people get both tools, using one for red [[fields]] and the other for blue fields (and whichever one they happen to have equipped for white/neutral fields).

Note that as you get more bees, your pollen collector starts making less and less difference, because you'll be getting most of your pollen from stacked bombs or critical hits.

Special note about [[goo]]: most people find the Scythe works better for collecting goo, because the Bubble Wand's collecting circle is bigger than many goo puddles, so it misses most of the goo.

<h3 class="faq">How do I get that royal jelly/[[ticket]] that's [insert location here]?</h3>
Check out the pages on [[Royal Jelly|royal jelly]] and tickets.

<h3 class="faq">What is today's [[Codes|code]]?</h3>
There's no such thing as "today's code". Codes are issued when Onett deems fit, which is generally not a daily occurrence. ("Monthly" is closer to the truth.)

<h4 class="faq">OK, OK, but I heard that there's a new code. How do I find out what it is?</h4>
Check the [[Codes]] page. If you're not a registered user, make sure to click both of the refresh links in the white box at the top of the page — otherwise, you'll get yesterday's page. Heck, even if you ''are'' a registered user, the refresh links can't hurt.

<h3 class="faq">I know a new code! How do I add it to the Codes page?</h3>
First, make sure the code is not already listed. '''Use the refresh links''' at the top of the Codes page. Once you're absolutely positive that you're looking at the latest version of the Codes page, '''search the page''' (using your browser's Find function) for the code you want to add. Make sure your search is '''not''' case-sensitive.

If (and only if) you've done both of those steps and did not find the code, post a comment at the bottom of the page listing the code, where you found it, and what rewards it gives.

<h3 class="faq">How long do I have until Photon/[[Cobalt Bee|Cobalt]]/[[Crimson Bee|Crimson]]/etc. Bee disappears?</h3>
There are currently no plans to remove any of the event bees. "Event Bee" does not mean "temporary"; it just means you can't get this bee through ordinary eggs or royal jellies.

<h3 class="faq">Should I buy the Parachute? What purpose does it serve?</h3>
The Parachute is very useful for obtaining some of the royal jellies and tickets hidden around the map. (In fact, the parachute is outright necessary for some of the locations.)

<h3 class="faq">I used a royal jelly on an [[:Category:Epic|Epic]] bee, and it turned into a [[:Category:Rare|Rare]] bee. Shouldn't it have turned into a [[:Category:Legendary|Legendary]] bee?</h3>
No; the probabilities for royal jelly don't depend at all on what bee you're applying it to. Even if you use royal jelly on an Event bee, it will more than likely turn into a Rare bee.

<h3 class="faq">If I use a royal jelly on a [[Bond|level]] 7 bee, will it reset to level 0?</h3>
Royal jelly only transforms the ''type'' of the bee, not its level, so the new bee will still be level 7. It's also very important to remember that Gifted status is part of the type, so it '''''does''''' reset if you use a royal jelly.

<h3 class="faq">If I use a royal jelly on Tabby Bee, will my Tabby Love stack reset?</h3>
No, your Tabby Love will be saved, so it's safe to use royal jelly on Tabby Bee. (But see caveat about Gifted status above.)

<h3 class="faq">Whoops, I accidentally deleted my event bee. How do I undo it?</h3>
Event Bees come with a special royal jelly that will turn any bee into the event bee in question. Note that you can only have one of each type of event bee in your swarm at the same time, so the only use for the special royal jelly is to recover from precisely this situation, i.e. getting your event bee back after converting it.

<h3 class="faq">Whoops, I accidentally deleted my gifted bee. Help?</h3>
Sorry, there's no way to undo this.

<h3 class="faq">''Why is there a "''1st"'' flag on my event bee?''</h3>
It means that your bee is a 1<sup>st</sup> edition. [[Bear Bee]]s bought during the first month of the game's release, [[Gummy Bee]]s obtained from [[Gummy Bear|Gummy Bear's]] quests, and Tabby Bees bought when it was first put on sale in the [[Ticket Tent]] are all first editions.

<h3 class="faq">There's a player stuck in my hive! He never moves, and I can't read his name.</h3>
You're in hive #6, aren't you? That's the Onett statue from on top of the hives. (Onett is the game's creator.) Sometimes, he falls down and lands in odd places, like in the honeycomb of hive #6. You should be able to just play as if he weren't there - your bees will ignore him, and the hive will still be functional.

<h3 class="faq">Do the royal jellies or tickets hidden around the map ever respawn?</h3>
Nope, sorry, they're a one-time-only thing.

<h3 class="faq">There's no way I'm finishing this Traveling Bear's quests before he leaves. Will I be able to get the quest rewards afterwards?</h3>
Ah, yes, those last few quests can seem interminable; I feel your pain. Good news: you ''will'' be able to buy the quest rewards somewhere. (Bad news: it'll probably cost you.) For example, after [[Sun Bear]] left, the [[Belt Bag]] became available from the [[Pro Shop]] for 440,000 Honey, and the [[Mondo Belt Bag]] was put on sale in the [[Mountain Top Shop]] for 12 million Honey.

<h3 class="faq">Help, I'm missing a leg/arm/head! -or- Help, I turned into a strange half-human/half-bear monster!</h3>
You died or reset while you were in [[Ability Tokens#Bear_Morph|Bear Morph]] mode, didn't you? It can also happen if you leave the game while in bear shape. The fix is to wait until you're not a bear anymore (or until you're only half bear), and then leave the game and come back.

<h3 class="faq">Where do I report bugs?</h3>
First, check that your bug doesn't appear on this page or the [[Glitches|Known Glitches]] page. Next, do the Roblox equivalent of turning it off and back on again: leave the game and come back. (Important: '''don't''' reset your character! Actually close the game window and then open the game again.) Is the bug still there?

If it's a new bug which isn't fixed by leaving and coming back, but it's not preventing you from playing and doesn't involve actual money, then it's probably sufficient to report it on the [https://goo.gl/forms/eLoUcvc0BIqE71qC2 BSS Discord server's Bug Report form]. If the bug is preventing you from playing, and/or if you don't have Discord, send a message within Roblox to '''''[https://www.roblox.com/users/556387844/profile BeeSwarmBugReport]'''''. If the problem involves purchases/Robux, send a message within Roblox to '''''[https://www.roblox.com/users/556383852/profile BeeSwarmSupport]'''''. (If you're not sure, send messages to both accounts.) Depending on your Roblox privacy settings, you may have to follow the accounts before being able to send messages to them.

<h3 class="faq">How do I get an Admin/Moderator position on this wiki?</h3>
If you would like to become an admin/mod, you must first display qualities that befit those positions, be helpful and considerate to other users, and have little to no actions taken against your account. Then, staff may notice you and consider giving you those roles.

<h3 class="faq">The page is too long. Is there any faster way to do this?</h3>
Yes, there is. In fact, our staff already had a page that compiles all the wiki coding in one page. It's called [[FAQ/Wiki Code]]. This page also has information about our templates. With this, you have the knowledge not only for basic wikitext formatting, but also the templates.

<h3 class="faq">I saw a vandal. What should I do?</h3>
There are two ways to remove the vandal: manually remove the edit by yourself, or report to the staff. To remove an edit yourself, go to the history section of the page (add '?action=history'), then go to the previous edit, and undo the edit. To report it to the staff, post a message on the wall of any of the admins or moderators (see the list in the sidebar of the main page) with a link to the vandalized page and/or the profile page of the offending user.
