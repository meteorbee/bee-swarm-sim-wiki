'''Festive Bean''' is a special variant of the {{Cost|Magic Bean|4=1}} that always plants a [[Sprout|Festive Sprout]] in a [[Fields|field]]. When harvested, it grants {{Cost|Ticket|4=}}, [[Crafting|crafting materials]], and unique tokens that are commonly found in [[Ability Tokens#Festive Gift|festive gifts]] in abundant quantities. There is a 120 minute (2 hour) cooldown between planting Festive Sprouts. Since the end of Beesmas 2019, only the player who planted can receive the {{Cost|Festive Blessing}} and {{Cost|Beesmas Cheer}} buffs to avoid overuse. When a player plants a Festive Sprout, the following message appears, reading:
{{Notification|Red|"🌱 {Username} has planted a Festive Sprout 🌱}}

==Trivia== 

*At the end of Beesmas 2019, tokens from Festive Sprouts became unshareable. However, they are now shareable again, with the "Festive Blessing" and "Beesmas Cheer" buffs being the only private rewards.
**If summoned by [[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] (or one of his many aliases), a Festive Sprout's {{Cost|Festive Blessing}}, and {{Cost|Beesmas Cheer}} can be shared between all the players. They cannot be grabbed by abilities that collect personal tokens (e.g. [[Ability Tokens#Token Link|Token Link]] or [[Ability Tokens#Triangulate|Triangulate]]) as the tokens do not belong to any player.
*This bean is the only variant of {{Cost|Magic Bean|4=}}.
*This and the {{Cost|Marshmallow Bee|4=1}} are the only event items that can be donated to the [[Wind Shrine]].
*The {{Cost|Festive Bean|4=1}} is the only way for a Festive Sprout to be planted, with the exception of Onett planting them.
*The {{Cost|Festive Bean|4=1}} is the item with the longest cooldown in the game, being 2 hours.
*This and the {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|4=1}} are the only event items that are infinitely renewable outside of an event.
*This item cannot be used during the [[Robo Bear Challenge]].
* Festive Beans are the only way to get {{Cost|Snowflake}} outside of Beesmas, though they won't appear in the player's inventory even if they pick up the tokens.  
**They will be visible if {{Cost|Snowflake}} are already in the player's hotbar.
