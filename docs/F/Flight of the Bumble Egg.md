# Flight of the Bumble Egg

{{RemovedContent}}[[File:Flight_of_the_Bumble_Egg.octet-stream.png|thumb]]
The '''Flight of the Bumble Egg''' is a cosmetic hat that could have been obtained by completing the quest from [[Egg Hunt Info]], from April 17, 2019, through May 13, 2019, which was during the Roblox [[Egg Hunt 2019]] event. As it is a Roblox hat, players can wear it on their avatar for display.

The quest required 3 [[Marshmallow Bee|marshmallow bees]], which could be obtained by any NPC on the map (at the time) except [[Onett]] and the [[Honey Bee (NPC)|Honey Bee NPC]], and through the [[Codes|code]], “Marshmallow” (Valid). When the player had 3 marshmallow bees in their inventory, they could turn it in to the Egg Hunt Info for the Flight of the Bumble Egg. Receiving the Flight of the Bumble Egg also rewards an [[Badges#Event Badges .5BCurrently Unobtainable.5D|Event Badge]] exclusive for the Egg Hunt.
