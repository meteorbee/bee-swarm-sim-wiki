# Nectar Shower Vial

{{Unobtainable}}{{Consumables|image=Nectar Shower Vial.png|description=Grants 4 hours of all Nectar types to every player in the server!|cooldown=5 Seconds|cap=10}}
The {{Color|Nectar Shower Vial}} is a consumable [[Nectar Vial|nectar vial]] [[Items|item]] that was added in the [[Updates#2024.12.25|12/25/2024 update]]. Once used, it grants 4 hours of [[Nectar|all nectar]] [[Buffs & Debuffs|buffs]] to every player in the server.

When one is used, a message pops up saying "Username has popped open a Nectar Shower Vial!" for every player in the server. 

== Ways to Obtain ==
There are currently no ways to obtain {{Cost|Nectar Shower Vial}}.
===The methods below are no longer possible.===
* Purchasing the Royal Winter Wonder Haul for {{Cost|Robux|4,500}} grants {{Cost|Nectar Shower Vial|10}} and other items. (Expires April 30th, 2025)
* Completing [[Bee Bear]]'s "Cheering Up Gloomy Cub (4/5): Material Things?" rewards {{Cost|Nectar Shower Vial|3}}.
* Completing [[Bubble Bee Man]]'s "BBM's Naughty List" during Winter Beesmas 2024 rewards {{Cost|Nectar Shower Vial|7}}.
