# Nectar

[[File:Nectar jar.png|thumb|128x128px|The [[Nectar Pot]] located in the 30 Bee Zone.]][[File:The Nectar Condenser2.png|thumb|162x162px|The [[Nectar Condenser]] located in the 35 Bee zone]]'''Nectar''' is a set of [[Buffs & Debuffs|buffs]] that were added in the [[Updates#2021.12.26|12/26/21 update]]. It is granted from [[planter]]s, either by harvesting them, by [[bees]] sipping them from planters, or by using [[Dapper Bear]]'s [[Samovar]] during Beesmas. [[Nectar Vial]]s give 4 hours of a specific Nectar when used. Some [[quests]] require a certain amount and type of nectar to be collected. All sources of getting Nectar can contribute to those quests (except collecting Nectar from the Nectar Pot).  

There are five types of Nectar: Invigorating, Satisfying, Motivating, Comforting and Refreshing.

Nectar buffs can last for up to 24 hours and the boosts granted scale with the amount of time remaining. 12 hours of Nectar can be converted to a [[Nectar Vial|Vial]] using the [[Nectar Condenser]]. If the player has more than 12 hours of nectar, the remaining nectar will not be stored. Nectar can also be stored inside the [[Nectar Pot]] located in the 30 Bee Zone for 5 [[Ticket]]s. All of the Nectar types can be stored, but in order to deposit Nectar, any stored Nectar must be withdrawn first. If the Nectar is not stored, it will continue to deplete even if the player is not in the game. 

The table below shows the different Nectar types, the fields where a player can obtain them, and which planters give more Nectar than usual. All nectar types have a x0.75 bonus from the [[Paper Planter]], x1.5 from [[The Planter Of Plenty]], x2 from [[Ticket Planter]]s and [[Sticker Planter]]s, and x3 from [[Festive Planter]]s. 

The {{Cost|Nectar Tester|4=0}} is a special item that acts like nectar vials, but instead of providing a single nectar for 4 hours, it provides every type of nectar for 24 hours. It was meant to be only available on Test Realm, but was presented to Main Game during 2024 Summer Beesmas as a reward for completing [[Bubble Bee Man]]'s quest.

==Trivia==
*Every nectar icon (except Refreshing) has a letter on it that represents the first initial of the type of nectar. 
**Refreshing has no letter, although it is presumed that the icon has the gaps of the letter 'R'.
**Comforting has the letter 'C' resembling a crescent moon shape.
**Satisfying has the letter 'S' resembling the lightning bolt in its decal.
**Motivating has the letter 'M' in the lightbulb resembling the filament.
** Invigorating has the lower part of 'I' or a small capital 'I' at the bottom of the fire.
*[[Shy Bee]] has the passive [[Passive Abilities#Nectar Lover|Nectar Lover]], where it is twice as likely to sip nectar and gather twice as much nectar. It also contributes twice as much to the planter's growth.
*Nectar cannot be collected from the [[Ant Field]] although Onett announced that Ant Field contains Invigorating Nectar.
**Likewise, nectar cannot be collected from the [[Hub Field]], though a glitch allowed planters to be placed there.
***As such, harvesting a planter in this field grants Comforting Nectar, though abusing this glitch may result in having your account flagged.
