# Nectar Vial

[[File:AllNectarVials.png|thumb|Nectar Vials inside a player's inventory.]]
'''Nectar Vials''' are a type of inventory [[Items|item]] that can be used to increase a player's current [[Nectar]] by 4 hours when used. Using any Nectar Vial grants 4 hours of their respective nectar type. You can hold a maximum of 10 Nectar Vials of each type at a time. However, this limit can be bypassed by obtaining them through [[quests]] or [[Robux Shop|Robux Packs]].

== Ways to Obtain ==
* Using the [[Nectar Condenser]] to convert 12 hours of nectar into a Nectar Vial which stores 4 hours of nectar.
* Adding the Satisfying, Refreshing, Motivating, Invigorating, and Comforting Nectar Icon [[sticker]]s to the [[Sticker Stack]] will reward the player with their respective nectar vial.
* As a reward from certain quests.
** Completing several [[Dapper Bear]] quests reward the player with at least one of every nectar vial.

== Variants ==
There is a total of six variants of Nectar Vials. Five of which; immediately grant 4 hours of a type of Nectar buff to the player. The other grants 4 hours of every Nectar buff to all players in the server. The type of Nectar that the player is granted depends on the type of Nectar Vial that they choose to use, with the exception of the {{Cost|Nectar Shower Vial||4=1}}.

== Trivia ==
* While most of the Nectar Vials have a letter on them which represents the first letter of their name (C for Comforting, I for Invigorating, M for Motivating, S for Satisfying), the {{Cost|Refreshing Vial||4=1}} doesn't have an R on it to represent the first letter of its name. The {{Cost|Nectar Shower Vial||4=1}} has no icon at all, as it just seems to be a mix of all the previous Nectars.
* There is only a limited amount of Nectar Vials that can be obtained by the player without the use of the [[Nectar Condenser]].
** For {{Cost|Comforting Vial||4=}}, the total amount that can be obtained is 23.
** For {{Cost|Invigorating Vial||4=}} Vials, the total amount that can be obtained is 13.
** For {{Cost|Motivating Vial||4=}}, the total amount that can be obtained is 10.
** For {{Cost|Refreshing Vial||4=}}, the total amount that can be obtained is 11.
** For {{Cost|Satisfying Vial||4=}}, the total amount that can be obtained is 16.
*The {{Cost|Nectar Shower Vial||4=1}} is the only vial that is not obtainable from the [[Nectar Condenser]] and is only obtained from the Royal Winter Wonder Haul robux pack.
*Using Nectar Vials does count toward collecting nectar in [[quests]].
**However, they do not contribute to the [[Daily Top Nectar Collectors|Top Nectar Collector Leaderboards]].
