# Naughty List

{{RemovedContent}}
[[File:Naughty List.png|thumb|203x203px|Bubble Bee Man's Naughty List after completing his Beesmas quest.|alt=]]The '''Naughty List''' is a recurring [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]] machine that could be unlocked after completing [[Bubble Bee Man]]'s Beesmas quest. 

It is located on top of the [[Bear Gate|30 Bee Gate]], between [[Memory Match|Night Memory Match]] and Bubble Bee Man. When used, a voiceline of presumably Bubble Bee Man saying "''Naughty Naughty Naughty"''  will play server wide, and a lump of coal will fall onto each player in the server, which deals lethal damage if the player does not move out of the way. The falling coal is similar to [[Coconut Crab]]'s ''Coconut Attack''. If a player is killed by the falling coal, the player receives a [[Lump Of Coal|Lump of Coal Beequip]] and the [[Buffs & Debuffs#Buffs|Beesmas Repentance buff]] for 2 hours. Players can use the Naughty List every 8 hours. 

If a player attempts to use it without completing Bubble Bee Man's quest, the pop up will read: <span style="background-color:#a61e19;color:white">There aren't any names on the Naughty List. That can't be right...</span>

== Trivia ==

* This, Black Bears Honey Wreath (Honey Bee as well), and Onett's Lid Art are the only Beesmas decorations to have voices play upon activation.
** It is also the only Beesmas decoration to say words instead of voices.
* If a player stands on a mob while the coal was falling, it can kill the player and deal 100 damage to the [[Mobs|mob]].
* The names that the player could see that are stated are: Aardvark Joe, Apple BEe ''[sic]'', and Applebee's Cofounder 1.
* The Naughty List is tied for the Beesmas machine with the longest cooldown, being tied with [[Onett's Lid Art]] and the [[Gummy Siege]].
* This is the only machine to kill both players and hostile mobs.
* The Beesmas Repentance Buff does not stack. However, the duration of the buff does stack.
* After finishing Bubble Bee Man's quest, the dialog states that the player can use it every 22 hours. However, the player can use it every 8 hours<!-- What year? was it fixed? -->.
* If two or more players use the Naughty List at the same time, you can receive two or more Lumps of Coal while only dying once.
