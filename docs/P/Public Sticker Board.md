# Public Sticker Board

The '''Public Sticker Board''' is located in the [[Hive Hub]], and allows players to add or take [[sticker]]s from it. There is a 22-hour cooldown for taking a sticker from the board and a 10-minute cooldown for adding a sticker to the board. The [[Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine]] might occasionally require players to donate a sticker to the Public Sticker Board as well. This is a great way of obtaining stickers or removing spare stickers.

==Trivia==
*Play Button and Game Chat Icon stickers cannot typically be donated to the Public Sticker Board.
**This restriction does not apply to people who can give out these stickers.
***If a player takes one of these two stickers from the Public Sticker Board, then it will become tradable, even if you aren't a content creator or Discord staff.
*During the Beesmas of Summer 2024, the [[Gift Boxes#2024 (Summer)|Fluorescent Gift Box]] was located behind the public sticker board.
