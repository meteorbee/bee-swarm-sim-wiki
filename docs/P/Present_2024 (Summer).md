# Present/2024 (Summer)

{{RemovedContent}}
Presents returned once again in Beesmas 2024 (Summer). They still have their main function of adding [[Ornaments]] to the [[Beesmas Tree]] when given to NPCs.

=== Ways to obtain ===
*[[Bee Bear]]'s 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th, 12th, 14th, 16th, 17th, 18th, 19th and 20th quests.
*Buying one from [[Bee Bear's Catalog]].
*Given by Onett upon claiming hive with other rewards and honeyday buff.
*Received with other rewards from [[Wind Shrine#Galentine Shrine|Galentine Shrine]] when donating strawberries.
*Rank 25 [[Robo Party]] reward.
