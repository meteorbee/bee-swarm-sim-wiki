# Present/2022

{{RemovedContent}}
Presents returned once again in Beesmas 2022. They still have their main function of adding [[Ornaments]] to the [[Beesmas Tree]] when given to NPCs.

===Ways to obtain===
*[[Bee Bear]]'s 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th, 12th, 14th, 16th, 17th, 18th, 19th and 20th quests.
* Buying one from [[Bee Bear's Catalog]].
* Receiving one (from Onett) upon claiming a hive first time after New Year's Day 2023.
*Obtaining one from the [[Wind Shrine#Galentine Shrine|Galentine's Shrine]].
*A drop from the [[Robo Party]] (Rank 25).
