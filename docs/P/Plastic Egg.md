}}The {{Color|Plastic Egg}} was a blue-colored egg that was scattered around the map for [[Brown Bear#Egg Hunt Quest 2019|Brown Bear’s 2019 Egg Hunt Quest]]. The player had to find all three eggs to finish Brown Bear's Egg Hunt Quest, which awarded a {{Cost|Marshmallow Bee|4=0}}. They had no special abilities and were removed from the player's inventory when turning in the [[Quests|quest]]. The egg locations were replaced by {{Cost|Ticket|4=0}} tokens after the [[Egg Hunt 2019]] ended and the [[Updates#2019.05.13|5/13/2019 update]].

== Locations ==

* In the [[Mazes#Blue Maze|Blue Maze]], next to the [[Ticket Tent]].
** Turn left first, and follow the right wall after that to find the token.
** In the ramp leading up to the [[Basic Bee Gate]], opposite the [[Blue Flower Field]] and behind the [[Honey Bee (NPC)|Honey Bee's]] original location.
** Inside the [[Obstacle Courses#Lava Obby|Lava Parkour]]. There will be a little window outside of the lava parkour that shows the token.
*** When entering, immediately turn right, and climb up the rocks.

== Trivia ==
* This is the first and only [[egg]] that can be obtained as an inventory item and yet can't hatch into a [[Bees|bee]].
* In the Egg Hunt 2019 lobby, the portal to Bee Swarm Simulator said, "Find 5 Plastic Eggs", though the player only needed 3 to obtain a {{Cost|Marshmallow Bee|4=0}}.
** Compounding the error, this was also not the only quest needed to complete the Egg Hunt.
* This and the {{Cost|Broken Drive|4=0}} are the only [[items]] that only serve a purpose to complete a [[Quests|quest]].
