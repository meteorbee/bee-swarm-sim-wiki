The '''Pink Balloon''' is a [[:Category:Consumables|consumable]] [[Items#Misc. Items|item]] that was added in the [[Updates|12/26/2021 update]]. Once used, it creates a pink [[balloon]] over the [[Fields|field]] the player is in. The balloon floats over the field, storing [[pollen]] collected underneath it. The pollen collected below is doubled. It holds up to double the player's capacity. It floats over the field for 3 minutes before floating to the [[hive]] balloon.

Pink balloons have a cap of 25 in the player's [[:Category:Inventory|inventory]], and 1 per player in the field.

== Ways to Obtain ==
* As an exceptionally rare drop from the [[Mantis]] and the [[Ladybug]].
* As a reward from [[Mother Bear|Mother Bear's]] "Waiting With Sun Bear (2/6): And Mother Bear" quest.
* As a drop from the [[Retro Swarm Challenge]].
