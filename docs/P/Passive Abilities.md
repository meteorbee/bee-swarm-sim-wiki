# Passive Abilities

[[File:Displayed_Passives.png|thumb|250px|Passive Ability icons which can be seen above the hotbar, or bottom right next to the hotbar on mobile. These icons are for Haste Pulser, Coconut Haste Surge, Ignite, X-Flame, and Emergency Coconut Shield.]]
'''Passive Abilities,''' or simply '''Passives''' are effects found on some [[:Category:Accessories|accessories]], [[amulet]]s, or [[bees]]. These abilities are active all the time or only activate when specific actions are done, as an additional effect of those actions.

Passive abilities granted by an accessory or an amulet are displayed near the hotbar. Some passive abilities require the player to do the same action multiple times, as shown by the number displayed on the passive's icon, indicating how close it is to activating the ability. When the ability is activated, the number will reset to 0.

Some passive abilities have a cooldown. When in cooldown, the number of seconds left will be displayed on the passive's icon in pale red. No trigger will activate the ability, nor will the trigger counter be increased. These passive abilities also have an initial cooldown when the player equips items that have those passives to prevent bypassing the cooldown.
== Accessory Passives ==
=== Haste Pulser ===
[[File:HastePulser.png|thumb|left|100px|The icon for Haste Pulser.]]
'''Haste Pulser''' activates every 30th [[Ability Tokens#Haste|Haste token]] collected. 

When activated, it fires a blue pulse that hops to each blue bee, identical to that of [[Cobalt Bee]].

Haste Pulser is granted by equipping the [[Cobalt Guard]].

If the player has no blue bees, then nothing happens.

Focus Pulser is granted by equipping the [[Crimson Guard]].

=== Coin Scatter ===

=== Petal Storm ===
[[File:pstorm.png|thumb|left|100px|The icon for Petal Storm.]]
[[File:Petal storme.png|thumb|Petal Storm in action.|alt=]]
'''Petal Storm''' activates every 30th [[Ability Tokens#Boost|boost token]] collected, with a 30-second cooldown.

When activated, it fires 30 Petal Shurikens around the player in a spiral pattern. When the shuriken passes through a bee, the bee instantly converts 10,000 pollen, increased by 7% of its conversion amount.

Petal Storm is granted by equipping the [[Petal Belt]], or the [[Coconut Belt]] if the player has the Petal Belt.

=== Combo Coconuts ===
[[File:Combococonuts.png|left|thumb|104x104px|The icon for Combo Coconuts.]]
'''Combo Coconuts''' activates every 40th {{Cost|Coconut|||0}} the player drops onto a field (through items or abilities). 

The 40th {{Color|Coconut}} dropped becomes a Combo Coconut. When caught by any player, the Combo Coconut is kicked and either falls onto the summoner's field (if on the first drop, the coconut was caught within the blue circle) or onto another player's field. 

Every time it is kicked, it speeds up and gives the kicker stacks of [[Buffs_&_Debuffs#From_Passives|Coconut Combo]]. Many more stacks are granted if caught by other players in fields with different colors. 

The buff grants between 10% to 75% Unique Instant Conversion, between x1.1 to x1.5 Pollen, and between +1% to +50% Pollen, Red Pollen, Blue Pollen, White Pollen, Bee Attack, and Honey From Tokens. 

When the Coconut fails to be caught, it despawns and reapplies the buff to all players who caught it at least once.

Coconut Combo is granted by equipping the [[Coconut Belt]].
{{Clear}}

== [[Star Amulet]] Passives ==
=== Guiding Star ===
[[File:GuidingStarIcon.png|thumb|left|100px|The icon for Guiding Star.]]
[[File:Guiding star.png|thumb|A Guiding Star in the Sunflower Field.]]
'''Guiding Star''' activates every 250th [[Ability Tokens#Boost|Boost token]] collected, with a 5-minute cooldown. Additionally, it permanently grants 1.25x capacity. 

When activated, it summons a Guiding Star over 1 of the 5 fields the summoner has collected the least from for 10 minutes. 

The Guiding Star grants x2.5 [[System Page#Pollen|Pollen]], Convert Rate, and [[System Page#Capacity Multiplier|Capacity]] to the player who summoned it, and x1.25 Pollen, Convert Rate, and Capacity to other players in that field. While active, the star pollinates 15 (+1 per Gifted Bee Type) flowers every 15 seconds. It will disappear if the player who summoned it leaves the server.

Guiding Star is granted by having a Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet with the Guiding Star passive.

Guiding Star can also be summoned if a player used [[Onett's Lid Art]] during the [[Beesmas]] event.
{{Clear}}

=== Star Shower ===
[[File:StarShowerIcon.png|thumb|left|100px|The icon for Star Shower.]]
[[File:Starshowere.png|thumb|A Star Shower on a field.]]
'''Star Shower''' activates every 40th [[Ability Tokens#Boost|Boost]] or [[Ability Tokens#Mark|Mark]] token collected, with a 30-second cooldown. Additionally, it permanently grants 1.25x capacity. 

When activated, 10 shooting stars will fall onto the field the player is standing on, which collect and instantly convert 30 pollen (+50% per Gifted Bee Type) from 5 flowers. Catching a shooting star grants a stack of [[Buffs_&_Debuffs#From_Ability_Tokens|Inspire]] and instantly converts 1,000,000 pollen, added by 200% of the player's [[System Page#Convert Total|total conversion amount]].

Star Shower is granted by having a Diamond or Supreme Star Amulet with the Star Shower passive.

The passive is similar to the falling Beesmas Lights, which could be activated by completing [[Science Bear]]'s [[Beesmas Lights]] quest during the Beesmas events.

For every individual star, the following audio plays: <br>
[[File:Starshowerstar.ogg]]

=== Pop Star ===
[[File:PopStarIcon.png|thumb|left|100px|The icon for Pop Star.]]
[[File:Popstare.png|thumb|A Pop Star.|43x43px]]
'''Pop Star''' activates every 30th [[Ability Tokens#Boost|Boost token]] collected, with a 1-minute cooldown. Additionally, it permanently grants x1.25 Blue Field Capacity. 

When activated, it summons a Pop Star, which grants 10% [[System_Page#Instant_Blue_Conversion|Instant Blue Conversion]] and x2 [[System Page#Blue Pollen|Blue Pollen]] for 45 seconds. 

When the Pop Star is activated, it automatically applies 2 minutes of [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Passives|Bubble Bloat]]. While the Pop Star is active, every one of the player's bubbles popped increases the bonus Blue Pollen by 1% (up to x4.5 from x2) and grants 4 seconds of Bubble Bloat (6 seconds if the bubble is gold, 1 second if the bubble is not the player's).

Bubble Bloat increases [[System_Page#Blue_Field_Capacity|Blue Field Capacity]] and [[System Page#Convert_Rate_At_Hive|Convert Rate at Hive]] by up to x6 for up to 2.5 hours. 

When it ends, the Pop Star spawns several bubbles over the field that increase with the number of Bubbles popped.

Pop Star is granted by having a Supreme Star Amulet with the Pop Star passive.

When Pop Star is activated, the following audio plays: <br>
[[File:Popstar.ogg]]
{{Clear}}

=== Gummy Star ===
[[File:GummyStarIcon.png|thumb|left|100px|The icon for Gummy Star.]]
[[File:Gummystare.png|thumb|A Gummy Star.]]
'''Gummy Star''' is activated at a 2% chance when using Gumdrops, or at the 75th use after the cooldown ends, with a 1-minute cooldown. Additionally, it permanently grants x1.25 White Field Capacity. 

When activated, it summons a Gummy Star, which grants 10% Instant [[System Page#White Pollen|White]] and Goo Conversion for 45 seconds. Collecting goo during this time will make the star grow, granting bonus White Pollen and Goo (up to x2). 

After the 45 seconds are up, the Gummy Star will pop, scattering honey tokens and {{Cost|Gumdrops|}} tokens on the field the player was last in. The honey tokens are worth, in total, a percentage of the goo collected during the Gummy Star's duration, equal to 1.5% per gifted colorless bee type on the player's hive. The number of {{Cost|Gumdrops|}} tokens increases the amount of goo collected.

Gummy Star is granted by having a Supreme Star Amulet with the Gummy Star passive.

When Gummy Star is activated, the following audio plays: <br>
[[File:Gummystar.ogg]]

=== Gathering Bubbles ===

Bubbles spawned by this passive ability can be generated by [[Bubble Bee]], [[Tadpole Bee]], and [[frog]]s. There is a 35% chance (50% if gifted) for Bubble Bees to spawn a bubble while gathering, and a 55% (75% if gifted) chance for Tadpole Bees to spawn a bubble while gathering.

When a bubble is popped, the following audio plays: <br>
[[File:Bubblepop.ogg]]

=== Gathering Flames ===
{{Main|Flame}}
[[File:NewFlame.png|thumb|125px|A flame in a field.]]
[[File:Passive-gathering-flames.png|thumb|The old texture of a flame.]]
'''[[Flame]]s''' have a percentage chance to be spawned by certain bees when gathering.

Flames spawned by this passive ability can be generated by the [[Fire Bee]], [[Demon Bee]], [[Spicy Bee|Spicy Bee's]] [[Ability Tokens#Inferno|Inferno]], and bees with the [[Electric Candle]] Beequip. There is a 35% chance (50% if gifted) for Fire Bees to spawn a flame while gathering, and a 55% (75% if gifted) chance for Demon Bees to spawn a flame while gathering.
{{Clear}}

=== Steam Engine ===
'''Steam Engine''' is a passive ability exclusive to Spicy Bee. It boosts any Spicy Bee's [[Stats#Speed|speed]] by up to 100% with [[Buffs_&_Debuffs#From_Passives|Flame Heat]]. While Steam Engine is active, Spicy Bee will increase the number of steam particles from its exhaust pipes proportionately with the amount of flame heat.
{{Clear}}

=== Fuzzy Coat ===
'''Fuzzy Coat''' is a passive ability exclusive to [[Fuzzy Bee]]. It has a chance to [[Pollination|pollinate]] up to 5 surrounding [[flowers]] (9 if gifted) when gathering. Lower-tier flowers have a higher chance of being pollinated than higher-tier flowers.
{{Clear}}

=== Nectar Lover ===
'''Nectar Lover''' is a passive ability exclusive to [[Shy Bee]]. It makes Shy Bee twice as likely to sip from a [[planter]] than any other bee. When sipping, it also collects twice as much [[nectar]] and contributes twice as much to the growth of the planter.
{{Clear}}

=== Shimmering Honey ===
[[File:90935ED9-7B1D-47E4-94EE-B714907A58E7.jpg|thumb|Diamond Bee's Conversion Link color when Shimmering Honey is activated.|alt=|120px]]
'''Shimmering Honey''' is a passive ability exclusive to [[Diamond Bee]]. Whenever this bee converts at the hive, it grants a 25% bonus honey (+2.5% per level). This bonus is doubled if the bee is Gifted.
{{Clear}}

=== Balloon Enthusiast ===
'''Balloon Enthusiast''' is a passive ability exclusive to [[Buoyant Bee]]. When converting from Balloons, this bee gains x3 its normal [[System_Page#Convert_Amount|convert amount]]. Additionally, this bee's base attack scales up to x3 with [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Ability Tokens|Balloon Blessing]].
{{Clear}}

=== Sniper ===
'''Sniper''' is a passive ability exclusive to [[Precise Bee]]. In battle, this bee uses slow, long-range, more accurate shots and deals x2 damage. The accuracy is calculated as if the bee were 1 level higher (2 if gifted), and it can never be below 5%. If the shot is a [[Critical Hits|Critical Hit]], the reload time after shots is cut in half. If it's a [[Critical Hits#Super-Crit|Super-Crit]], it's reduced by 75%.
{{Clear}}

=== Drive Expansion ===
'''Drive Expansion''' is a passive ability exclusive to [[Digital Bee]]. Using a [[Drives|Drive]] while this bee is active in [[Robo Bear Challenge|Robo Bear's Challenge]] corrupts the field you're standing in by an amount proportional to the number of flowers that match the Drive's color (Glitched Drives corrupt all fields equally). Additionally, it permanently enhances this bee, increasing the Corruption of its abilities and granting it the following stats:
<br>- Red Drive: +0.03 [[Stats#Attack|Attack]]
<br>- Blue Drive: +2 [[Stats#Production Amount|Convert Amount]]
<br>- White Drive: +0.25 [[Stats#Gather Amount|Gather Amount]]
<br>- Glitched Drive: +0.05% [[System Page#Bee Ability Rate|Ability Rate]]

Every {{Cost|Glitched Drive|10}} Enhancements grants a Hive Bonus of 0.02% Ability Duplication Chance.

Digital Bee can be enhanced with up to 500 of each type of Drive. You can view the number of enhancements by clicking on Digital Bee's [[Hive Slot|hive slot]].

Upon maxing out with all 2000 Drives, Digital Bee gains +10 [[Stats#Speed|Movespeed]].
{{Clear}}
