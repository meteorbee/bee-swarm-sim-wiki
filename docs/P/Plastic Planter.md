The {{Color|Plastic Planter}} is a [[planter]] added in [[Updates#2021.12.26|12/26/21 update]]. It takes 2 in-game hours to fully grow naturally and usually drops around 12 tokens. The planter can be bought from [[Dapper Bear's Shop]]. Harvesting a fully grown Plastic Planter has a 1/10 (10%) chance to spawn a [[Puffshroom]] and will return the Planter back into the player's inventory.
