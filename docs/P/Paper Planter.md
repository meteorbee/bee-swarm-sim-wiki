The {{Color|Paper Planter}} is a single-use [[planter]] added in the 12/26/2021 update. It can be bought in the [[Pro Shop]] for {{Cost|Honey|25,000}} and {{Cost|Sunflower Seed|1}}.

{| class="article-table"
!Crafting Ingredients

|{{Cost|Honey|25,000}}<br>{{Cost|Sunflower Seed|1}}
|}

When used, it plants a Paper Planter in the [[Fields|field]] you planted it in. Different fields have different [[Nectar]] types. This planter takes 1 hour of playtime to fully grow. Harvesting a fully grown Paper Planter has a 1/30 chance to spawn a [[Puffshroom]]. It drops 9 items.

A player can only have {{Cost|Paper Planter|1}} active. Attempting to plant a Paper Planter while there is already one active will prompt a message that says, "Paper Planter is already in the [The field it is in]".

== Trivia == 
* This is the cheapest and fastest-growing planter in the game.
* It is one of the many planters to not be sold in [[Dapper Bear's Shop]], the others being the [[Red Clay Planter]], [[Blue Clay Planter]], [[Festive Planter]], [[Ticket Planter]], [[Sticker Planter]], [[Petal Planter]], [[Hydroponic Planter]] and [[Heat-Treated Planter]].
* This is the cheapest item in the [[Pro Shop]] in terms of honey, only costing 25,000 honey.
* This is the only item in the Pro Shop that is cheaper than an item in the [[Noob Shop]], being the [[Helmet]].
* This, the Ticket Planter and Sticker Planter the are the only one-time use planters that are renewable.
* This is the only planter in the game that stores more pollen than the honey it costs.
* It is one of the four planters that are one-time use. The remaining three are the [[Sticker Planter]], [[Ticket Planter]] and the [[Festive Planter]].
** It is also the only one-time use planter that can be bought from a store.
*This is also the only planter that ''reduces'' nectar gained, multiplying all nectar by 0.75x. All other planters either boost nectar gain (although some of them only boost certain types) or do not change it at all.
