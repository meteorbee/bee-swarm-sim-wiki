# Present/2021

{{RemovedContent}}
Presents returned in Beesmas 2021. They function the same as the years before that they have appeared in, but can now be given to the new NPC, [[Dapper Bear]]. Presents must be given to obtain [[Ornaments]] from NPCs, and they will be needed to be delivered to do quests from [[Bee Bear]].

===Ways to obtain===  
*Obtained by completing Quests from [[Bee Bear]].
*Buying one from [[Bee Bear’s Catalog]].
* Receiving one (from Onett) upon claiming a hive first time after New Year's Day 2022.
*Obtaining one from the Galentine's Shrine.
