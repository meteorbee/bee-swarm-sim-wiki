# Poinsettia

{{Unobtainable}}
{{Beequip}}
A {{Color|Poinsettia}} is a [[Bond|level]] 6 [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]] [[Beequip]]. It has an equip limit of two. There is no color requirement. They can only be equipped to bees that know the [[Ability Tokens#Boost|Boost]] ability.

==Trivia==
* After Beesmas 2022, this Beequip was still available to be obtained in Ant Challenge. 
** This makes the Poinsettia the second beesmas beequip that still can be obtained after Beesmas, with the first being [[Icicles]]. 
* This Beequip, along with the {{Cost|Lei|||0}} and the {{Cost|Rose Headband|||0}} are the only Beequips to have Gather Pollination Chance buff and makes it the only way for bees to pollinate flowers other than the [[Fuzzy Bee]] abilities.
