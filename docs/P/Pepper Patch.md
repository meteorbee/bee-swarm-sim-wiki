The '''Pepper Patch''' is a [[field]] that was added in the [[Updates#2019.9.28|9/28/2019 update]], located past the [[Windy Bee Gate]], to the right of the [[Memory Match#Extreme Memory Match|Extreme Memory Match]] and to the left of the [[Wind Shrine]]. There are no [[mobs]] that protect this field. 

This is a 21x27 field, containing 567 [[flowers]]. The flowers are red and white, and the only natural size is triple.

This field can spawn [[strawberry]], [[bitterberry]], [[Royal Jelly|royal jelly]], [[Red Extract|red extract]], [[ticket]] and [[Magic Bean|magic bean]] tokens when gathering [[pollen]]. The only [[Bees|bee]] that likes the Pepper Patch is [[Spicy Bee]]. Bees that dislike the Pepper Patch are [[Looker Bee]], [[Frosty Bee]], [[Baby Bee]] and [[Fuzzy Bee]].

This field grants Invigorating [[Nectar]] when a [[planter]] is planted here.

A level 9-12 [[Rogue Vicious Bee]] has a chance to spawn here.

== Obtaining Boosts ==
* Using the [[Red Field Booster]] may give a Pepper [[Field Boost|Patch Boost]] (only if the player has 35+ bees). When it does, it will give +100% Pepper Patch pollen for 15 minutes.
* Using [[glitter]] will add 100% Pepper Patch pollen onto the current boost for 15 minutes if it isn't already maxed out.
[[File:Pepper Patch Icon.png|thumb|75px|The Pepper Patch boost icon.]]
* Using [[Field Dice|field dice]], [[Smooth Dice|smooth dice]], or [[Loaded Dice|loaded dice]] may add +100% - +300% Pepper Patch pollen for 15 minutes if the current boost isn't already maxed out.
* Pepper Patch [[Field Wind|Winds]] can be obtained through the [[Wind Shrine]] or [[codes]]. They have a base of +20% instant Pepper Patch conversion and give +5% instant Pepper Patch conversion and +10% Pepper Patch pollen for each stack. Pepper Patch Winds can stack up to a total of 15 times.

=== The methods below are no longer possible. ===
* Redeeming certain expired codes:
** Sriracha (Gave Pepper Patch Boost x2 and Pepper Patch Capacity x2 + other stuff).
** 10mMembers (Gives Pepper Patch Code Buff + other stuff).
** DemiDecade (Gives Pepper Patch Boost x4 + Pepper Patch Winds x15 + other stuff).
** Gumaden10T (Gave Pepper Patch Boost x1 and Pepper Patch Winds x3 + other stuff).
** RedMarket (Gave Pepper Patch Boost x1, Pepper Patch Market Boost, and Pepper Patch Capacity x1).
**FrozenBugReboot (Gave Pepper Patch Boost x4 and Pepper Patch Code Buff + other stuff).
* Purchasing a Pepper Patch Market Boost from the [[Boost Market]] or certain codes gave x1.25 Pepper Patch Pollen along with 1.5x [[Field Capacity|Pepper Patch Capacity]], x1.25 [[System Page#Pollen From Flames|Flame Pollen]], and x1.25 [[System Page#Hive Convert Rate|Convert Rate At Hive]].

== Trivia ==
*This, the Coconut Field, the [[Stump Field]], the [[Ant Field]] and the [[Hub Field]] are currently the only fields released in updates.
*This, the [[Mountain Top Field]], Stump Field, and Coconut Field are the only fields to solely have natural triple flowers.
*The Pepper Patch is the only field past a bee gate with nothing guarding it.
* This and the Coconut Field are the only fields where [[sprout]]s cannot appear naturally or be planted with the [[Special Sprout Summoner]].
*The [[Badges#Pepper Badge|Pepper Badge]], [[Badges#Coconut Badge|Coconut Badge]], [[Badges#Mountain Top Badges|Mountain Top Badge]] and the [[Badges#Hive Hub Badges|Hive Hub Badge]] require more pollen collected than the other field badges.
*The Pepper Patch used to cover the entire platform it is placed on. Now, the field has shrunk a little and the area was amplified. As a result, it does not cover the edges of the platform anymore.
**The peppers used as decoration were also reorganized.
*This is the only red field that gives [[Nectar|Invigorating Nectar]].
*There used to be a {{Cost|Marshmallow Bee|4=1}} token next to this field.
