{{Quote|A small bag.}}The '''Pouch''' is the starter [[:Category:Bags|bag]] that new Players automatically receive at the beginning of the game, along with the [[scooper]], the starter [[:Category:Tools|tool]]. You can equip it in the [[Noob Shop]].

It doesn't have a price as it is the first bag given to the player for free.

== Stats ==
* +200 Capacity.

== Trivia ==
* In early versions of the game, the pouch stored 150 pollen.
** It was later buffed to 250 then later nerfed to 200.
* This and the [[jar]] are the only bags that cost less {{Cost|Honey|}} than their base capacity.
* This, the [[scooper]], and a {{Cost|Basic Egg|4=0}} are the only items that the player starts off with when joining the game for the very first time.
