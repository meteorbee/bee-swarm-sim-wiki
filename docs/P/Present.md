A {{Color|Present}} is a Beesmas-exclusive [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] that appears in every [[Beesmas]] event since 2020 following its debut in 2018 and hiatus in 2019. Presents can be given to most NPCs in the game in exchange for various prizes from the NPC. Each NPC can only receive one gift.

To give a present, the player needs to talk to an NPC of their choice. When interacted with, special dialogue will appear, allowing the player to give the present to the chosen NPC. If the player does not have a present in their inventory or have already recently given said NPC a present, normal dialogue will display. Some NPCs, such as [[Gummy Bear]], [[Bubble Bee Man]] and [[Onett]], have other requirements for the player before it allows itself to receive a gift. Gummy bear requires the player to complete his Beesmas quest, while Onett requires the player to have completed Star Journey One to receive a present. Bubble Bee Man requires the player to have given a present to all other NPCs before allowing itself to receive a present.

2024 (Winter)=
Presents returned once again in Beesmas 2024 (Winter). They still have their main function of adding [[Ornaments]] to the [[Beesmas Tree]] when given to NPCs, but can now be given to [[Sun Bear]].

=== Ways to obtain ===
*[[Bee Bear]]'s 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th, 12th, 14th, 16th, 18th, and 20th quests.
*Buying one from [[Bee Bear's Catalog]].
*Received with other rewards from [[Wind Shrine#Galentine Shrine|Galentine Shrine]] when donating strawberries.
*Rank 25 [[Robo Party]] reward.
*Recieved from [[Onett]] for free during Honeyday.
*Using the code "LatePresents" (Gave 2 Presents, exclusive to this Beesmas only).
*Using the code "LatePresent" (Gave 1 Present, exclusive to this Beesmas only).

==Trivia == 
*During Beesmas 2021, players who had given a present to [[Honey Bee (NPC)|Honey Bee]] before 01/06/22 would be refunded with their present due to a bug with the Bag of Honey Ornament's boost.
*Stick Bug, Gifted Riley Bee, and Gifted Bucko Bee require a {{Cost|Translator|||0}} before giving them a present.
*At some point, Onett could not be given a present immediately as he required the completion of the Star Journey questline to claim the reward. This is no longer the case, at least during the 2024 winter event.
**Gummy Bear was the only other NPC who required the player to complete his quest before giving him a present.
*On Christmas, Onett gave players a free present along with other prizes. The present was able to be obtained any day until the x2 event ended. To receive the gift, the player had to claim a hive during the x2 event.
*There were 11 presents in Beesmas 2018: 6 from Bee Bear, 3 from locations, 1 from Onett from the Beesmas x2 event, and 1 from the code "ByeBeeBear" (Expired). However, only 10 presents could have been used.
*Stick Bug was the only NPC who didn't give any field boosts when given a present.
