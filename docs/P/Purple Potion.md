# Purple Potion

{{Consumables
|image = Purplepotion.png
|description = Grants x1.25 Capacity and the improved boosts of Red and Blue Extracts and Glue for 15 minutes.
|cooldown = 1 second
}}
A {{Color|Purple Potion}} is a [[:Category:Crafted|craftable]] [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] added in the [[Updates#2020.04.06|4/6/2020 update]]. When used, it grants the following for 15 minutes:
*x1.25 [[System Page#Capacity Multiplier|Capacity]].
*x1.25 [[System Page#Convert Rate At Hive|Convert Rate At Hive]].
*x1.5 [[System Page#Red Pollen|Red Pollen]].
*x1.5 [[System Page#Blue Pollen|Blue Pollen]].
* x1.3 [[System Page#Bee Gather Pollen|Bee Gather Pollen]].
* x1.3 [[System Page#Pollen From Tools|Pollen From Tools]].

A Purple Potion buff cannot exist simultaneously along with {{Cost|Red Extract|||0}}, {{Cost|Blue Extract|||0}}, or a {{Cost|Glue|||0}} buff. If any of these buffs are active when a purple potion is used, they will be overridden. Using a {{Cost|Super Smoothie|||0}} will override the buff, due to the Purple Potion being included in its craft.

==Trivia ==

* It is one of four [[:Category:Consumables|consumables]] that can boost capacity, with the first being the  {{Cost|Marshmallow Bee|4=1}}, the third being the {{Cost|Super Smoothie|4=1}} and the fourth being the {{Cost|Snowflake|4=1}}
*Prior to the purple potion being officially added to the game, there was a [[Glitches|glitch]] that caused it to be obtainable from King Beetle and Tunnel Bear early. The item could not be seen in the inventory, but could be donated to the Wind Shrine, where it had the appearance of a [[Egg#Basic Egg|basic egg]] and had no donation value.
*Donating one purple potion to the wind shrine can give winds ranging from 5x-9x for 4-6 red/blue fields.
