# Pineapple

{{Consumables
|image=Pineapple.png
|description=Increases your bond with a bee by 25.

[[File:Pineapple Token.png|thumb|A {{Cost|Pineapple||4=1}} token.|168x168px]]A {{Color|Pineapple}} is a type of [[Treats|treat]] that can be fed to a [[Bees|bee]] to raise its bond by 25. However, when fed to a [[Hasty Bee|Hasty]], [[Brave Bee|Brave]], [[Honey Bee]], [[Exhausted Bee|Exhausted]], [[Shocked Bee|Shocked]], [[Fire Bee|Fire]], [[Demon Bee|Demon]], [[Lion Bee|Lion]], or [[Fuzzy Bee|Fuzzy]] Bee, the amount of [[bond]] they gain is doubled. These bees also have a small chance of becoming gifted when fed {{Cost|Pineapple||4=}}. [[Bees/Mythic|Mythic bees]] have a 1/24,000 chance to be gifted; [[Bees/Legendary|Legendary Bees]], a 1/12,000 chance; [[Bees/Epic|Epic bees]], a 1/10,000 chance; and [[Bees/Rare|Rare bees]], a 1/8,000 chance.

== Ways to Obtain ==
* {{Cost|Pineapple|5|4=}} can be found behind the pineapple near the [[Treat Dispenser]].
* {{Cost|Pineapple|1|4=0}} is given to the player every time they use the Treat Dispenser.
* As a reward from the [[Ant Challenge]].
* As a reward from [[Memory Match]]es (Every type except [[Memory Match#Night Memory Match|Night Memory Match]] and the [[Memory Match#Winter Memory Match|Winter Memory Match]]).
* As a stack reward for adding certain [[Stickers]] to the [[Sticker Stack]]:
** {{Cost|Shocked Hive Slot Sticker|||0}} rewards {{Cost|Pineapple|50|4=}}.
** {{Cost|Rhubarb Sticker|||0}} rewards {{Cost|Pineapple|50|4=}}.

===Drops===
*Farming in the [[Pineapple Patch]].
**Harvesting [[leaves]] in this field may also yield {{Cost|Pineapple||4=}}.
*As a drop from [[mantis]]es, [[scorpion]]s, [[spider]]s, [[Werewolf|werewolves]], [[Commando Chick]], [[Stick Nymph]]s, [[Stick Bug]], [[King Beetle]], and [[Tunnel Bear]].
*As a drop from a [[Ability Tokens#Festive Gift|Festive Gift]] or [[Ability Tokens#Festive Mark|Festive Mark]].
*As a gift from a [[Cub Buddy|cub buddy]].
*As a reward for completely growing any kind of [[sprout]] except for Moon, Gummy, and Debug sprouts.
**Planting a sprout in the Pineapple Patch yields 7x more {{Cost|Pineapple||4=}} than other fields.
*Harvesting [[sparkles]] (Higher chances in the [[Pineapple Patch]]).
*As a drop from [[Puffshroom]]s.
*As a drop from [[Planter|planters]], which include:

* Digging into the [[Beesmas Feast]] sometimes grants {{Cost|Pineapple||4=}}.
