# Present/2018

{{RemovedContent}}

In Beesmas 2018, presents were awarded by [[Bee Bear]], [[Onett]], and the "ByeBeeBear" [[Codes|Code]] (expired). After the [[Updates#2019.04.05|4/5/2019 update]], players could not obtain or use presents. The player could not give a present to Bee Bear, [[Bubble Bee Man]], or [[Gummy Bear]].

===Token Locations===
*Behind the presents that were by the Bee Bear during the [[Updates#2018.12.25|Beesmas 2018 update]].
* On the top of the honeycomb in the Ace Shop in the [[Badge Bearer's Guild]].
*Inside the [[Bear Gate]] with the [[Obstacle Courses#30 Bee Zone Obby|Moon Parkour]] on the other side of [[Bubble Bee Man]].
