# Ornaments

{{RemovedContent}}
'''Ornaments''' are limited-time [[items]] that are obtainable only during [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]] event. Ornaments can be obtained from all non limited-time [[Quest Givers|quest givers]] upon player giving them a [[Present]]. When the player receives an ornament, it gets placed on the [[Beesmas Tree]]. The ornaments will grant additional buffs, always with one field capacity buff, with the exception of Gifted Riley Bee, Gifted Bucko Bee, and Stick Bug, who gives two. Honey Bee just gives extra capacity, no field capacity. Every NPC's ornament will also give +25% Convert Rate, except Gummy Bear, Robo Bear and Bubble Bee Man, who all give none.

|2019|{{=}}{{=}} Beesmas 2019 {{=}}{{=}}
{{RemovedContent}}In 2019, ornaments could be obtained from most quest givers by completing their special ornament quest. Every ornament would give the player extra capacity, a [[pollen]] boost from one or two specific [[fields]], and a bonus stat [[Buffs & Debuffs{{!}}buff]]. Unlocking ornaments were required for completing [[Bee Bear{{!}}Bee Bear's]] quests during his second visit and opening up [[Ornament Presents{{!}}ornament presents]].
