# Onett's Lid Art

{{RemovedContent}}
[[File:Onett's Lid Art.png|alt=|thumb|244x244px|Onett's Lid Art after completing Onett's Beesmas quest.]]
'''Onett's Lid Art''', or '''Yard Art''' is a beesmas decoration which is unlocked after completing [[Onett|Onett's]] Yard Art [[Quests|quest]].

The art is located past the [[Bear Gate]] next to Onett. It can be used every 8 hours. When activated, the player receives 3 temporary bees, an assortment of loot, and a [[Guiding Star]] is activated.

If a player attempts to use the Lid Art before completing Onett's quest, it displays the message: <span style="background-color:#a61e19;color:white">This art is missing something... It's not very impactful.</span>
==Appearance==
The Lid Art consists of (left to right) [[Spirit Bear]] on [[King Beetle]], [[Black Bear]], [[Mother Bear]], [[Bumble Bee]] and [[Stick Bug]], and a [[Basic Bee]] fence gate.

The names confirmed by Onett so far are: Queen Spirit Bear (Spirit Bear), King Stick Bug (Stick Bug), and King Bumble Bee (Bumble Bee).[[File:LidArtIncomplete.png.png|thumb|Onett's Lid Art before the completion of Onett's Beesmas 2020 quest and beyond.]]
After completing Onett's Yard Art Quest, [[Baby Bee]], wearing a Santa hat, appears in between Black Bear and Mother Bear in the cradle.

==Trivia==
*Onett's Lid Art is the only Beesmas decoration to activate a passive ability, that being the Guiding Star.
*Originally, the message that would appear if the player tried to use Onett's Lid Art before they completed [[Onett]]'s quest was <span style="background-color:#a61e19;color:white">This art isn't as impactful without Baby Bee in the middle...</span>
*This, the [[Gummy Beacon]], and the [[Naughty List]] are tied for the longest cooldown of Beesmas decorations of 8 hours.
*This and the Gummy Beacon were the only ways to have multiple [[Bees/Event|Event bees]] of the same kind, although they are temporary.
*This art is a reference to the birth of Jesus, which is celebrated by people of Christian faith during Christmas.
*There was a [[Glitches|glitch]] that caused the player's temporary bees to end up stuck midair once they despawned.
*The summoned bees can level up, even if they are not in the player's [[hive]].
**Players cannot make the temporary bees level up by feeding them treats. However, the summoned bees can level up by other means such as [[Ability Tokens#Puppy Love|Puppy Love]] or the [[Beesmas Feast]].
*This is one of the 4 machines that grant the player temporary bees, the others being the [[Honeyday Candles]], the [[Gummy Beacon]] and the [[Honey Wreath]] (after completing [[Honey Bee (NPC)|Honey Bee]]'s quest).
*Sometimes when players look at it from a certain view, some parts of it don't show up.
*Having a Cobalt Bee from the lid art with Crimson Bee in your hive also activates both a red and blue pulse.
*[[Bees]] summoned by Onett's Lid Art are able to attack the mobs the player's actual bees are attacking. However, they cannot convert at the [[hive]].
*Bees summoned by the lid art in 2020 may also have been gifted. It is unknown whether they granted a hive bonus or not.
*A [[Tabby Bee]] from summoning can spawn a Tabby Love token which will increase Tabby Love (if it isn't maxed).
*This is the only way to summon a Guiding Star without a [[Star Amulet#Supreme Star Amulet|Supreme]] or [[Star Amulet#Diamond Star Amulet|Diamond]] Star Amulet with the respective passive.
*Summoned bees don't have to rest, even if you die.
