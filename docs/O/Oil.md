# Oil

{{Color|Oil}} is an [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] that was added in the [[Updates|11/25/2018 update]]. When used, it grants x1.2 [[Stats#Speed|Bee]] and [[System Page#Movespeed|Player Movespeed]] for 10 minutes when activated. Using another will reset the timer, but it does not stack. It can also be used to craft other [[:Category:Accessories|accessories]] or [[items]]. Using a {{Cost|Super Smoothie|4=0}} will override the buff.

== Ways to Obtain ==
* Crafting it via [[Blender]] for:
** {{Cost|Sunflower Seed|50}}
**  {{Cost|Royal Jelly|10}}
* Matching a pair of oil in [[Memory Match]]. 
* As a reward for donating to the [[Wind Shrine]]. 
* As a common drop from [[Robo Bear Challenge]].
* A common reward from [[Stick Bug Challenge]].
** Stick Bug drops oils.
** Defense Totems also yield oils.
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain [[Stickers]] to the [[Sticker Stack]]:
** Flying Photon Bee rewards 1 oil.
** Drooping Stubborn Bee rewards 1 oil.
** Fuzz Bomb rewards 1 oil.
** Yellow Coffee Mug rewards 1 oil.
** White Flag rewards 1 oil.
** Standing Beekeeper rewards 1 oil.
** Honey Dipper rewards 1 oil.

=== Drops ===
* As a drop from certain [[mobs]]:
** [[Tunnel Bear]]
** [[Coconut Crab]]
** [[Stump Snail]]
** Stick Bug
** [[Mantis]] (Rare)
** [[Werewolf]] (Rare)
** [[Aphid#Armored Aphid|Armored Aphid]] (Rare)
** [[King Beetle]] (Rare)
** [[Wild Windy Bee]] (Rare)
* Farming in the [[Sunflower Field]] may rarely yield oil.
** Harvesting [[leaves]] in this field may also yield oil.
* A rare drop from [[Sprout|sprouts]] (the odds increase depending on the rarity of the sprout and if the sprout is in the Sunflower Field).
* As a drop from a [[Ability Tokens#Festive Gift|Festive Gift]] (if [[Festive Bee]] is [[Gifted Bee|gifted]]) or [[Ability Tokens#Festive Mark|Festive Mark]].
*  As a rare gift from a [[Cub Buddy]].
* As a drop from [[Planter|planters]].
** Fully grown [[Planter|planters]] in Sunflower Field are almost always guaranteed to drop an oil when harvested (Cooldown Unknown).
** The {{Cost|Hydroponic Planter|||0}} often drops a lot of oil.
*** Growing it 3 times in a row at the Sunflower Field guarantees 100 oil.
* Activating the [[Target]] in the Sunflower Field in front of the [[Red HQ]].

=== Quests ===
* [[Black Bear|Black Bear's]] repeatable quests have a chance of yielding oil after completion.
* [[Brown Bear]] rewards 1 oil at the 35th, 105th, 160th, 185th, 255th, and 285th quests. 
* [[Science Bear]] gives oil on the following quests:
** Subsidized Agriculture gives 1 oil and other items.
** Meditating On Phenomenon gives 1 oil and other items.
* [[Polar Bear]] occasionally gives oil after the completion of one of his quests.
