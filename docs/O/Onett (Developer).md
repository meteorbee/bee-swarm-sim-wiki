# Onett (Developer)

[[File:Onett2023.png|thumb|220px|Onett’s Roblox avatar.]]
''<sup>This page is for the developer of the game. For the quest-giving NPC, see [[Onett]].</sup>''

'''Piercen Harbut''', better known as '''[https://www.roblox.com/users/1912490/profile Onett]''' on Roblox, is the owner and programmer of [[Bee Swarm Simulator]]. Bee Swarm Simulator is his first and main game on Roblox. He owns the [[Onett's Testing Group]].

Onett also runs alternate accounts to handle messages about errors experienced while playing Bee Swarm Simulator. [https://www.roblox.com/users/556387844/profile BeeSwarmBugReport] handles bug reports and [https://www.roblox.com/users/556383852/profile BeeSwarmSupport] handles the purchase and saves file reports. Onett also has a secret alternate account named [https://www.roblox.com/users/1953773238/profile/ ltpb2020], which he mainly used to upload decals for the [[Ready Player Two]] event. Another account was found by the name [https://www.roblox.com/users/5098329817/profile HiddenBeePotential] but seems to only be used for testing as of now.

[https://twitter.com/OnettDev/ Onett's Twitter account] can be found by searching @OnettDev.

Onett also has an [https://www.instagram.com/beeswarmsimulator/ Instagram account], a [https://www.youtube.com/@PiercenHarbut YouTube account] and  a [https://www.reddit.com/u/BeeSwarmDev/s/iutuKGHL2H Reddit account].

Onett lives in the CST timezone, as confirmed on Discord.

== Trivia ==
*Onett has a cat in real life, as he states that [[Tabby Bee]] looks like his cat in [[Onett|his NPC's]] quest dialogue. The cat he owns is named Sam (revealed in Discord).
*Onett has a [https://www.youtube.com/@PiercenHarbut YouTube] account, a [https://twitter.com/OnettDev Twitter] account, an [https://www.instagram.com/beeswarmsimulator/ Instagram] account, a [https://www.twitch.tv/OnettBSS Twitch] account, and a Discord account (onett1).
*Onett's favorite bees are [[Basic Bee]], [[Rascal Bee]], [[Brave Bee]], and Tabby Bee.
**Onett claimed he likes Basic Bee because it knows it is not as talented as other bees, but it does not care and smiles non-stop and always exudes pride. He said he likes Rascal Bee because of its laughing face.
*Onett's favorite ability is Triangulate, due to the fact that he thinks it's satisfying.
*Onett has a sister (confirmed on Discord).
*His username could be a reference to a city in the Nintendo game EarthBound.
*Onett owns a special [[Tools|tool]] called Onett's Honey Hammer.
*Onett's favorite [[Mobs|mob]] is the [[Rhino Beetle]], as confirmed on Discord.
*Onett's favorite in-game music is [[Music|bpatrol]].
*Onett has broken his hands once.
*Occasionally, Onetts summons mini events. They have various aliases which replace the username, such as "[Alias] has summoned (a) [mini event]".
*Onett's birthday is on 12/24/89, and he is currently 35 years old.
