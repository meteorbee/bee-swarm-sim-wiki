# Comforting Vial

{{Consumables|image=comforting vial.png|description=Grants 4 hours of Comforting Nectar. (Boosts: Blue Pollen, Convert Rate, Convert Rate at hive.)|cooldown=1 Second|cap=10}}

The {{Color|Comforting Vial}} is a consumable [[Nectar Vial|nectar vial]] [[Items|item]] that was added in the [[Updates#2022.12.26|12/26/2022 update]]. Once used, it grants the player 4 hours of the [[Nectar|Comforting Nectar]] [[Buffs & Debuffs|buff]] instantly. It can also be used to craft other [[:Category:Accessories|accessories]] and [[tools]].

== Ways to Obtain ==
* Completing [[Dapper Bear|Dapper Bear's]] 6th, 10th, and 13th quests each grant 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Completing [[Dapper Bear#Blue Drive Quest|Dapper Bear's Blue Drive quest]] grants 1 Comforting Vial and other items.
* Using the [[Nectar Condenser]] to condense 12 hours of Comforting Nectar.
* Adding the Comforting Nectar Icon to the [[Sticker Stack]] rewards 1 Comforting Vial.
