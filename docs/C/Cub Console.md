# Cub Console

The '''Cub Console''' is a menu that can be found in the [[System Page]] and accessed by clicking on the green Cub Console button, under the options menu. It is where the player is able to customize and check their [[Cub Buddy]]. In the Cub Console, the player can equip or unequip their Cub Buddy, change its changeable stats, and view its unchangeable stats.[[File:Cub_Console_New_(Sticker_Update).png|thumb|293x293px|The Cub Console.]]

If the player has a Cub Buddy, they may rename it by clicking on the "Name" window, typing in the desired name, and pressing enter. Cub Buddies age depending on how long it has been equipped while the player is in-game. If it is at the age of newborn, that means that it has been equipped for less than an hour. Once it has been equipped for more than one hour, the Cub Console will measure its age in hours. Once it has been equipped for more than 24 hours, the Cub Console will measure its age in days and hours. Once it has been equipped for more than 3 days, the Cub Console will no longer measure its age in hours. Instead, the Cub Console will solely measure its age in weeks and days. Once it has been equipped for more than 4 weeks, the Cub Console will measure its age in months and weeks.

In the Cub Console, it shows the available [[Cub Buddy]] Skins they own. To equip it, you'll have to open your Inventory, click on [[Sticker Book]] at the top, and if you have a Cub Buddy skin isn't in your Inbox, you'll be able to drag the cub buddy skin from a slot to your hive. Doing this puts a sticker on your hive but also equips the Cub Buddy skin. Skins only change the appearance of the Cub Buddy and will not alter its stats nor provide any benefits. Some skins can be obtained year-round, and some are limited and cannot be obtained after a specific deadline. Each skin looks unique, and some even represent a different [[Bears|bear]] in-game.
