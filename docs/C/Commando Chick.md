}}The '''Commando Chick''' is a [[:Category:Mini Bosses|mini-boss]] [[Chicks|chick]] that spawns in [[Commando Chick's Hideout|its hideout]]. Its hideout is located to the left of [[Brown Bear]] and the [[Wealth Clock]]. To cut the vines, the [[Clippers]], [[Scissors]], or [[Scythe]] needs to be equipped. Once the vines are cut, they will never regrow back.

The Commando Chick has a respawn time of 30:00 (25:30 with [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]] [[Vicious Bee]]). When Commando Chick hides in its egg, it throws grenades (1 if it has more than 2/3 of its health, 2 if it has less than 2/3 of its health, 3 if it has less than 1/3 of its health, 4 if less than 1/100).

The Commando Chick deals 20 damage when colliding with the player and 50 damage if the player is near a grenade when it explodes. Starting at 150 health and level 3, the chick increases in health and level with each successive capture, reaching level 6 by the fifth capture. The chick will grow another level after a progressively higher amount of captures per level up.

Commando Chick has a similar appearance to other Chicks but is twice as large as a normal one. It also has a green shell and red eyes.

The Commando Chick stays still and blocks attacks when it is in its shell. After some time, it jumps out of its shell and resumes running around within the hideout.

== Strategy ==
Standing on the last stair, nearest to Commando Chick's hideout normally makes the chick flee. However, if the player stands on the edge of that stair (as close as possible to the hideout), Commando Chick does not flee and the player's bees can defeat Commando Chick whilst they take no damage. This strategy does not 100% work as there could be a slight chance of the Chick fleeing or accidentally jumping onto the stair because of the hop it does when entering the egg. The player could just move into the hideout and then back onto the stair again to fix this. If the player doesn't want the Commando Chick to stay in its egg, moving out and re-entering the hideout will cause it to change back to its roaming state, do note that it will spawn grenades again though. Also however, there is a glitch in the strategy that the chick that is in its egg will flip upside-down and another chick will come from its same spawner. Another strategy is to get another player to help the player defeat the chick.

==Trivia == 
*This is one of the few mobs to have its own hideout with the others being [[King Beetle]] and [[Tunnel Bear]].
**Commando Chick’s hideout is the only one of the three where you have to complete an obby to enter it.
*Commando Chick is the only chick to have a green color egg.
*Commando Chick can spawn multiple grenades if you keep leaving and entering back in multiple times. However, you may die to this risky trick.
*Commando Chick is the only chick with red eyes; all other chicks have black eyes.
*Commando Chick is one of the two chicks to drop a [[Egg#Mythic Egg|Mythic Egg]], the other being the [[Mondo Chick]].
*Commando Chick is one of two [[mobs]] whose health is saved if the player leaves mid-session, the other being the [[Stump Snail]].
* Other player's bees can attack your Commando Chick.
*Only your Commando Chick can damage you by contact, but all grenades can damage all players.
*There used to be a [[Leaderboards|leaderboard]] behind where it spawns called the [[Most Commando Captures Leaderboard]]. Since 4/7/2020, the leaderboard has been removed.
*There is a glitch where Commando Chick will disappear while being actively fought. When this occurs, a different fleeing message will appear: [🐣 Commando Chick got away! 🐣]
**The normal message is [🐣 Commando Chick fled! (HP Saved) 🐣]
*There is a glitch where Commando Chick's feet clip into the ground. This will cause it to slide and move extremely fast, even if it is in its egg. If it hits a corner, it will not change direction and stay there until it goes into its roaming stage again.
*There was a glitch where Commando Chick would stay in its egg and would not go out of its egg, even when it was moving. This can also make some player's bees keep on missing their shots. The easiest way to fix this glitch was to exit Commando Chick's Hideout and re-enter it.
*Towards the higher levels, Commando Chick can give millions of honey. This means that using a stronger alt account or friend to kill your commando chick can be a quick way to get honey on a newly made account.
*Sometimes, when a players game is extremely laggy or they have high ping, the vines may reappear and can’t be cut. This can be fixed by rejoining the server.
