# Cloud Vial

{{Consumables
|image = CloudVial.png
|description = Summons a Cloud in the field you're standing in. Lasts for 3 minutes.
|cooldown = 1 second
|cap = 10
}}The {{Color|Cloud Vial}} is a [[:Category:Consumables|consumable]] [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] that was added in the [[Updates|9/28/2019 update]]. Once used, it will spawn a [[Clouds|cloud]] in the field you are standing in. 

The player can have a maximum of 10 in their inventory at a time. Obtaining more than 10 will not be added into the inventory unless obtained from a [[Quests|quest]], [[Sticker Stack]] reward, or [[Ornament Presents]].

The summoned [[Clouds|cloud]] will move around the [[Fields|field]]. It grants a 25% (50% with [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]] [[Windy Bee]]) [[pollen]] boost if the player is under it, while causing [[flowers]] under the cloud and [[sprout]]s in the field to grow faster. If there is a sprout in the field, the clouds will help the sprout grow by connecting to the sprout with a straight blue line. The growing speed applied to the sprout increases with the pollen amount required for the sprout. Using it on a field with a [[Planter|planter]] causes the planter to grow 8% faster per cloud, capping at 20%. Each field has a limit of clouds, with bigger and higher-tier fields having a higher limit. If the limit for clouds has reached, using anymore clouds will result in the following message: <span style="background-color:red; color:white;">"There are too many clouds in this field."</span> Windy Bee's [[Ability Tokens#Rain Cloud|Rain Cloud]] ability will ignore this field and will not further summon anymore clouds into the field.

It can't be used in the [[Ant Field]], attempting to do so will result in the following message: <span style="background-color:red; color:white;">"You can't make clouds in a challenge field.".</span>

It also can't be used in the [[Hub Field]], attempting to do so will result in the message: <span style="background-color:red; color:white;">"You can't make clouds in the Hub Field.".</span>

Donating a {{Cost|Cloud Vial||4=1}} to the [[Wind Shrine]] after having donated a {{Cost|Spirit Petal||4=1}} will give the player a chance to obtain a [[Egg#Event Bee Eggs|Windy Bee Egg]] if they do not already own a [[Windy Bee]].

The following audio plays when a {{Cost|Cloud Vial||4=1}} is used:[[File:Vialuse.ogg]]

==Trivia==

* {{Cost|Cloud Vial||4=}} do not spawn a [[Wild Windy Bee]]. Instead, if a {{Cost|Spirit Petal||4=1}} isn't donated yet, it will summon clouds. To summon a Wild Windy Bee, the player would need to donate items to the Wind Shrine.
*{{Cost|Cloud Vial||4=}} are the only items that can directly help grow [[Sprout|sprouts]].
* Clouds, [[sprinklers]] and [[bubbles]] are the only ways to regenerate [[Flowers]] without them naturally regenerating.
*With high enough jump power, collecting the {{Cost|Cloud Vial||4=1}} token past the [[Windy Bee Gate]] is possible without 35 bees. Jumping from the roof of the [[Red HQ]] to the area where the token is makes it possible.
