==Stats==

*+1,000,000 Capacity.
*x1.25 [[Capacity]].
*+400% [[System Page#Convert Rate|Convert Rate]].
*x1.5 [[System Page|Convert Rate at Hive]]
*+10% [[Instant Conversion]].
*+10% [[System Page#Instant White Conversion|Instant White Conversion]].
*+100% [[System Page#White Pollen|White Pollen]].
*[[Passive Abilities#Emergency Coconut Shield|+Passive: Emergency Coconut Shield]].
**Will activate upon taking damage. When it does, the Coconut Canister summons 5 falling [[coconut]]s and grants the "Coconut Shield" effect, which creates a translucent coconut around the player and gives +100% [[System Page#Defense|Defense]] and x1.25 [[System Page#Bee Attack|Bee Attack]] for 10 seconds. It has a 5 minute-cooldown between activations.
*[[Passive Abilities#Inspire Coconuts|+Passive: Inspire Coconuts]].
**Will activate after obtaining 5 stacks of [[Ability Tokens#Inspire|Inspire]]. When it does, it'll summon 5 falling coconuts in the [[Fields|Field]].

==Trivia==

*This, the [[Porcelain Port-O-Hive]], the [[Red Port-O-Hive]] and the [[Blue Port-O-Hive]] are the only containers in the game that require [[Crafting|materials]] other than honey to craft it. 
**If its items were to be crafted using the [[Blender]], it would cost 25 billion {{Cost|Honey}}, 1750 {{Cost|Coconut}}, 15000 {{Cost|Pineapple}}, 15000 {{Cost|Sunflower Seed}}, 7500 {{Cost|Strawberry}}, 7500 {{Cost|Blueberry}}, and 9000 {{Cost|Royal Jelly}} to craft. It would also take 87.5 hours of Playtime to craft the Materials, or 1050 {{Cost|Ticket}} to speed up crafting.
*When the Coconut Canister was first added, it was crafted with {{Cost|Coconut|150}} instead of {{Cost|Coconut|250}}, as this was the maximum amount you could hold at the time.
*This is the only container that gives [[Passive Abilities]].
**This is one of the only two items in the game that gives 2 passives without having to buy another [[Items|item]]. The other one is [[Gummy Boots]], not including certain [[Star Amulet|Supreme Star Amulets]].
*The red and blue "buttons" on each side of the canister repeatedly glow, alternating to represent the lights on ambulances. This was likely added to fit with the passive Emergency Coconut Shield, which also gives off a siren sound upon activation. 
**The buttons' flashing alternation is synced with every player who has the item equipped.
*Note that the player must take damage directly from a mob to activate the Emergency Coconut Shield. For example, taking damage from the fire trail of [[Ants#Fire Ant|Fire Ants]], from the lawn mower or dying from an [[Obstacle Courses|Obby]] does not activate this ability.
**When the Emergency Coconut Shield is activated, it does not protect you from the 'Twigs' ability from [[Stick Bug]].
**There is a rare bug that makes it when the Emergency Coconut Shield is activated when you should've died, you will have a very small amount of health instead.
*The x1.25 capacity stat currently does not function.
