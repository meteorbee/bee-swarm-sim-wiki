''<sup>Not to be confused with [[Party Cogturret]]s, the Beesmas Edition of these.</sup>''

A '''Cogturret''' is a [[mob]] that appears during [[Robo Bear Challenge]].

This is the third type of mob the Player will encounter while doing the challenge, first appearing at round 11.

Cogturrets always position themselves on the field's edge and follow the player similar to cogmowers where they descend to the former field and ascend to the current/recent field the player has visited. Cogturrets behavior is a fixed cycle. First, Cogturrets hide under the field and move along the field's edges, either randomly or at the player, during this state, cogturrets cannot be targeted. Then, they ascend and become targettable again. After a few seconds, they blast a giant red cog projectile in a straight line that destroys flowers, removing all layers of the flower and any goo covering it. After this, the Cogturret hides again and repeats the cycle.

Cogturrets' level can range from 11-22 depending on the current round of the Robo Bear Challenge.

==Strategies ==
*Cogturrets aim either at the player or randomly and have a delay on attacking whenever they ascend. Because of this, moving to the side after they fire a cog will often result in Cogturrets missing their attack.
*Cogturrets will always shoot straight at where they are facing so you can avoid their attacks simply by not being in front of them.
*Stand near the Cogturrets, this allows your bees to lock on to the Cogturrets and quickly deal damage.
*[[Vicious Bee]] and [[Windy Bee]] provide excellent crowd control, especially since Cogturrets often come in pairs or packs. [[Digital Bee]] can also stun many Cogturrets at once and allow them to take extra damage via [[Ability Tokens#Mind Hack|Mind Hack]].
