# Caustic Wax

{{Consumables
|image=Causticwax.png
|description=A glowing hunk of beeswax. Has a 25% chance to GREATLY improve a Beequip - but a 75% chance to DESTROY it!}}
{{Color|Caustic Wax}} is an [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] and a type of [[Waxes|wax]] added in the [[Updates#2021.12.26|12/26/2021 update]] that can be used on a [[Beequip]] to boost its stats. Upon usage, it has a 25% chance of greatly improving the Beequip's stats; however, it has a 75% chance of permanently destroying the Beequip instead, destroying the Beequip and also wasting the Caustic Wax. This wax cannot be used on permanent Beequips. Caustic Wax has [[Beequip#Stats, Waxes, Chances|Upgrade Value]] of 4.

It also can be used to craft a variety of different items, such as [[planter]]s and [[tools]].

When successfully applying Caustic Wax to a bee's Beequip, a message pops up saying:<br>{{Notification||The Wax improved the Beequip!}}

If the wax fails to upgrade the Beequip, a message will pop up saying:<br>{{Notification|red|The Wax destroyed the Beequip...}}

Prior to the removal of Permanent Beequips, using a Caustic Wax on a {{Cost|Festive Wreath|||0}} or {{Cost|Reindeer Antlers}} results in the following message:<br>{{Notification||Cannot use this wax on a Permanent Beequip}}

== Trivia ==
* Caustic Wax's raw ingredients cost:
** {{Cost|Honeysuckle|75}}
** {{Cost|Sunflower Seed|750}}
** {{Cost|Pineapple|1,750}}
** {{Cost|Bitterberry|165}}
** {{Cost|Neonberry|25}}
** {{Cost|Royal Jelly|6,067}} or {{Cost|Honey|{{Hover|6.06B|6,067,000,000}}}}
* The probability of Caustic Wax improving a Beequip 5 times in a row is 1/1024 or ~0.098%. Because of its low chances and its ability to destroy the Beequip, it is not recommended to try and use 5 Caustic Waxes on a Beequip when you are not prepared yet, instead people usually use {{Cost|Swirled Wax|||0}} right after getting 1 successful caustic, to get upgrade rolls in desired stats.
* For some time, this wax could not be used on permanent Beequips.
* This is the only wax that can destroy Beequips.
* [[Bubble Bee Man]]'s Beesmas 2022 quest required players to destroy 31 Beequips with Caustic Wax.
* Prior to the [[Updates#2024.12.25|12/25/2024 update]], this item used to share the same icon with the {{Cost|Debug Wax|4=0}}.
** Although the Debug Wax texture has been changed, both waxes do the same thing, just that Debug Waxes have a 100% chance of working.
