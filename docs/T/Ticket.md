# Ticket

[[File:Ticket symbol.png|thumb|The {{Color|Ticket|4=1}}.|124x124px]]
{{Color|Ticket|Tickets}} are a form of [[:Category:Currency|currency]] added in the [[Updates#2018.4.27|4/27/2018 update]]. They can be used to purchase certain [[items]]. 

==Ways to Obtain==
[[File:TicketShop-0.png|thumb|The [[Ticket Shop]].]]
*The player can obtain {{Cost|Ticket}} as a reward for acquiring [[badges]]. Collecting badges gives {{Cost|Ticket|5}} for earning Cadet badges, {{Cost|Ticket|10}} for earning Hotshot badges, {{Cost|Ticket|15}} for earning Ace badges, {{Cost|Ticket|25}} for earning Master badges, and {{Cost|Ticket|40}} for earning Grandmaster badges. In total, {{Cost|Ticket|2375}} can be obtained from Badges. ({{Cost|Ticket|2335}} if not counting the currently impossible Sticker Stack Grandmaster Badge.)
*The player can buy {{Cost|Ticket}} from the [[Ticket Shop]] with {{Cost|Honey}}, located outside the [[Mountain Top Shop]]. The price increases with every {{Cost|Ticket||4=1}} bought.
*Some [[:Category:Bears|bears]] give {{Cost|Ticket||4=}} as rewards for completing certain [[quests]].
*Redeeming certain valid [[codes]]:
**38217 (Gives {{Cost|Ticket|5}}).
**Bopmaster (Gives {{Cost|Ticket|5}}).
**Crawlers (Gives {{Cost|Ticket|5}}).
**Roof (Gives {{Cost|Ticket|5}}).
**Connoisseur (Gives {{Cost|Ticket|5}}).
**Wax (Gives {{Cost|Ticket|5}} + other items).
* Buying them from the [[Robux Shop]] for {{Cost|Robux}}: 
**1 for 10 ( {{Cost|Robux|10}} per {{Cost|Ticket||4=1}})
**100 for 400 ( {{Cost|Robux|4}} per {{Cost|Ticket||4=1}})
**510 for 1,700 ( {{Cost|Robux|3.33}} per {{Cost|Ticket||4=1}})
** 1,800 for 4,500 ( {{Cost|Robux|2.5}} per {{Cost|Ticket||4=1}})
*The [[Wealth Clock]] gives {{Cost|Ticket}} every hour after interacting with it, and increases incrementally every hour, stopping at the fifth (1 ticket first time, 2 second time, 3 third time, 4 fourth time, and 5 fifth time and onwards).
*They can spawn as a potential match from Normal, Mega, Night and Extreme [[Memory Match]]es.
*Clicking on any Hidden [[Sticker]] grants {{Cost|Ticket|1|4=}} each (excluding seeker-stickers).
*Adding certain [[Sticker|Stickers]] to the [[Sticker Stack]] grants {{Cost|Ticket}}.
*As a Reward for being in the top 100 of any daily leaderboard, ranging from 25, 50 to 100 {{Cost|Ticket||4=}} depending on the leaderboard.


=== Drops===
*As a drop from [[Planter|planters]]: 
**The number of {{Cost|Ticket}} it can potentially drop depends on the type of planter.
**{{Cost|Ticket Planter|||}} always drop {{Cost|Ticket|10+|4=}} when harvested at full growth.
*As a common drop from [[Mythic Meteor Shower|Mythic Meteors]].
*There is a chance of getting a {{Cost|Ticket||4=1}} as a reward for defeating [[mobs]]. The probability depends on the type of mob as well as the player’s Loot Luck.  
**[[Tunnel Bear]], [[Stump Snail]], and [[King Beetle]] are guaranteed to grant {{Cost|Ticket}} upon defeat
**[[Wild Windy Bee]] will yield a limited amount of {{Cost|Ticket}} per day.
**The [[Commando Chick]] will always drop {{Cost|Ticket|1|4=}} upon being captured.
*** Capturing it for the 25th time gives {{Cost|Ticket|100}}.
*Through a {{Cost|Cub Buddy|4=0}} gift.
* [[Puffshroom|Puffshrooms]] drop {{Cost|Ticket||4=}} (the rarer the Puffshroom, the more it gives).
* [[Honeystorm]]s are guaranteed to spawn {{Cost|Ticket||4=1}} tokens.
* As a Retro Swarm Challenge Reward.
* As a rare drop from [[Ability Tokens#Festive Gift|Festive Gifts]].
* As a [[Sprout]] reward, besides Moon and Debug variants (the rarer the sprout, the more {{Cost|Ticket}} it will yield; rare sprouts and above guarantee {{Cost|Ticket}}).
*[[Sparkles]] may yield {{Cost|Ticket}}.
*Very rarely, [[flowers]] may generate a {{Cost|Ticket||4=1}} token. The base probability is around 1/1000 (0.1%), however it does increase with [[Loot Luck]] and [[System Page|Ticket Chance]].
*Activating a [[Golf#Targets|target]] rewards {{Cost|Ticket}} and other items.

=== Token Locations===
*On top of the biggest pineapple in the [[Pineapple Patch]]. Grants {{Cost|Ticket|3}}.
*In the back of [[Red HQ]], near the ceiling (the best way to get it seems to be to get x5 [[Ability Tokens#Haste|Haste]], climb on top of the [[Red Teleporter|teleporter]], jump up and walk on the edge of the leaderboard, and then glide to the {{Cost|Ticket||4=1}}. The enhanced jump ability granted by certain [[:Category:Hats|hats]], [[:Category:boots|boots]], and/or the [[Ability Tokens#Bear Morph|bear morph]] will also help).
* Next to the Glider inside the [[Mountain Top Shop]].
*On a platform in the [[Obstacle Courses#Cloud Obby|Cloud Obby]], fairly high above the [[Pine Tree Forest]] and [[Pumpkin Patch]]. Grants {{Cost|Ticket|5}}. (Jump off the tip of the red shoulder guard of the [[30 Bee Gate]], then glide or parachute down to it).
*On a small ledge between the [[Lion Bee Gate]] and the [[Brave Bee Gate]]. Grants {{Cost|Ticket|5}}. The player must use the slingshot and move to the left. Parachute at the right moment to land on the rock. Alternative: Jump down from in front of the Lion Bee Gate. A Parachute/Glider can be helpful, but not absolutely necessary.
* Behind the red shoulder guard of the [[Bear Gate]] (i.e. inside the 30 Bee Area). Grants {{Cost|Ticket|10}}.
*On the awning over [[Mother Bear|Mother Bear's]] area. (Easily reached from the ramp that leads to the [[Honey Bee Gate]]).
*On top of the [[Ant Gate]]. Grants {{Cost|Ticket|5}}.
*Behind the [[Honey Dispenser]] in the [[Obstacle Courses|Lava Obby]]. Upon entering, immediately turn right. Grants {{Cost|Ticket|3}}. This replaced one of the [[Plastic Egg]] locations.
*Underneath the [[Basic Bee Gate]] ramp, where [[Honey Bee (NPC)|Honey Bee]] used to be. Grants {{Cost|Ticket|3}}. This replaced one of the {{Cost|Plastic Egg|4=0}} locations, when they were removed
*In the [[Mazes#Blue Maze|Blue Maze]], turn left and then take the first right. Grants {{Cost|Ticket|5}}. This replaced one of the {{Cost|Plastic Egg|4=0}} locations.
*Inside the tunnel behind the [[Coconut Dispenser]] in the [[Coconut Cave]]. Grants {{Cost|Ticket|5}}.

==Uses==
[[File:TicketTent2021.png|thumb|The Ticket Tent.]] 
*Buying a {{Cost|Royal Jelly|4=1}} From the [[Royal Jelly Dispenser]] for {{Cost|Ticket|6|4=}} with a cooldown of 3 seconds.
*Buying {{Cost|Gumdrops}} from the [[Gumdrop Shop]] at the cost of {{Cost|Ticket|1|4=}} per 3 {{Cost|Gumdrops}}.
*Buying {{Cost|Stinger}} from the [[Stinger Shop]] at the cost of {{Cost|Ticket|10}} per {{Cost|Stinger|4=0}}.
* Buying {{Cost|Magic Bean}} from the [[Magic Bean Shop]] at the cost of {{Cost|Ticket|20|4=}} per {{Cost|Magic Bean|4=0}}.
* {{Cost|Ticket|1|4=}} is required to use an [[Instant Converter]].
*{{Cost|Ticket|10}} are required to use the [[Ant Pass Dispenser]].
*The [[Ticket Tent]] has the following items available: 
**{{Cost|Silver Egg|||0}} ({{Cost|Ticket|50|4=}})
**{{Cost|Gold Egg|||0}} ({{Cost|Ticket|100|4=}})
** {{Cost|Diamond Egg|||0}} ({{Cost|Ticket|400|4=}})
**{{Cost|Mythic Egg|||0}} ({{Cost|Ticket|2500|4=}})
**{{Cost|Cobalt Bee Egg|||0}} and {{Cost|Crimson Bee Egg|||0}} ({{Cost|Ticket|250|4=}} each, one time purchase)
**Miscellaneous [[Bees/Event|Event Bees]] ({{Cost|Ticket|500|4=}} each, one time purchase)
**{{Cost|Star Treat|||0}} ({{Cost|Ticket|1000|4=}}).
*{{Cost|Ticket|50|4=}} are required to start another [[Stick Bug Challenge]] when the player’s free challenge is already used.
*{{Cost|Ticket}} can be used to speed up materials that are currently being made in the [[Blender]].
*{{Cost|Ticket|5}} are required to store [[nectar]] in the [[Nectar Pot]].
*{{Cost|Ticket|50|4=}} are required to use the [[Robo Pass Dispenser]].
* Varying amounts of {{Cost|Ticket}} can be used to increase the maximum amount of [[Beequip|Beequips]] one can hold in their Beequip Storage, requiring {{Cost|Ticket|123500|4=}} in total to max it out.
*Varying amounts of {{Cost|Ticket}} (Starting at 20 and capping at 1,000 per upgrade) can be used to increase the maximum amount of [[Sticker|Stickers]] one can hold in their Sticker Storage, requiring {{Cost|Ticket|42420|4=}} in total to max it out.
*{{Cost|Ticket}}, along with {{Cost|Honey}}, are used to purchase Beequips in [[Dapper Bear's Shop]].

==Trivia==
*It bears the face of a [[Basic Bee]].
*Currently, the most expensive purchase that can be made using {{Cost|Ticket}} is the final slot upgrade for the [[Beequip|Beequip Storage]], costing {{Cost|Ticket|20000}} to increase storage from a maximum of 95 stored Beequips to 100 stored Beequips.
*{{Cost|Ticket}}, along with {{Cost|Honey}}, were used to purchase Market Boosts from the [[Boost Market]].
*You need a total of {{Cost|Ticket|179420}} in total to Max out Sticker and Beequip storages, buy all event bees from the [[Ticket Tent]] and gift all of the event bees with {{Cost|Star Treat|4=0}}.
