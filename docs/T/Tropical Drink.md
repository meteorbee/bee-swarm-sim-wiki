# Tropical Drink

{{Consumables
|image = TropicalDrinkInventory.png
|description = Grants x1.25 White Pollen, +10% Instant Conversion and +5% Critical Chance for 10 minutes.
|cooldown = 1 second
}}

{{Color|Tropical Drink}} is a [[:Category:Crafted|craftable]] [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] added in the [[Updates#2019.09.28|9/28/2019 update]]. When used, it grants following buffs for 10 minutes:
*x1.25 [[System Page#White Pollen|White Pollen]]
*+5% [[System Page#Critical Chance|Critical Chance]]
*+10% [[Instant Conversion]]
It can also be used as a material to craft certain items.

== Ways to Obtain==
[[File:Tropical drink.jpg|thumb|A Tropical Drink inside the player’s inventory]]
* [[Crafting]] it via [[Blender]] for: 
** {{Cost|Coconut|10}}
** {{Cost|Enzymes|2}}
** {{Cost|Oil|2}}
* Completing certain [[Quests]]:
** [[Brown Bear]]'s 85th Quest Rewards 1 Tropical Drink.
** [[Black Bear]]'s Boss Battles Quest Rewards 3 Tropical Drinks.
*** Black Bear has a rare chance to reward Tropical Drinks after the completion of one of his repeatable quests.
* As a drop from [[Sprout|Sprouts]] in the Coconut Field (the odds increase depending on the rarity of the sprout).
* Matching a pair of Tropical Drink in [[Memory Match#Extreme Memory Match|Extreme Memory Match]] or [[Memory Match#Mega Memory Match|Mega Memory Match]] or [[Memory Match#Winter Memory Match|Winter Memory Match]].

=== Drops ===
* As a drop from from certain mobs:
** [[Wild Windy Bee]]
** [[Aphid#Armored Aphid|Armored Aphid]]
** [[Coconut Crab]] (guaranteed, amounts depend on the time it took to kill the crab).
* As a very rare [[Cub Buddy]] gift.
* As a drop from fully-grown [[Planter|Planters]] harvested on [[Coconut Field]].
* As a very rare drop from [[leaves]] in the [[Coconut Field]].
