Every 3rd swing releases a wave of water that collects pollen, pops [[bubble]]s, and converts pollen when a bee is hit by the wave.

When a bubble from the player is popped by any means, it grants a stack of "Tide Power" (2 stacks if it was a [[Bubble#Golden Bubbles|golden bubble]]), which grants faster tool swinging speed and stronger waves. Higher stacks grants a stronger buff. 

When Tide Power reaches 495 stacks or greater, each swing of the tool will release a large wave. When Tide Power reaches 500 stacks, it is replaced with "Tidal Surge". It lasts for 10 seconds and grants x2.5 to x1.5 tool swinging speed (starting at x2.5 and gradually decreasing until it reaches x1.5), and every swing releases a large tidal wave. Popping a bubble during a Tidal Surge extends the duration of it.

Activating a Tidal Surge grants 1 minute of "Tide Blessing". Whenever a tidal wave hits a Balloon of the player's, it causes the balloon to convert up to 8% of your Convert Total into Honey Tokens worth x2.5 that (these tokens can not be collected by token link) and if the balloon is golden, grants 10 seconds of Tide Blessing. Tide Blessing stacks up to 4 hours, and grants up to x1.2 Blue Pollen, Honey From Tokens, Convert Rate at Hive, Pollen from Bees, and Pollen from Tools.

== Trivia== 
*The Tide Popper was originally called the "Aqua Popper".
*Its red counterpart is the [[Dark Scythe|Dark Scythe,]] which is sold in the [[Red HQ|Red HQ,]] and its white counterpart is the [[Gummyballer]], which is sold in [[Gummy Bear's Lair|Gummy Bear’s Lair]].
*If a wave is as high as a balloon, it can make the balloon convert pollen without Tidal Surge.
*This is the only blue tool that doesn't multiply the amount of blue pollen. It has the same case as the [[Dark Scythe]].
*There is currently a glitch that makes the player holding the Tide Popper have black trails.
*This is tied with the Dark Scythe for being the fifth most expensive item in the game, both having a cost of {{Hover|2.5T|2,500,000,000,000}} Honey. In front of the Tide Popper and Dark Scythe is the [[Petal Planter]], costing {{Hover|5T|5,000,000,000,000}} Honey, [[Coconut Belt]] costing 7.5T Honey, the Gummyballer, costing {{Hover|10T|10,000,000,000,000}} Honey, and [[The Planter Of Plenty]], costing {{Hover|100T|100,000,000,000,000}} Honey.
*If the ingredients for the item were to be crafted, the total cost would be {{Hover|2.5T|2,500,000,000,000}} Honey, 6,750 [[Honeysuckle]]s, 112,500 [[Treat#Sunflower Seed|Sunflower Seeds]], 641,250 [[Pineapple]]s, 528,750 [[Strawberry|Strawberries]], 603,750 [[Blueberry|Blueberries]], 450 [[Magic Bean]]s, 4,500 [[Coconut]]s, 150 Stingers, 7,425 [[Bitterberry|Bitterberries]], 1,950 [[Neonberry|Neonberries]], and 411,150 [[Royal Jelly|Royal Jellies]].
*On December 28, 2021, the Tide Popper was obtainable through an exploit that was able to give you the Tide Popper, the same going for the Gummyballer and Dark Scythe. This was later patched by Onett and everybody who used the exploit would get a full reset on their account.
*On 1/01/2022, there was a [[Glitches|glitch]] where if the Tide Popper was viewed in the shop, it would cause the game to freeze and not let the player exit.
*There was a glitch allowing players to buy the Tide Popper without completing 250 Gifted Bucko Bee quests. If the Tide Popper was bought without doing 250 Gifted Bucko Bee quests, the item could be equipped after doing 250 Gifted Bucko Bee quests. This was also the case with the [[Dark Scythe]].
*After the [[Updates|05/23/2024 update]], the Tide Popper needs {{Cost|Comforting Vial|3}} and 150 Stingers, more than the previous 0 and less than the previous 200 (respectively) before.
