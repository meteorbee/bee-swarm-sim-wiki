}}A {{Color|Ticket Planter}} is a single use [[planter]] that always drops [[ticket]]s when harvested. It's also guaranteed to spawn a [[Puffshroom]] at 100% growth.

Fully grown Ticket Planters gives at least {{Cost|Ticket|10}} upon harvesting.

== Ways to obtain ==
* [[Dapper Bear]] gives 3 Ticket Planters if the player speaks to him for the first time.
* If the player completes Dapper Bear’s quests and they already got Beequip slots from Bee Bear in previous years, they get a Ticket Planter for each Beequip slot.
* As a rare reward in Retro Swarm Challenge.
* As a rare drop from [[Sprout|Sticker Sprouts]].
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain stickers to the Sticker Stack:
** {{Cost|Dapper From Above Sticker|4=0}} rewards 1 Ticket Planter.

== Trivia == 

* This is one of the two planters that is guaranteed to spawn a [[Puffshroom]]. The other is the {{Cost|Sticker Planter|||0}}.
* This, the {{Cost|Sticker Planter|||0}} and the {{Cost|Paper Planter|||0}} share the same base model.    
** They also share the same outline for their decals.
* It is one of two planters that mentions an in-game item in its name, the other planter is the {{Cost|Sticker Planter|||0}}.
* It gives the second-best nectar multiplier in the game, 2x, behind the [[Festive Planter]] with 3x.
* It is one of 4 single-use planters, the others being the {{Cost|Paper Planter|||0}}, the {{Cost|Festive Planter|||0}} and the {{Cost|Sticker Planter|||0}}.
* This, the {{Cost|Sticker Planter|||0}} and the {{Cost|Paper Planter|||0}} are the only one-use planters in the game that can be obtained infinitely.
