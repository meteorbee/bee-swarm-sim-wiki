# Turpentine

{{Color|Turpentine}} is a craftable inventory [[Items|item]] added in the [[Updates|12/26/2021 update]]. It is used to remove all [[waxes]] from a [[Beequip]]. 

Turpentine have a maximum cap of 25. Obtaining while having more than 25 turpentines will not add them to the player's inventory unless received through [[quests]], redeeming [[codes]], or purchasing them in the [[Robux Shop]] through limited-time offers.

== Ways to Obtain ==

* [[Crafting]] via [[Blender]] for:
** {{Cost|Super Smoothie|10}}
** {{Cost|Caustic Wax|10}}
** {{Cost|Star Jelly|100}}
** {{Cost|Honeysuckle|1000}}
* As an extremely rare drop from an Epic [[Puffshroom]], a very rare drop from a Legendary Puffshroom and an uncommon drop from a Mythic Puffshroom.
* As a very rare drop from any [[planter]].
** {{Cost|The Planter Of Plenty|||0}} has a ~10% chance to drop a Turpentine.
** {{Cost|Petal Planter|||0}} has a ~1% chance to drop a Turpentine.
* As a reward for reaching the Top 25 Daily [[Leaderboards|Leaderboard]] for [[Retro Swarm Challenge]].
* As a very rare reward from the Retro Swarm Challenge.
* As an extremely rare reward from the [[Robo Bear Challenge]].
* From the following [[:Category:Beesmas Decorations|Beesmas Decorations]] available only during the [[:category:Beesmas|Beesmas event]]:
** Brown Bear's [[Stockings]] has an exceptionally rare chance to drop a Turpentine.
** Dapper Bear's [[Samovar]] drops a Turpentine on the 25th use.
** Completing [[Robo Party]] on rank 45 and 50 for the first time gives the player who started it a Turpentine and other rewards.
* As a reward for [[Stick Bug|Stick Bug's]] Beesmas quest.
* Purchasing the Triumphant Turp Pack from [[Bee Bear's Catalog]] gives 1 Turpentine + other items
* By purchasing the Royal Winter Wonder Haul for {{Cost|Robux|4,500}} gives 1 Turpentine + other items.
