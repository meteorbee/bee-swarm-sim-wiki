# Invigorating Vial

{{Consumables|image=Invigorating vial.png|description=Grants 4 hours of Invigorating Nectar. (Boosts: Red Pollen, Convert Rate, Bee Attack.)|cooldown=1 Second|cap=10}}

The {{Color|Invigorating Vial}} is a consumable [[Nectar Vial|nectar vial]] [[Items|item]] that was added in the [[Updates#2022.12.26|12/26/2022 update]]. Once used, it grants the player 4 hours of the [[Nectar|Invigorating Nectar]] [[Buffs & Debuffs|buff]] instantly. It can also be used to craft other [[:Category:Accessories|accessories]] and [[tools]].

== Ways to Obtain ==
* Completing [[Dapper Bear|Dapper Bear's]] 9th and 12th quests.
* Using the [[Nectar Condenser]] to condense 12 hours of Invigorating Nectar.
* Adding the Invigorating Nectar Icon to the [[Sticker Stack]] grants 1 Invigorating Vial.
* Dropped by a Level 10 [[Snowbear]].
