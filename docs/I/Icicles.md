# Icicles

{{Unobtainable}}
{{Beequip}}
'''Icicles''' are a [[Bond|level]] 10 [[:Category:Beesmas|Beesmas]] [[Beequip]]. They have an equip limit of three. They have a blue color requirement. They can only be equipped to bees that know the [[Ability Tokens#Bomb|Blue Bomb]] ability.

** This makes the Icicles the first Beequip obtainable outside of Beesmas with the [[Poinsettia]] being the second.
