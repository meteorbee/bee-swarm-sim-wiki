# Eviction

}}The {{Color|Eviction}} was an [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] that used to be purchasable in the [[Mountain Top Shop]] for {{Cost|Honey|50,000}}. When used, it removed a [[Bees|bee]] from the [[hive]]. It was the cheapest item available at the Mountain Top Shop before being removed.

{{Color|Eviction|Evictions}} were mainly used to remove bees and replace them with [[egg]]s if the player couldn't afford a [[Hive Slot]], taking note that eggs could not be placed on hive slots with a bee in the game version that had evictions.
[[File:Screenshot 31.png|thumb|210x210px|The Eviction Shop in the Mountain Top Shop.]]
Evicting a bee would completely reset the hive slot, meaning that the bee's [[bond]], level, and [[Gifted Bee|gifted]] status, would be reset, so if a new egg were hatched, the new bee would have started at level 1. The previous bee will not affect the bee type or gifted status of the new bee. 

'''All evictions that anyone might have had will no longer be in their inventory.'''

== Way(s) To Obtain ==
There are currently no methods to obtain the {{Cost|Eviction|4=0}}, as it has been removed from the game.

'''The methods below are no longer possible.'''
* Purchasing an {{Cost|Eviction|4=0}} in the Mountain Top Shop for {{Cost|Honey|50,000}}.
* Finishing [[Bubble Bee Man|Bubble Bee Man's]] Beesmas 2018 [[Quests|quest]] 'B.B.M. Mission' rewarded {{Cost|Eviction|800}} and other items.

== Removal Reason ==
As of the [[Updates#2018.09.28|9/28/2019 update]], the player can now use eggs on existing bees, removing the need to evict bees, while also allowing a bee to keep its level. Thus, [[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] removed the {{Cost|Eviction|4=0}} from both the shop and every player's inventory.
