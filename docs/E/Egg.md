# Egg

[[File:New egg page.png|thumb|Eggs available inside the [[Robux Shop]].]]'''Eggs''' are a type of [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] where the player drags into their [[hive]] to hatch a [[Bees|bee]], can be donated to the [[Wind Shrine]] or get used to roll the [[Sticker Printer]]. They are also the items that help players start playing in the [[Bee Swarm Simulator|game]]. Eggs come in many different types, some offering chances at different rarities of bees, and some hatching a specific bee. Currently, a total of 6 (or 12 if counting the [[Gifted Bee|gifted]] variants of the Basic, Silver, Gold, Diamond and Mythic eggs and the Choose-A-Mythic Egg) different types of eggs exist in the game, not including [[Plastic Egg]]s, specific bee eggs, the [[Flight of the Bumble Egg]], the [[Swarming Egg of the Hive]].

Ever since the [[Updates#2019.09.28|9/28/2019 update]], players do not need a vacant [[Hive Slot|hive slot]] to hatch an egg. Now, players can hatch an egg on top of an existing bee to replace it. The level of the bee remains the same, acting like a [[Royal Jelly]].

Please also note that due to regulations in Netherlands and Belgium, any eggs in the Robux shop won't be available to purchase for Dutch and Belgian consumers. This restriction counts for every egg in the shop.

{{Tabber
|Basic Egg|{{=}}{{=}}{{=}} Basic Egg {{=}}{{=}}{{=}}
[[File:Basic Egg.png{{!}}left{{!}}100px]] The {{Color|Basic Egg}} is the most common type of egg found in the game. It will almost always hatch into a [[Basic Bee]] (87% chance), but in rare cases, it can hatch into a [[Bees/Rare{{!}}Rare Bee]] (10% chance), an [[Bees/Epic{{!}}Epic Bee]] (2.5% chance), a [[Bees/Legendary{{!}}Legendary Bee]] (0.5% chance), or into a [[Gifted Bee]] (0.4% chance), with a 0.348% (1 out of 287.35632184) chance of getting a Gifted Basic Bee from the egg. [[Bees/Event{{!}}Event bees]] cannot be hatched from this egg. This egg cannot be used on a Basic Bee.
{{clear}}

<u>Ways to obtain a Basic Egg:</u>

* First-time players receive a free {{Color|Basic Egg}} in their inventory.
* Purchasing from the [[Basic Egg Shop]] located near the [[Sunflower Field]] for a base price of {{Cost|Honey|1,000}} and capping at {{Cost|Honey|10,000,000}}. Every time the player purchases a {{Color|Basic Egg}}, the price of the next {{Color|Basic Egg}} scales up.
* Adding the Round Basic Bee [[Sticker]] to the [[Sticker Stack]] rewards 1 {{Color|Basic Egg}}.
|Silver Egg|{{=}}{{=}}{{=}} Silver Egg {{=}}{{=}}{{=}}
[[File:Silver Egg.png{{!}}left{{!}}100px]] The {{Color|Silver Egg}} is a type of egg that will most likely hatch into a Rare bee (64.9% chance), but it has a small chance to hatch into an Epic bee (30% chance), a Legendary bee (5% chance), or a Mythic bee (0.1% chance). A Basic Bee or an Event bee cannot be hatched from this egg.
{{clear}}

<u>Ways to obtain a Silver Egg:</u>

* Purchasing from [[Ticket Tent]] for {{Cost|Ticket|50}} each. 
* Purchasing from the [[Robux Shop]] for {{Cost|Robux|100}}. 
* By purchasing the {{Color|Silver Egg}} in [[Bee Bear's Catalog]] for {{Cost|Snowflake|25}} and {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear|5}} during Winter Beesmas 2024.
* Finishing [[Black Bear{{!}}Black Bear's]] and [[Panda Bear{{!}}Panda Bear's]] {{Color|Silver Egg}} [[quests{{!}}questline]].
* As a reward from [[Brown Bear{{!}}Brown Bear's]] 25th quest.
* Completing the 25th [[Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine{{!}}Sticker-Seeker quest]] rewards you 1 {{Color|Silver Egg}}.
* An extremely low chance of dropping from a [[Mantis]] (in increments of 1 or 5).
* A low chance of dropping from the [[King Beetle]] and [[Tunnel Bear]].
* A low chance to drop from [[Sprout{{!}}Rare sprouts]].
* An extremely rare chance from [[leaves]] when collecting [[pollen]] in the [[Clover Field]].
* As a drop from a {{Cost|Festive Planter|||0}}.

== Specific Event Bee Eggs ==
[[Bees/Event|Event bee]] eggs are a special type of egg that will always hatch into a specific type of Event bee. After an Event bee egg is hatched, the player will receive an exclusive type of [[Royal Jelly#Royal Jelly Variants|permanent royal jelly]] for that specific Event bee. The Event bee royal jelly will turn any bee into that kind of Event bee. It cannot be applied if the player currently has that Event bee in their hive though.
And instead of hatching into a random bee of a particular rarity,  '''specific event bee eggs''' will always hatch into the egg's respective event bee. All of these eggs share the same appearance of the basic egg.

== Trivia ==

* The [[Digital Bee]], [[Vicious Bee]], [[Windy Bee]], [[Festive Bee]], and [[Gummy Bee]] Eggs are the only types of [[Bees/Event|Event bee]] eggs that could or can be obtained without spending robux or [[ticket]]s. (The Festive Bee Egg can now be purchased in the [[Ticket Tent]] for {{Cost|Ticket|500}}.)
* Star Eggs and Basic Eggs are the only two eggs that can hatch into a [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]] [[Basic Bee]].
* [[Plastic Egg]]s, the [[Flight of the Bumble Egg]] (Egg Hunt 2019), and the [[Swarming Egg of the Hive]] (Egg Hunt 2020) are the only types of eggs that do not hatch into a [[Bees|bee]].
* Any kind of specific bee egg and event bee egg will have the same icon as a Basic Egg in the inventory.
* The [[Bear Bee]], [[Festive Bee]], [[Puppy Bee]], and [[Windy Bee]] Eggs are the only type of Event bee eggs that can/could be obtained directly by spending robux.
* Basic Eggs are the only type of eggs that can be obtained with honey.
* The star that appears on the Star Egg is slightly larger than the stars on the other types of gifted eggs.
* The Mythic Egg is the only type of egg with the name of the bee rarity that it hatches into.
** The Mythic Egg is also the only egg that has a 100% of hatching into a certain bee rarity (except for Event bee eggs and specific bee eggs). However, before mythic bees were added, diamond eggs used to have a 100% chance of hatching into a legendary bee.
** The Mythic Egg is also the only egg with a letter on it.
** The Choose-a-Mythic Egg is the only Egg that allows the player to choose what bee hatches.
* The [[Fuzzy Bee]] egg, [[Buoyant Bee]] egg and the [[Precise Bee]] egg are the only non-event and the only specific bee eggs that could be bought from the Robux Shop.
* The Gifted Silver Egg and the Star Egg are the only Gifted eggs that can be obtained by completing the main questlines from [[:Category:NPC|NPCs]] (Black Bear's "Getting Stumped", Mother Bear's "Seven To Seven", and Panda Bear's "Royal Rumble").
* The player cannot hatch a specific bee egg on that specific bee. For example, a Bumble Bee egg cannot be hatched on a Bumble Bee.
* The Basic Egg is the only egg without a gifted variant of itself, excluding specific bee eggs.
** Similarly, the Star Egg is the only Gifted egg without a regular variant.
* The Diamond and Basic Eggs are the only non-specific or event eggs that can hatch into a bee of their name (the [[Diamond Bee|Diamond]] and Basic Bees, respectively), though you can get other bees from these eggs.
* The Bubble Bee Egg is the only non-Event specific bee egg that can be obtained from a quest (B.B.M's Naughty List).
* The Tadpole Bee Egg is the only egg that can be obtained by giving a present (to Bubble Bee Man).
