# Enzymes

{{Consumables

|description = Grants +10% Instant Conversion and x1.25 Convert Rate for 10 minutes.
|cooldown = 1 second
}}
{{Color|Enzymes}} are an [[:Category:Inventory|inventory item]] that was added in the [[Updates#2018.11.25|11/25/2018 update]]. When used, it grants +10% [[System Page#Instant Conversion|Instant Conversion]] and x1.25 [[System Page#Convert Rate|Convert Rate]] for 10 minutes when activated. Using another {{Cost|Enzymes|}} will not stack them, but will reset the timer. {{Cost|Enzymes|}} can also be used for crafting other [[:Category:Accessories|Accessories]] or items. Using a {{Cost|Super Smoothie||4=1}} will override the buff.

== Ways to Obtain ==
* [[Crafting]] it via [[Blender]] for:
** {{Cost|Pineapple|50}}
** {{Cost|Royal Jelly|10}}
* As a potential match from all [[Memory Match|Memory Matches]].
* The [[Stick Bug Challenge]] includes other sources of {{Cost|Enzymes||4=}} on top of rewarding {{Cost|Enzymes||4=}} upon completion:
** Stick Bug can drop {{Cost|Enzymes||4=}}.
** [[Stick Bug Challenge#Defense Totem|Defense Totems]] can drop {{Cost|Enzymes||4=}}.
* Redeeming certain valid [[Codes]].
* As a Stack Reward for adding certain [[Sticker|Stickers]] to the [[Sticker Stack]]:
** Honey Bee Bear rewards {{Cost|Enzymes|1|4=}}.
** Panicked Science Bear rewards {{Cost|Enzymes|1|4=}}.
** Forward Facing Spider rewards {{Cost|Enzymes|1|4=}}.
** Standing Caterpillar rewards {{Cost|Enzymes|1|4=}}.
** Yellow Hi rewards {{Cost|Enzymes|1|4=}}.
** Taunting Doodle Person rewards {{Cost|Enzymes|1|4=}}.
** Orange Step Array rewards {{Cost|Enzymes|1|4=}}.
** Orange Green Tri Deco rewards {{Cost|Enzymes|1|4=}}.

== Trivia ==
* {{Cost|Enzymes||4=}} used to look like pills. However, Roblox removed them because they appeared to have violated the Roblox Terms Of Service. They were later re-created, published under the name "Pineapple Candies" and with the appearance they have currently.
* If multiple {{Cost|Enzymes||4=}} are used in a [[Blender|Blender's]] crafting recipe, the chat log displaying the lost materials would display "Enzymess" adding an extra 's' instead of the proper plural. This small bug applies to {{Cost|Gumdrops}} and various other items.
