# Whistle

{{Beequip}}
A {{Color|Whistle}} is a [[Bond|level]] 8 [[Beequip]]. It has an equip limit of one. There is no color requirement. It can only be equipped to [[Brave Bee]], [[Commander Bee]], [[Rascal Bee]], [[Demo Bee]], [[Cool Bee]], and [[Rage Bee]].

==Ways to Obtain==   
*Can be a product available for purchase in [[Dapper Bear's Shop]].
* As an exceptionally rare drop from harvesting the {{Cost|Plastic Planter|4=0}}.
*As a very rare drop from harvesting {{Cost|The Planter Of Plenty|4=0}}.
*As a reward from [[Robo Bear Challenge]].
