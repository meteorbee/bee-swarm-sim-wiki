# Wind Shrine

[[File:WindShrine.png|thumb|225px|The Wind Shrine.]]
[[File:Buginess.png|thumb|A bug that happens with the Wind Shrine when the chimes go too high up.|224x224px]]
[[File:Obraz 2023-12-30 165548097.png|thumb|225x225px|Sometimes, one or more chimes disappear due to a bug when they move too high up.]]

The '''Wind Shrine''' is located beyond the [[Windy Bee Gate]], next to the [[Pepper Patch]]. It allows players to donate items to it in exchange for rewards such as [[Field Wind]]s and [[honey]]. The Wind Shrine has a cooldown of 1 hour between each donation. Donating [[Cloud Vial|cloud vials]] after donating a [[Spirit Petal]] is currently the only way to obtain a [[Windy Bee]].

The Wind Shrine also indicates if a [[Wild Windy Bee]] is active in one of the [[fields]]. If the chimes are continuously moving around and nobody has donated recently, a Wild Windy Bee will be somewhere around the map.
[[File:Moving shrine.mp4|thumb|254x254px|A video of Wind Shrine moving.]]
The following audio plays when a player has donated something to the Wind Shrine:<br/>[[File:Windchimedonate.ogg]]

==Usage==
Upon interacting with the shrine, it says "Would you like to make an offering to the Wind Shrine?" If the player clicks on the dialogue, a window pop-up will appear, in which it allows the player to donate any amount of almost any item in their inventory to the shrine. Upon doing so, a message follows, with the chimes moving around swiftly. If the player cancels the donation, it instead says "You canceled the donation."

If the player interacts with the Shrine during cooldown, it says "You may only donate to the Shrine once per hour. Come back in x: y." (x = minutes, y = seconds)

If the player wants to donate multiple items at once, they can hold down the "+" button. The rate at which the amount of that certain item increases the longer the "+" button is held down.

===Obtaining a Windy Bee===
'''To get the Windy Bee from the Shrine, players must first donate a spirit petal to it.''' After donating a Spirit petal, any Cloud Vial donated gives the player a chance to obtain a Windy Bee Egg. The player's chance to obtain the Windy Bee Egg depends on how much favor the player has with the shrine.

If the player receives a Windy Bee Egg, the Wind Shrine will display the following message: "And it calls back to you!". The Windy Bee victory jingle will play, and server-wide message will appear in the chat, reading "🎉 [Player] has tamed a Windy Bee! 🎉", but if it fails, the message will instead read, "But it doesn't respond..."
[[File:RobloxScreenShot20201012 223413205.png|thumb|The message that pops up if a player has successfully tamed Windy Bee.]]

If the player had purchased a Windy Bee Egg from the [[Robux Shop]], they would get a [[Star Treat|star treat]] in place of a [[Egg#Specific Bee Eggs|Windy Bee Egg]].

===Badge Shivers===
Badge shivers are rewards that are linked to the player's [[Badges]]. The player can shiver a badge when they have the required badges by donating certain items. Shivering a badge does not do anything to the badge itself, only granting certain rewards if you have certain badges. Donating more items at a time gives a higher chance of shivering the related badge. Some badge shivers may require multiple badges or certain tiers of badges. Other badges cannot be shivered, or they are currently unknown. Below is a list of all known badge shivers. Each shiver can only be performed once.

==Donations==
The shrine will grant different rewards depending on the item donated. All donations will grant Honey and favor (both of which increase based on the amount donated), but other effects may vary. A list of possible outcomes is written below.
[[File:Spirit Petal Rewards.png|thumb|The rewards from donating a Spirit Petal to the Wind Shrine.]]
*Summoning clouds on fields and granting field winds.
*Summoning a [[Wild Windy Bee]].
*Summoning [[Bean Bug]]s and granting a [[Jelly Beans|Jelly Bean]].
*Granting badge rewards.
*Enabling the player to receive a [[Windy Bee]] and granting other rewards. 
**This refers to when you donate the [[Spirit Petal]], which can only be done once, which grants 500 [[Treat]]s, 1 [[Star Jelly]], 5 [[Enzymes]], 5 [[Oil]]s, 5 [[Red Extract]]s, 5 [[Blue Extract]]s, 5 [[Tropical Drink]]s and 5 [[Magic Bean]]s.
*Failing to obtain a [[Windy Bee]] and granting [[Treat]]s.
*Succeeding to obtain a [[Windy Bee]].
*Granting [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Areas|Galentine's Blessing]], and other rewards.
*Granting 7 Jelly Beans ([[Ready Player Two]] Event, although still possible as of 2/18/2025).

Certain items are more likely to have certain effects. For example, donating eggs other than Basic Eggs and Honey Bee Eggs will always grant field winds and donating Star Jellies or Field Dice is likely to summon a Wild Windy Bee. Some items also have biases when granting field winds, such as [[Purple Potion]]s being more likely to grant field winds for colored fields. Donating a diamond egg (or blue extracts) has a higher chance of granting winds for blue fields.

'''Keep in mind that the rewards are somewhat random, as offering the same donation twice in the same amount may have a different result. However, more items in the donation can grant better rewards.'''

==Galentine Shrine==
[[File:Galentine Wind Shrine.png|thumb|250px|The Galentine Shrine's appearance.]]
[[File:Buff.png|thumb|The Blessing icon.]]

The Galentine Shrine can be obtained after the player completes [[Spirit Bear]]'s Beesmas 2020, 2021, 2022 or 2024 quests. It is the Wind Shrine decorated specifically for Galentine’s Day. There are five hearts, a large red bow on the top, and a smaller white bow between two hearts. The Wind Shrine's two 'pillars' are also coated in pink paint.

If the player donates {{Cost|Strawberry}}, there is a chance (increased chance when donating more strawberries, with a 100% chance if the player donated 50,000 or more '''at once''') for the player to receive several rewards and the [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Areas|Galentine's Blessing buff]] which lasts for 2 days:

==Trivia == 
* The Wind Shrine is based on a percussion instrument, the wind chimes.
* According to [[Spirit Bear]], the Wind Shrine is one of the oldest standing monuments in the game. She also mentions that it is even older than she is.   
**She also says that the Wind Shrine gives you different responses depending on your favor.
[[File:Error .png|thumb|The pop-up that appears when a player has less than 35 bees and attempts to donate to the Wind Shrine.]]
*Using the Wind Shrine is the only way to obtain a [[Windy Bee]], other than buying it in {{Cost|Robux}} Shop when it was first released.
* If the player donates something, but disconnects before the rewards fade out, the next donation will give the rewards of the last donation and set a cooldown of 1 hour. This will not happen if at least one reward is collected from the Wind Shrine.[[File:Refund.webp|right|thumb|Obtaining 8 oil from a refund.]]
*If a player happens to reach the Wind Shrine without having 35 [[bees]] and tries to donate an item, a message will come up saying "Error: Player does not have 35 bees. Please show Onett. (Version {Server version})".
*{{Cost|Super Smoothie}}, {{Cost|Translator}}, [[Sprinklers|Sprinkler Builders]], certain [[Egg#Specific Bee Egg|Specific Bee Eggs]] (all except for [[Egg|Honey Bee Egg]]), [[Royal Jelly#Specific Bee Jelly Variants|Specific Bee Jellies]], {{Cost|Spirit Petal}} (if the player has already donated one), {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear}}, {{Cost|Snowflake}}, [[Balloon|Balloons]], [[Planter|Planters]], [[Robo Pass|Robo Passes]], the {{Cost|Beequip Case|||0}} and {{Cost|7-Pronged Cog}} are items in the player's inventory that cannot be donated.
*[[File:WindShrineError7.png|thumb|The pop-up that appears when someone tries to donate 0 items.]]When donating 0 of something (e.g. 0 tickets), a message will appear saying "Error: Player does not have selected quantity of items. (Version {Server version})", or "Error: Cannot donate 0 items."[[File:Cannotdonate0.webp|thumb|right]]
*The chimes on the Wind Shrine are collidable, and running into them will cause them to move.
*The Galentine Shrine is a possible reference to the Parks & Recreation episode “Galentines Day”
*According to [[Spirit Bear]], an [[Instant Converter]] was supposed to take the spot of the Wind Shrine.
*Donating any amount of {{Cost|Jelly Beans}} for the first time will always give 7 in return.
*The chimes (especially the right one) can sometimes get stuck on the top of the Wind Shrine. This is uncommon.
