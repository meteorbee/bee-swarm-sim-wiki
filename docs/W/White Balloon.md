The {{Color|White Balloon}} is a [[:Category:Consumables|consumable]] [[Items#Misc. Items|item]] added in the [[Updates|12/26/2021 update]]. Once used, it creates a white balloon over the [[Fields|field]] the player is in, storing [[pollen]] collected underneath it. The pollen collected below it increases by 10%, with white pollen increasing by 20%. It holds up to 10x the player's capacity and floats over the field for 5 minutes before floating to the [[hive]] balloon.

White balloons have a cap of 25 in the player's [[:Category:Inventory|inventory]], and 1 per player in the field.
[[File:White Balloom.webp|thumb|A White Balloon floating above a field.]]

== Ways to Obtain ==
* As a exceptionally rare drop from a [[Spider]].
* As a rare reward after finishing a [[Robo Bear Challenge]].
* Completing certain [[Dapper Bear]] quests:
** Wandering Ambition rewards 1 white balloon and other items.
** The Long Haul rewards 1 white balloon and other items.
** Solitarily Steadfast rewards 3 white balloons and other items.
