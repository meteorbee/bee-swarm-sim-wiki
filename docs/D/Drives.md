# Drives

'''Drives''' are inventory items that were introduced in the [[Updates#2022.12.26|12/26/22 update]]. They can be used to repair [[Robo Bear]], purchase [[Digital Bee]], upgrade Digital Bee, and grant boosts during a round of the [[Robo Bear Challenge]]. All Drives (except Broken Drives) can be purchased from Robo Bear's Shop for {{Cost|Cog|50}} ({{Cost|Cog|100}} for Glitched, and 5 of each other drive), and an increasing amount of {{Cost|Honey}} with each purchase.

== White Drive ==
[[File:White Drive.png|thumb|150x150px]]
The {{Color|White Drive}}, when used, grants the following boosts during a single round of Robo Bear's Challenge:

* x1.25 [[System Page#White Pollen|White Pollen]]
* x1.25 [[System Page#White Field Capacity|White Field Capacity]]
* +1 [[Bee Attack#Colorless Bee Attack|Colorless Bee Attack]]

=== Ways to Obtain: ===
* Purchasing it for {{Cost|Cog|50}} and {{Cost|Honey|250,000}} (Honey price increases each time you buy one with an unknown cap) in [[Robo Bear's Shop]].
* Chance to receive from Robo Bear's Challenge.
* Completing [[Science Bear]]'s "Repairing The White Drive" quest gives {{Cost|White Drive|1}}, replacing the Broken Drive.

== Glitched Drive ==
[[File:Glitched Drive.png|thumb|150px]]
The {{Color|Glitched Drive}}, when used, grants the following boosts during a single round of Robo Bear's Challenge:

* x1.25 [[Pollen]]
* x1.25 [[Field Capacity]]
* +1 [[Bee Attack#Regular Bee Attack|Bee Attack]]
=== Ways to Obtain: ===
* Purchasing it for {{Cost|Cog|100}}, {{Cost|Red Drive|5}}, {{Cost|Blue Drive|5}}, {{Cost|White Drive|5}}, and {{Cost|Honey|1,000,000}} (Honey price increases each time you buy one with an unknown cap) in Robo Bear's Shop. It requires a Gold Cog Amulet to unlock the ability of purchasing it.
* Completing 10 or more rounds of Robo Bear's Challenge has a chance to reward a {{Cost|Glitched Drive|||0}} (Chance increases as the player finished the challenge on a higher round).
* Completing [[Science Bear]]'s "Corrupting The Glitched Drive" quest gives {{Cost|Glitched Drive|1}} (Requires you to complete [[Science Bear]]'s White Drive quest, [[Dapper Bear]]'s Blue Drive quest, and [[Panda Bear]]'s Red Drive quest).

== Broken Drive ==
{{Main|Broken Drive}}
[[File:Broken Drive.png|thumb|113px]]
The {{Color|Broken Drive}} is a unique drive added in the [[Updates#2023.05.07|2023.05.07 update]] that can be collected only once, from an area behind the [[Robo Pass Dispenser]] and [[Free Robo Pass Dispenser]]. Once collected, it initiates Science Bear's drive questline. After completing Science Bear's quest, the Broken Drive will be replaced with a {{Cost|White Drive|||0}}.

==== Ways to Obtain: ====
* Found behind the Robo Pass Dispensers in the [[Bear Gate|30 Bee Zone]].

* Excluding the Broken Drive, these are the only items that can only be used during a challenge.
* The existence of a Yellow Drive is hinted through Robo Bear’s 2022 present dialogue.
