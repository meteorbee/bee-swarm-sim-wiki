# Daily Top Honeymakers

The '''Daily Top Honeymakers''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. This leaderboard shows how much [[honey]] players have made each day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time, with the scroll bar going all the way down to the 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM, CST.

If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message, "<span style="background-color:#3370CC; color:white;">You've made ''(x)'' Honey today</span>", will appear, with x being the number of honey you made since the last daily reset.

==Location==
It is located close to the [[All-Time Top Honeymakers]] leaderboard, next to the [[Basic Egg Shop]] and in front of the [[Sunflower Field]].

==Prizes==
If the player has reached the top 25 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with either the {{Cost|Right Gold Swirl Fleuron Sticker|4=0}} or the {{Cost|Left Gold Swirl Fleuron Sticker|4=0}}.
