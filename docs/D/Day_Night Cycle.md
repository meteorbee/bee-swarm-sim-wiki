# Day/Night Cycle

The '''Day/Night Cycle''' is a feature added in the [[Updates#2018.09.10|9/10/2018 update]]. Before the update, it was always daytime.

==Day==
[[File:RobloxPlayerBeta 2018-09-15 16-34-53.png|thumb|250px|The game during daytime.]]
Daytime lasts from around 10 to 30 minutes (for an average of 20 minutes). All [[music]] plays as normal. This is the default setting for the game.

==Night==
[[File:RobloxPlayerBeta 2018-09-24 18-02-11.png|thumb|250px|The game during nighttime.]]
When the day ends, the sun goes down quickly, and the sky shifts to black, indicating that it is nighttime. During this cycle, all outdoor music is replaced by [[Music#mountaincall|mountaincall]]. Entering any area that plays the [[Music#vendor|vendor]], [[Music#crawlers|crawlers]], [[Music#gbtune1|gbtune1]], [[Music#hibernation1|hibernation1]], [[Music#starhall|starhall]] music or music playing during an [[Ant Challenge|Ant]], [[Robo Bear Challenge|Robo]] or [[Stick Bug Challenge]] will override the night music. [[Fireflies]] will also appear, stopping in different [[fields]]. The background of the server and walls will turn pitch black. A [[Rogue Vicious Bee]] has a higher chance of spawning every night. A moon sprout also has a 60% chance of spawning every night. 

Walking near a firefly will make it fly into the air and leave a 1x1 patch of [[sparkles]]. Once a player has removed all the fireflies, one of the tokens below will appear in the middle of their formation:
*{{Cost|Moon Charm}} (increments of 1, 5, or 10).
*{{Cost|Glitter|4=0}} (Very rare).
*{{Cost|Star Jelly|4=0}} (Extremely rare).
*{{Cost|Night Bell|4=0}} (Extremely rare).
*{{Cost|Star Treat|4=0}} (Exceptionally rare).
*{{Cost|Waxing Crescent Moon Sticker|||0}} (Rare)
*{{Cost|Glowing Smile Sticker|||0}} (Extremely Rare)
*{{I|hivesticker aries star sign}}[[Sticker#Sticker Index|Star Signs]] (Extremely Rare)
*{{Cost|Cyan Star Sticker|||0}} (Exceptionally Rare)
*{{Cost|Shining Star Sticker|||0}} (Exceptionally Rare)

Fireflies can only stop in the same field three times. After removing all the fireflies in a field three times, they will fly to a different field. Fireflies will only stop in the [[Rose Field]], [[Cactus Field]], [[Pineapple Patch]], [[Spider Field]], [[Strawberry Field]] and [[Bamboo Field]].

Night lasts 2 minutes 35 seconds, which is the duration of the night theme (mountaincall). Ever since the [[Updates#2018.11.25|11/25/2018]] and [[Updates#2018.12.19|12/19/2018]] updates, moon-shaped platforms leading to the [[Diamond Mask]] and the platforms leading to [[Bubble Bee Man]] and the [[Memory Match|Night Memory Match]] are solid yellow only during nighttime if the player has a [[Moon Amulet]] (if the player owns the Diamond Mask, the platforms will be blue and stay permanently solid). When [[sprout|sprouts]] are planted at night, there is a 60% chance for it to be a moon sprout, with the odds of all other sprout types being altered (Supreme Sprouts become more common while the rest are rarer).

==Trivia==
*If the Stick Bug Challenge ends at night, the normal music will play instead of the night music. If the time cycles from night to day or day to night while a [[Honeystorm]] or [[Mythic Meteor Shower|mythic meteor shower]] is active, the fog will disappear, but the honeystorm or mythic meteor shower will still be active.
*In some occasions, the nighttime OST doesn't have to end for it to be daytime, it just simply fades.
*A {{Cost|Night Bell|4=0}} and [[Spirit Bear]] can manually summon nighttime.
**[[Onett (Developer)|Onett]] can also summon nighttime, but does it globally and with a message (ex: "🌙 One of [[Mother Bear]]'s cubs has summoned nighttime... 🌙").
***A full gallery of these messages can be found in the {{Cost|Night Bell|4=0}} page.
