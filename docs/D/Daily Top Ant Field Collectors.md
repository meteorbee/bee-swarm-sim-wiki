# Daily Top Ant Field Collectors

{{RemovedContent}}
The '''Daily Top Ant Field Collectors''' was, at the time, one of the 62 [[leaderboards]] in the game. Unlike other leaderboards, it did not appear in the [[System Page]], nor anywhere else in-game. This leaderboard measured how much [[pollen]] players had collected in the [[Ant Field]] that day. It reset every day at 12:00 AM CST.

==Prizes==
If the player had reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they would be awarded with the {{Cost|Ant Field Stamp Sticker|4=0}}.

==Trivia==
* This was the only leaderboard not to be displayed anywhere in the game.
* This was the fourth leaderboard to be removed, the rest being the [[Most Commando Captures]], [[Highest Snowbear Level]] and [[Highest Robo Party Cake Rank]] leaderboard.
* This leaderboard shares a reward with the [[Top Ant Exterminators]] leaderboard.
** These are the only leaderboards to have shared rewards.
