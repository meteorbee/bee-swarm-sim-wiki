# Daily Fastest Crab Slayers

The '''Daily Fastest Crab Slayers''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game, and is only viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the [[System Page]]. This leaderboard shows players' fastest time to defeat the [[Coconut Crab]] on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM CST.

==Prizes==
If the player has reached the top 5 by the end of the day, they will be awarded the {{Cost|Cyan Hilted Sword Sticker|4=0}}.

If the player has reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with {{Cost|Ticket|25}}.

==Trivia==
*The leaderboard has a glitch where the leaderboard inputs the players' best recorded time of slaying the crab even if your kill on the Coconut Crab is lower than your best.
