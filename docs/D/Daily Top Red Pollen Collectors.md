# Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors

The '''Daily Top Red Pollen Collectors Leaderboard''' is one of {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. This leaderboard shows how much red [[pollen]] players have collected on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM, CST.

If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message <span style="background-color:#0090BF; color:white;">"You've collected "X" Red Pollen today (Resets in "Y")"</span> will appear.

==Location==
It is located inside the Red HQ, between the ladder and the [[Red Field Booster]].

== Prizes ==
If the player has reached the top 25 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with either the '''Left Mythic Gem Fleuron [[Sticker]]''' or the '''Right Mythic Gem Fleuron Sticker'''.

If the player has reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with {{Cost|Ticket|25}}.
