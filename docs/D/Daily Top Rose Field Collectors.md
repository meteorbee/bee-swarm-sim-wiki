# Daily Top Rose Field Collectors

The '''Daily Top Rose Field Collectors''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game, and is only viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the [[System Page]]. This leaderboard shows how much [[pollen]] players have collected in the [[Rose Field]] on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM CST.

==Prizes==
For being in the Top 25 when the leaderboard resets, you will be awarded with the {{Cost|Rose Field Stamp Sticker|4=0}}.

For being in the Top 100 when the leaderboard resets, you will be awarded with {{Cost|Ticket|25}}.
