# Daily Top Bomb Pollen Collectors

The '''Daily Top Bomb Pollen Collectors''' was, at the time, one of the 62 [[leaderboards]] in the game, and was only viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the [[System Page]]. This leaderboard showed how much [[Pollen]] players had collected with only [[Ability Tokens#Bomb|Bomb Abilities]] on that day. It showed 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player could scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. It reset every day at 12:00 AM CST.

==Prizes==
For being in the Top 100 when the leaderboard reset, you would've been awarded with the '''Bomber Bee Bear [[Sticker]]'''.
