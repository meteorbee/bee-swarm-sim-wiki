'''Dapper Bear''' is a [[Quest Givers|Quest Giver]], and one of the eight permanent quest [[Bears]] that can be accessed in the game, the others being [[Black Bear]], [[Brown Bear]], [[Mother Bear]], [[Panda Bear]], [[Science Bear]], [[Polar Bear]] and [[Spirit Bear]]. Dapper Bear is located inside his [[Dapper Bear’s Shop|shop]] next to [[Stump Field]].  

Inside Dapper Bear's shop, he sells many different [[planter]]s ranging from{{Cost|Honey|750K-100T}} (alongside [[crafting]] materials). He also sells many different types of [[Beequip]]s for {{Cost|Honey}} and {{Cost|Ticket}}, with some being locked behind a certain amount of Dapper Bear Quests completed.

After speaking with him, Dapper Bear rewards the player {{Cost|Ticket Planter|3}} and a {{Cost|Beequip Case|||0}} (if the player does not already have one). 

==Requirements==
You need the following Accessories to enter Dapper Bear's shop, those being the [[Hiking Boots]] and the [[Propeller Hat]].  Players can also use gear that is better than the ones previously listed.

==Quests==
Dapper Bear currently has 16 Quests. His 15 main quests focus on collecting nectar, collecting tokens from planters, and popping [[Puffshroom|Puffshrooms]], and the one quest is for obtaining the {{Cost|Blue Drive|4=1}} in order to repair [[Robo Bear]].

His 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th, 12th and 14th Quest rewards an additional <small>{{Cost|Beequip Case|||0}}</small> slot. If you received <small>{{Cost|Beequip Case|||0}}</small> slots from Bee Bear previously, you will instead be rewarded one {{Cost|Ticket Planter}} until you catch up to the amount of additional slots a new player would have.

==Dialogue==
Currently, Dapper Bear has 15 quests that were added in [[Beesmas|Beesmas 2022]]. However, more quests for him will be added upon further updates. Upon interacting with him, he will first give dialogue explaining [[Planter|Planters]] and a reward of {{Cost|Ticket Planter|||}}. Since the Beesmas 2022 update, a questline has been given to him. Dapper Bear gives plenty of information in his dialogues. Some of the information he gives is not found anywhere else in the game.

== Trivia==
*Despite Dapper Bear's appearance, he is male.
*Dapper Bear is shorter than every other bear in the game. This is referenced in his dialogue and is a key detail in the {{Cost|Tacky Planter|||}} chain that rewards the {{Cost|Dapper From Above Sticker|4=0}}.
*Dapper Bear, [[Gummy Bear]], [[Tunnel Bear]], [[Noob Bear]], [[Pro Bear]], [[Top Bear]], and [[Shadow Bear]] are the only bears to reside indoors.
** Dapper Bear and Gummy Bear are the only quest bears to reside indoors.    
*** Dapper Bear is the only permanent quest bear to reside indoors.
*This  bear, [[Gummy Bear]], [[Robo Bear]] and [[Bee Bear]] are the only bears to own a [[:Category:Shops|shop]].
**This bear and [[Bee Bear]] are the only quest bears to own a [[:Category:Shops|shop]].  
**This bear and [[Robo Bear]] are the only permanent bears to own a [[:Category:Shops|shop]].
***Dapper Bear is also the only permanent quest bear to own a shop.
*Like [[Mother Bear]], Dapper Bear uses the [https://www.roblox.com/bundles/83/Stylish-Animation-Pack Stylish Animation Pack].
*Like Mother Bear and [[Gummy Bee]], Dapper Bear's face emits light. This is most noticeable if the player looks at Dapper Bear at [[Day/Night Cycle|night]].
*He is the only permanent quest giver whose first quest was a seasonal one.
* Dapper Bear may have a possible Russian background. When the player completes his [[Samovar]] quest (the Samovar is also of Russian origins), Dapper Bear mentions that he made room for some beequips and the Samovar on his travel back to Moscow.
* In Beesmas Winter 2024, one of the requirements for his samovar quest was “Collect 25 tokens from planters in the Pineapple Patch” However, it was broken and no tokens collected in Pineapple Patch would count towards the quest. This was later fixed by setting the requirement to finished for all players.
* Dapper Bear, [[Sun Bear]], and [[Spirit Bear]] are the only bears to wear 3D accessories.  
**Of the three, Dapper Bear is the only one to wear accessories that the player can't use.
