# Daily Top Aphid Exterminators

The '''Daily Top Aphid Exterminators''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game, and is only viewable via the Global Leaderboards menu in the [[System Page]]. This leaderboard shows the the number of [[aphid]]s players have killed on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM CST.

==Prizes==
If the player has reached the top 5 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with the {{Cost|Wavy Cyan Hive Skin|4=0}}.

If the player has reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with {{Cost|Ticket|50}}.
