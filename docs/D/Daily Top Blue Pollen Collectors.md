# Daily Top Blue Pollen Collectors

The '''Daily Top Blue Pollen Collectors Leaderboard''' is one of {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. It is located inside of the Blue HQ, to the right of the [[All-Time Top Blue Collectors]] Leaderboard. This leaderboard shows much blue [[pollen]] players have collected on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM, CST.

If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message <span style="background-color:#0090BF; color:white;">"You've collected "X" Blue Pollen today (Resets in "Y")"</span> will appear.

== Location ==
It is located on the second floor of the [[Blue HQ]], to the right of [[Gifted Bucko Bee]].

==Prizes==
If the player has reached the top 25 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with the '''Left Shining Diamond Fleuron [[Sticker]]''' or the '''Right Shining Diamond Fleuron Sticker'''.

If the player has reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with {{Cost|Ticket|25}}.
