# Daily Top White Pollen Collectors

The '''Daily Top White Pollen Collectors Leaderboard''' is one of {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. This leaderboard shows how much white [[pollen]] players have collected on that day. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to 100th place. It resets every day at 12:00 AM CST.

If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message <span style="background-color:#0090BF; color:white;">"You've collected "X" White Pollen today "</span> will appear.

==Location==
It is located inside of the [[Petal Shop]], to the right of the [[All-Time Top White Collectors]] Leaderboard.

==Prizes==
If the player has reached the top 10 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with the '''Basic White [[Sticker|Hive Skin]].'''

If the player has reached the top 100 by the end of the day, they will be awarded with {{Cost|Ticket|25}}.
