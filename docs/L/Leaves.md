# Leaves

<sub>''Not to be confused with [[Flowers]] or [[Pollen]].''</sub>
[[File:Leaf.png|thumb|A leaf.]]
'''Leaves''' are entities that appear in most [[fields]]. It works similarly to [[sparkles]] and emits leaf particles. To get a reward from leaves, the player needs to collect [[pollen]] on the tile it is on, similar to sparkles. If the leaves are collected upon, a rustling sound plays, and multiple leaf particles fly out before the token appears. Rewards vary depending on which field the leaves are in but will always be in the quantity of 1. For example, leaves will spawn blueberries in blue fields. Leaves also have a chance of spawning an [[aphid]] based on field activity, with a higher chance to spawn if in a field with lower activity, they can also summon leaf [[Sticker#Sticker Index|stickers]] in most fields. 

Because the [[Mountain Top Field]] cannot naturally spawn treats, leaves in the Mountain Top Field are very rare and will always spawn an [[aphid]], [[Bitterberry]], [[Neonberry]], or [[Magic Bean]] token. The [[Stump Field]] and the [[Ant Field]] cannot spawn Aphids.
