== Trivia ==
* This is one of eight beequips to give a negative stat.
** The other seven are [[Kazoo]], [[Whistle]], [[Beesmas Top]], [[Toy Horn]], [[Demon Talisman]], [[Pink Eraser]] and [[Toy Drum]].
** This is the only beequip to give two negative stats.
* The Lump Of Coal, tied with the [[Charm Bracelet]], has the most possible hive bonuses out of any Beequip, at eight.
