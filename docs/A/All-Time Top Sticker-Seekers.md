The '''Top Sticker-Seekers''' was added in the 01/13/2024 [[Updates|update]] and is one of {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. This leaderboard shows how many [[Sticker-Seeker Quest Machine|Sticker-Seeker Quests]] players have completed. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place.

== Location ==
It is located inside of the [[Hive Hub]] next to the [[Hub Field Shop]].

== Achievements ==
* XSxriptX was the first player to reach 25 Sticker-Seeker quests completed.
* NoahvsZombie was the first player to reach 50 Sticker-Seeker quests completed.
* MyloDaBoss was the first player to reach 75 Sticker-Seeker quests completed.
* piterozero20001 was the first player to reach 100 Sticker-Seeker quests completed.
* TorchyGem was the first player to reach 500 Sticker-Seeker quests completed.

== Trivia ==
*The Top Sticker-Seekers and the [[All-Time Tallest Sticker Stack]] are the only leaderboards in the game to have a maximum amount, being 579 as of right now, meaning that the leaderboard will eventually become impossible to get on to.
** This is because there are only 288 different stickers in-game and Rank 580 requires 290 stickers stacked, making it impossible to complete.
* This is the only leaderboard located inside of the Hive Hub.
