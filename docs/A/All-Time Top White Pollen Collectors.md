The '''All-Time Top White Pollen Collectors''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game, and one of the 20 leaderboards with an in-game model. This leaderboard shows how much white [[pollen]] players have collected over time. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on the leaderboards for lengths of time (hackers/exploiters will be removed).         

If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message <span style="background-color:#0090BF; color:white;">"You've collected "x" White Pollen"</span> will appear. "x" being the amount of white pollen you have collected.

== Location ==
It is located inside the 35 Bee zone on the left wall of the [[Petal Shop]], near the entrance.

== Achievements ==

* MrMocito was the first user to reach 10 trillion total white pollen collected.
* MrMocito was the first user to reach 100 trillion total white pollen collected.
* MrMocito was the first user to reach 1 quadrillion total white pollen collected.
