The '''Global Top Ant Exterminators Leaderboard''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. This leaderboard shows the top [[Ant Challenge]] scores of players. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at one time. However, the player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more scores of other players, all the way down to the 100th place.

If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message <span style="background-color:#0090BF; color:white;">"Your Highscore: X"</span> will appear. (X = Your Highscore)

The structure conceals a secret passage containing an [[Ant Pass|ant pass]], and the [[Gummy Bee Egg Claim]].

== Location ==
It is located in front of the Ant Challenge room near the [[Ant Pass Dispenser]] and the [[Free Ant Pass Dispenser]]s.

== Achievements ==
* On 8/1/2018, SDMittens was the first user to reach 200 Ant Exterminations.
* On 12/26/2018, SDMittens was the first user to reach 300 Ant Exterminations.
* On 12/14/2019, Lipsisas was the first user to reach 400 Ant Exterminations.
* On 1/10/2020, Zlib was the first user to reach 500 Ant Exterminations.

==Trivia==
*When the [[Updates#2018.11.25|11/25/2018 update]] came out, all of the scores on this leaderboard had been reset.
*The lowest score on the leaderboard is 530 points, which was achieved by the players from 67th to 100th place.
*On 01/12/2024, many players on the leaderboard, including Natmab, lost their Top Ant Exterminator points highscore. The reason behind the data wipe is currently unknown.
