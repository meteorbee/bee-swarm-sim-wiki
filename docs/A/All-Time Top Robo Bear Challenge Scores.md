The '''Highest Robo Bear Challenge Scores Leaderboard''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. This leaderboard shows the top 100 people who've earned the highest scores in the [[Robo Bear Challenge]], showing ten ranks at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place.

If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message <span style="background-color:#0090BF; color:white;">"Your highest score in Robo Bear's Challenge: {Player's High Score}"</span> will appear.

==Location==
It is located behind [[Robo Bear]] to the left of his [[Robo Bear's Shop|shop]].

== Trivia ==
*This and the [[Highest Damage to a Single Puffshroom]] leaderboard are the only ones with a different background frame color other than brown.
** Additionally, this is the only leaderboard to not have a frame made out of wood.
