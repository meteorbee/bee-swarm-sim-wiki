The '''All-Time Top Honeymakers''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. This leaderboard shows how much [[honey]] players have collected over time. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at one time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on the leaderboards for lengths of time (exploiters will be removed).

If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message <span style="background-color:#0090BF; color:white;">"You've made [x] Honey"</span> will appear; x being the amount of honey the player has made in total.

==Location==
It is located near the [[hive]]s and on the opposite side of the fence to the [[Dandelion Field]]. It is on the opposite side of the path in front of the hives from the [[Daily Top Honeymakers]] leaderboard.

==History== 

*SDMittens was #1 for over 11 months before being surpassed by Gumaden on 6/11/2019, meaning she had the record of being #1 the longest. Gumaden surpassed her due to SDMittens playing other games besides [[Bee Swarm Simulator]].
**This record was broken by e_lol, he had been #1 for 1 year, 10 months, and 4 days.
*Gumaden was #1 for only roughly 1–3 hours before being surpassed by zlib. But then a few days later, Gumaden took back the #1 spot.
**Gumaden kept that spot for over 6 months before once again being passed by zlib.
**zlib kept the #1 spot for just under 1 month before being surpassed by Gumaden.
*On 12/31/2019, zlib broke the world record of most honey in a day, making over 8 trillion honey (excluding exploiters).
**On 1/9/2020, he broke that record, as he made over 10 trillion honey in a single day.
**On 8/22/2020, IcedMizu made 28 trillion honey in a single day.
**On 8/26/2020, e_lolYouTube made 116 trillion honey in a single day.
**On 2/17/2021, Gifted_Ty made 383 trillion honey in a single day (he was later removed from the leaderboard for abusing glitches).
**On 2/27/2022, OnettsStepSis made 1 Quadrillion honey in a single day.
**On 10/05/2022, FragileSilence made 2 Quadrillion honey in a single day.
*On 2/27/2021, Gifted_Ty was blocked from the All-Time Top Honeymakers leaderboard due to glitch abusing.
**This put e_lolYouTube in first place, riaz1987 in 2nd place and MrMocito in 3rd place at the time.
*On 3/01/2022, e_lolYouTube hit 10 Quadrillion honey, but the leaderboard reached its limits of displaying more than 10 quadrillion. It was later fixed on 3/03/2022.
*On 3/03/2022, A player named "moivnvodich" made 2.5 quintillion honey through exploiting.
*On 3/04/2022, User "moivnvodich" got their data resetted and was banned from BSS. In an interview with the 2 users behind the "moivnvodich" account, they revealed that they had modified the old infinite honey exploit (involving the wind shrine), in order to not get leaderboard banned. The reason they didn't show up prior to 3/04/22 is because leader boards previously didn't support numbers with more than 17 digits (10 quadrillion).
*On 3/11/2022, Three players named "123404az", "ProSupHeo" and "Grete_HD" made a large amount of honey through exploiting, with 123404az making 9.2 Quintillion honey, ProSupHeo making 2.4 Quintillion honey and Grete_HD making 550 Quadrillion honey.
**Interestingly, 9.2 Quintillion honey is the 64-bit integer limit.
*On 3/13/2022, Six players appeared on the leaderboard, with honey amounts ranging from 22 quadrillion honey to 9 Quintillion honey. Interestingly, three of said exploiters had the exact same amount of honey, at around 9.2 quintillion.
*On 3/14/2022, Three players appeared on the leaderboard, with honey amounts of 60 Quadrillion, 1 Quintillion and 2.28 Quintillion (when rounded off). They exploited their way through the leaderboard, and were removed the next day.
*On 12/29/2022, BuyKocmoc, GRANNY5556665, and six other players went up the leaderboard by exploiting with honey amounts of 175 Quadrillion, 55 Quadrillion, and other amounts. They were later removed and then Onett went on #1 of the leaderboard.
*On 3/29/2023, e_lol is the first player to be #1 for an entire year.
*On 12/20/2023, e_lol hit 100 quadrillion honey for the first time.
* On 1/12/2024, due to the honey being gathered during a round in Robo Bear Challenge counted in general honeymaking. Every player's honey made during the Robo Bear Challenge was fixed and the total honey displayed in the leaderboard was reduced by the calculated amount of honey made in all of the players' Robo Bear Challenge sessions.

* On 01/31/2024, e_lol hit 100 quadrillion honey for the first time after losing several quadrillions of honey 19 days prior.
* On 2/01/2024, FragileSilence passed e_lol. That put him in 1st place on the leaderboard.

==Trivia== 
*This leaderboard and the Daily Top Honeymakers leaderboard were the only leaderboards to have abbreviated numbers before the 1/12/2024 update.
*This leaderboard is one of two leaderboards to be in the game since its release date, the other one is the [[Global Top Battlers (Most Battle Points)|Global Top Battlers]] leaderboard.
