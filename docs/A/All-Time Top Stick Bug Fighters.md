The '''Global Top Stick Bug Fighters Leaderboard''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. It can display 10 leaderboard positions at a time 
If the player stands on the white pad in front of the leaderboard, <span style="background-color:#0090BF; color:white;">"Your highscore: "..ScoreVariable..""</span> "ScoreVariable" being the score obtained from the [[Stick Bug Challenge]] This allows for players to view their current Highscore for the Stick Bug Challenge. This leaderboard tracks the top 100 scores from fighting [[Stick Bug]] in the Stick Bug Challenge. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place.

The Top Stick Bug Fighters leaderboard was reset at one point due to glitches with the fight. The glitches were fixed on 9/27/19.

It was later reset again on 12/26/2022.
[[File:Stickbug and Stickbug Leaderboard.png|thumb|Stick Bug next to the Top Stick Bug Fighters leaderboard.]]

The leaderboard was recently reset once more in 1/12/2024 due to the technique of using attack buffs in Robo Bear's Challenge to increase damage drastically.

==Location==
It is located right behind Stick Bug, on top of the biggest mushroom in the [[Mushroom Field]]. One method to get there is to use the [[Red Cannon|Red]], [[Blue Cannon|Blue]], or [[Yellow Cannon]]s paired with either the [[Parachute]] or [[Glider]]. Another way to get there is to parachute or glide from a tall place, like near the [[Lion Bee Gate]]. Doing the [[Obstacle Courses#Mushroom Field Obby|Mushroom Field Obby]] and jumping and gliding to the tallest mushroom is also a viable solution.

==Achievements==
*zlib was the first player to reach a score of 100 million or more.
*Rara0w0x3 was the first player to reach a score of 1 billion or more.

== Trivia ==
* This leaderboard, the [[Highest Snowbear Level]] leaderboard, and the [[Fastest Crab Slayers]] leaderboard are the only three leaderboards to rank "[[:Category:Bosses|bosses]]".
* After the leaderboard got reset again in 1/12/2024, the score displayed when stepping on the podium is your score before the reset. But it does not display in the leaderboard.
