An {{Color|Aged Gingerbread Bear}} is a type of [[Treats|treat]] that was added in the [[Updates#2021.12.26|12/26/2021 update]]. When fed to a bee, it raises its [[bond]] by 500 and has a 1.1% chance of turning the bee gifted. Unused {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear}} would transform into aged gingerbread bears after the next [[Beesmas]] has started. These cannot be used to purchase items from [[Bee Bear's Catalog]]. 

The formula for the chance of turning a bee gifted is as follows: <math>([1 - 0.989^x]* 100)\%</math>, where x is the number of {{Cost|Aged Gingerbread Bear}} fed to a bee.

== Ways to Obtain ==
* The only intended way to obtain Aged Gingerbread Bears is by saving {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear}} until the start of the next Beesmas.

=== Outdated Ways ===
*Collecting the Gingerbread Bear token hidden next to the cave in the Retro Swarm Challenge sometime after Beesmas Winter 2024 began.

== Trivia ==
* This, along with {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear}} and {{Cost|Star Treat}}, are the only items that can turn [[Bees/Event|Event bees]] gifted (as they lack favorite treats.)
* {{Cost|Aged Gingerbread Bear}} do not apply for quests that ask for the player to feed {{Cost|Gingerbread Bear}} to their bees, such as [[Bee Bear|Bee Bear's]] "Snow Cub Reformation (2/5)" quest.
* If you got the free Gingerbread Bear from the Retro Swarm Challenge before Winter Beesmas 2024, even if it wasn’t a year before, when Beesmas started, that Gingerbread Bear immediately turned into an Aged one.
* Feeding 100 Aged Gingerbread Bears to a bee does not guarantee to turn it gifted. The chance of it becoming gifted is about 67%.
