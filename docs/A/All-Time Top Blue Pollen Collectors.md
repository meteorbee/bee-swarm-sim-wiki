The '''All-Time Top Blue Pollen Collectors Leaderboard''' is one of {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. This leaderboard shows how much blue [[pollen]] players have collected over time. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on the leaderboards for lengths of time (hackers/exploiters will be removed).

If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message <span style="background-color:#0090BF;color:white">"You've collected "x" Blue Pollen"</span> will appear, "x" being the amount of blue pollen the player has collected.

== Location  ==
It is located on the first floor of the [[Blue HQ]], next to the ramp to the second floor.
