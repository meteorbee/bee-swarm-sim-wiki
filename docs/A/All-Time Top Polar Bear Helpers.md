The '''All-Time Top Polar Bear Helpers''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game, and one of the 41 leaderboards without an in-game model. This leaderboard shows how much [[Polar Bear]] [[Quests]] players have completed over time. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more players, all the way to the 100th place.
