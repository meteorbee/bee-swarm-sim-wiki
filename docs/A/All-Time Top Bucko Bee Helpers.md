The '''All-Time Top Bucko Bee Helpers''' is one of the {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. It displays the top 100 people who've completed the most [[quests]] for Gifted Bucko Bee. It can display 10 leaderboard positions at a time. If the player stands on the white circle, they can see how many Gifted Bucko Bee quests that they have completed.

==Location==
It is located on the second floor of the [[Blue HQ]] next to [[Gifted Bucko Bee]].

==Achievements==

* imnotthinksfanser was the first user to reach 1,000 Gifted Bucko Bee quests completed.
* gfdftggv was the first user to reach 2,000 Gifted Bucko Bee quests completed.
* nickname9999999999 was the first user to reach 3,000 and 4,000 Gifted Bucko Bee quests completed.
