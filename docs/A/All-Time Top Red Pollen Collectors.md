The '''All-Time Top Red Pollen Collectors Leaderboard''' is one of {{WikiVariables|LeaderboardCount}} [[leaderboards]] in the game. This leaderboard shows how much [[:Category:Red|red]] [[pollen]] players have collected over time. It shows 10 leaderboard positions at a time. The player can scroll down the leaderboard to see more leaderboard positions, all the way to 100th place. Only legitimate players appear on the leaderboards for lengths of time (hackers/exploiters will be removed).

If the player steps on the white pad in front of it, the message <span style="background-color:#0090BF; color:white;">"You've collected "x" Red Pollen"</span> will appear, "x" being the total amount of red pollen you have collected in total.

==Location==
It is located inside the [[Red HQ]], between the ladder and the [[Dark Scythe]].
