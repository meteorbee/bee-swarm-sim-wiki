# Atomic Treat

[[File:Atomic Treat Token.png|thumb|An atomic treat token.|120px]]
An {{Color|Atomic Treat}}, when fed to a [[Bees|bee]], raises its [[bond]] by 1000 and gives the bee a random [[mutation]]. The mutations are [[Bee Attack|Attack]], [[System Page#Convert Amount|Convert Amount]], [[System Page#Gather Total|Gather Amount]], [[Energy]], and [[System Page#Bee Ability Rate|Bee Ability Rate]]. These are not the favorite treat of any bee.

== Ways to obtain ==
*As a very rare drop from defeating the [[Coconut Crab]].
*As a rare reward from the [[Robo Bear Challenge]].
*As a rare drop from [[Sprout#Debug Sprout|Debug Sprouts]].
*As a very rare drop from a [[Mythic Meteor Shower|Mythic Meteor]].
=== Quests ===
* Brown Bear rewards atomic treats on the completion of these quests:  
** Brown Bear's 125th quest rewards an Atomic Treat.
** Brown Bear's 225th quest rewards an Atomic Treat.
** Brown Bear's 375th quest rewards an Atomic Treat.
* Completing Science Bear's "Repairing the White Drive" quest.
