    Ability Tokens

    [[File:AbilityTokens.png|thumb|300px|An array of Ability Tokens,]] '''Ability Tokens''' are tokens generated by [[bees]], which can be collected to activate special abilities and buffs. Almost all bees spawn ability tokens, with the exceptions being ungifted [[Basic Bee]] and [[Brave Bee]] without [[Beequip]]s. If the player doesn't collect a token within a certain amount of time, it fades away. Players can't collect the tokens generated by other players' bees and vice versa, as the tokens are client sided. Prior to the [[Updates#2018.05.07|May 7th, 2018 update]], other players' tokens were visible, but still couldn't be collected by others.

    [[File:Focus Bee token.gif|thumb|A [[Looker Bee]] generating a focus token.]] Ability tokens can be generated in 3 situations: '''Gathering:''' Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are collecting [[pollen]]. Most ability tokens that can be generated under this situation can collect pollen and/or boost pollen collection. '''Combat:''' Ability tokens that can be generated when the bees are targeting/attacking [[Mobs|mob]]. Most ability tokens that can be generated under this situation can inflict damage or [[Buffs & Debuffs|debuffs]] to enemies and boost bees' combat process. *'''Idle:''' Ability tokens that can be generated when neither the bees are collecting pollen or in combat situation. Some ability tokens can be generated under 2 or 3 different situations. Gathering and combat situations are known as "active" situations.

    Some tokens aren't generated by the player's bees (such as honey tokens from flowers). The player can tell apart the two types of tokens by the fact that tokens dropped by bees give off gold sparkles, while tokens generated through other means do not sparkle. Also, tokens dropped by someone else's bees are not visible to the player, which means every visible token is collectible (although there is a bug where some loot tokens cannot be collected, usually at the end of a Honeystorm or Snowstorm. Check the [[Glitches]] page for more information).

    Each ability token has a use cooldown, a trial cooldown and a trial success rate. When a bee reaches a flower, it checks if its use cooldown for each token is off. When its use cooldown for a token is off, the bee will check if its trial cooldown for the token is off every time it reaches a flower and attempts to summon the token if it can. If it successfully summons the token, it goes back into its use cooldown.

    A list of ability tokens and their cooldowns can be found here: [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1hj235xRqhqGlKhkUYtoxloC5aAgJDRqMpQgpjtBndBQ/edit?usp=sharing Ability Token Spreadsheet]

    TOC ==Ability Token Stats== These are stats which affect the characteristics of ability tokens. ===Bee Ability Rate=== Bee Ability Rate determines the increment or decrement of use cooldown and trial cooldown of bees' ability tokens, by dividing the original cooldown and trial cooldown, and will also increment the trial success rate. For example: a 200% bee ability rate will reduce the cooldown to 50% of the original cooldown (100% ÷ 200%), and will increase the success rate by 100% (or double it).

It can be boosted by a [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]] [[Stubborn Bee]] and [[Cog Amulet]] and by the [[Paper Angel]] and [[Paperclip]] beequips. The maximum amount currently possible is 144%.

    ===Ability Duplication Chance=== [[File:Dupe token.gif|thumb|An ability token being duplicated and that token being collected]] When there is a chance, ability tokens will spawn its duplicated/duped variants randomly on a field. Duped tokens hover over the field and last for double the tokens' lifespan, and that lifespan is further increased by 5% per [[Digital Bee|Digital Bee's]] level. Duped tokens can be collected/activated by standing beneath it for 1 second.

    Ability duplication chance can be increased by [[Buffs & Debuffs|field corruption]] from Digital Bee's [[#Glitch|Glitch]] and [[#Map Corruption|Map Corruption]] ability tokens. It also can be be increased from [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]] Digital Bee. Other than from Digital Bees, ability duplication chance can also be increased by the 'Corrupt' upgrade purchasable from the [[Robo Bear Challenge]] and only applies during that challenge. It can also be provided by [[Science Bear]] during his "Corrupting The Glitched Drive" quest.
