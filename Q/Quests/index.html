    Quests

    {{Stub}}{{Update}} [[File:QuestMenuIcon.png|thumb|85x85px|The icon for the Quest Menu.]] '''Quests''' are a major [[Mechanics|mechanic]] in [[Bee Swarm Simulator]]. It allows the player to do tasks in order to receive various rewards that allow them to progress further into the game. Quests are not necessary for progression, but are highly recommended. There are a few exceptions, such as [[Science Bear]]'s [[Science Bear#Translator Quest List (15)|Translator]] questline, and [[Spirit Bear]]'s questline, which are required to complete to progress.

    Quests can be obtained and turned in through conversing with various [[Quest Givers|quest givers]]. As of now, 8 active quest [[bears]], 3 [[:Category:Quest Bees|quest bees]], and [[Onett]] can give out quests. [[:Category:Traveling Bears|Traveling bears]] and other various creatures also give quests but were only available for a limited time in the game.

    Quests have many different types of requirements, such as collecting various amounts of [[pollen]] from [[fields]], [[Ability Tokens|ability tokens]], [[goo]], [[Treats|treat]] tokens, defeating [[mobs]], crafting [[items]], obtaining [[badges]], finding [[Stickers]], and discovering [[Bees|bee]] types.

    ==Audio== The following audio plays when the player has received a quest: [[File:Queststart.ogg]]

    The following audio plays when the player has completed and handed in a quest: [[File:Questend.ogg]]

    The following audio plays when the player has just completed a quest or when the player joins a game with a completed quest not handed in: [[File:Ring.ogg]]

    ==Quest Bears== Quest Bears are quest givers that have an appearance of a bear.
