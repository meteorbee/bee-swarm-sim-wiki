    Dapper Bear

    {{Bear|image =DapperBearHD.png |type = Quest Bear |location = [[Dapper Bear’s Shop]] |bees = 10 |basecoloramount=#92735e |basecolor1 =#92735e |shadecoloramount =#947a5f |shadecolor1 =#947a5f |snoutcoloramount =#282623 |snoutcolor1 =#3e3835 |snoutcolor2 =#282623 |torsocolor1 =#8f4e3e |torsocoloramount =#8f4e3e |armscolor =#2b344c |legscolor =#555653 |enableother = no |basecolor2 = #5d5e5b}} '''Dapper Bear''' is a [[Quest Givers|Quest Giver]], and one of the eight permanent quest [[Bears]] that can be accessed in the game, the others being [[Black Bear]], [[Brown Bear]], [[Mother Bear]], [[Panda Bear]], [[Science Bear]], [[Polar Bear]] and [[Spirit Bear]]. Dapper Bear is located inside his [[Dapper Bear’s Shop|shop]] next to [[Stump Field]].

    Inside Dapper Bear's shop, he sells many different [[planter]]s ranging from{{Cost|Honey|750K-100T}} (alongside [[crafting]] materials). He also sells many different types of [[Beequip]]s for {{Cost|Honey}} and {{Cost|Ticket}}, with some being locked behind a certain amount of Dapper Bear Quests completed.

    After speaking with him, Dapper Bear rewards the player {{Cost|Ticket Planter|3}} and a {{Cost|Beequip Case|||0}} (if the player does not already have one).

    ==Requirements== You need the following Accessories to enter Dapper Bear's shop, those being the [[Hiking Boots]] and the [[Propeller Hat]]. Players can also use gear that is better than the ones previously listed.

    ==Quests== Dapper Bear currently has 16 Quests. His 15 main quests focus on collecting nectar, collecting tokens from planters, and popping [[Puffshroom|Puffshrooms]], and the one quest is for obtaining the {{Cost|Blue Drive|4=1}} in order to repair [[Robo Bear]].
