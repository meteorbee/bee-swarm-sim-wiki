    Spirit Bear

    {{Bear|image = Spirit bear333.png |type = Quest Bear |location = In between the Coconut Field and the Pepper Patch, in front of the Extreme Memory Match. |bees = 35 |basecoloramount= 2 |basecolor1 =#6f6f6d |basecolor2 =#5d5e5b |shadecoloramount = 1 |shadecolor1 =#70706e |snoutcoloramount = 1 |snoutcolor1 =#393a35 |torsocolor1 =#939586 |torsocoloramount = 1 |armscolor =#656663 |legscolor =#555653 |enableother = no}} '''Spirit Bear''' is a [[Quest Givers|quest giver]] and is one of the eight permanent quest [[bears]] that can be accessed in the game, the others being [[Black Bear]], [[Brown Bear]], [[Mother Bear]], [[Panda Bear]], [[Science Bear]], [[Dapper Bear]], and [[Polar Bear]]. She holds a [[Petal Wand]] and wears a [[Petal Belt]]. The player must have 35 [[bees]] or more to get access Spirit Bear. She is located between the [[Coconut Field]] and the [[Pepper Patch]], and in front of the [[Memory Match#Extreme Memory Match|Extreme Memory Match]], she has a total of 30 quests she can give to players, no repeatable quests.

    Her [[Quests]] are one of the hardest quests to complete and take much longer to complete than the ones given by [[Science Bear]] or [[Onett]], as the later quests will require billions of [[Pollen]], Grandmaster [[Badges]], and huge amounts of [[Ability Tokens|Tokens]] collected. The main rewards from her quests are [[Spirit Petal]]s, which are granted every ten quests. In total, the player can obtain three, one to donate to the [[Wind Shrine]] (to get the [[Windy Bee]] or a [[Star Treat]] if the player already obtained a [[Windy Bee]] through buying it in an expired robux pack), another for the [[Petal Wand]], and another for the [[Petal Belt]].

    == Trivia == * She is one of the two female bears in the game, the other being [[Mother Bear]]. * You can get to her without 35 bees by using the [[Red Cannon]], then using the [[Parachute]] or [[Glider]] to glide over, then make sure you have high ping so you are able to talk to her before you get kicked out of the 35 bee zone. * Her idle animation comes from the [https://www.roblox.com/bundles/48/Elder-Animation-Package Elder Animation Package]. * She quite often refers to her sister, another bear who was very close to her. * She, [[Stick Bug]], the [[Cub Buddy|Cub Buddies]], Bee Bear and [[Shadow Bear]] are the only NPCs that dance. * She is the second NPC to hold a tool, the first being [[Onett]], which makes her the only bear holding a tool. * She is the third NPC to wear a belt, the other two being [[Sun Bear]] and [[Onett]]. ** Spirit Bear, Sun Bear, and Dapper Bear are the only [[Bears]] to wear accessories, Spirit Bear and Sun Bear being the only bears to wear [[:Category:Accessories|in-game accessories]]. * Spirit Bear is based off of [[Wikipedia:Kermode bear|Kermode Bears]], which are sometimes referred as spirit bears. ** Kermode bears are a rare subspecies of the black bear. * She mentions the [[Bee Swarm Simulator Wiki]] in the quest "Spirit's Starter". * Both she and [[Science Bear]] have [[items]] that are only obtainable from their quests, which are [[Spirit Petal]]s and [[Translator]]s respectively. * The [[Codes|Code]] "BeesBuzz123" is her account's password on her own website, proven from her dialogue in "QR Quest". * She, [[Panda Bear]], [[Black Bear]], [[Gummy Bear]] and [[Science Bear]] are the only bears that mention/have a code. * It is possible to glitch into Spirit Bear and get a quest from her. The player has to jump on the [[Windy Bee]] statue on top of the [[Windy Bee Gate|35 Bee Gate]] and glide or jump to her, or, if the player has enough ping, climb up the big rock left of the gate and nearest to the [[Coconut Field]] and talk to her. * She has the most words per quest in her dialogue, at around 2,000 per quest, even surpassing Science Bear with the most words per quest dialogue. ** She has around 21 thousand words in her whole dialogue. * She and [[Dapper Bear]] are the only permanent quest bears to not have a [[Ability Tokens#Bear Morph|Bear Morph]]. * All of Spirit Bear's quests require collecting an increasing amount of white pollen. (With the exception of her Beesmas quests.) * Spirit Bear is the second bear to require the completion of another bear's quest, the first being [[Bee Bear]] and [[Sun Bear]] being third. * Spirit Bear is the only quest giver that can summon [[Nighttime]], a [[Wild Windy Bee]], and a [[Snowstorm]]. However, Spirit Bear, along with [[Honey Bee (NPC)|Honey Bee]], are the only quest givers that can summon a [[Honeystorm]]. ** According to [[Bee Bear]], [[Bubble Bee Man]] can also summon honeystorms.[[Honeystorm]] * She is self-aware that she is in a game, as she mentions "game balance'' in ''Sway Away." ** This could be because of multiple reasons, such as her saying "Ol' Stumpy", her mentioning the wiki you are viewing this on, or her age. * She is the only permanent quest giver that requires the player to play [[Memory Match]]es. * She is the only permanent quest giver that requires the player to use the [[Wealth Clock]] for her quests. * Spirit Bear refers to the Stump Snail as ''Ol' Stumpy''.
