    System Page

    ''This page is for stats that apply to the player, collecting [[pollen]], and the hive. For stats that apply to [[bees]], see [[Stats]].'' [[File:SystemPageIcon.png|thumb|The icon for the System Page.]][[File:1-12-2024 update settings menu.png|thumb|A small part of the System Page.]] The '''System Page''' is shown by clicking the cog in the menu bar. It is in between the [[Badges]] page and the [[Robux Shop]] page. Here, the player can redeem [[codes]], send [[Trading|Trade Requests]], view the [[Leaderboards|Global Leaderboard]], use the [[Cub Console]], turn on and off in-game [[music]], toggle Pollen Text, Abbreviate Pollen, allow Trade Requests, display and hide other players' [[bees]], toggle Reducing Strobing Vfx, and toggle Auto-Jelly. Turning off all in-game music only silences the music, not the sound effects (such as the sounds of [[Ability Tokens]] and obtaining [[Bees/Legendary|Legendary]], [[Bees/Mythic|Mythic]], [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]], and [[Bees/Event|Event]]). You can mute all sound by turning off the in-game sound on the Roblox menu.

    == Character Stats == The tab also lists many of the player's active stat values, though not all of them. The following values are listed and explained: === Convert Amount === Boosts the base conversion of your bees before multipliers by a certain amount.
    Example: A [[Basic Bee]] with 20 Convert Amount will convert {{Cost|Honey|100||0}} in 4 seconds instead of {{Cost|Honey|80||0}} in 4 seconds.
    It can be boosted with [[amulet]]s, the [[B.B.M. Mask]], the [[Honey Mask]] and certain [[:Category:Belts|belts]]. This stat was formerly named 'Conversion Amount' until it was changed in the [[Updates#2019.12.23|Beesmas 2019 update]].

    ===Convert Rate === Boosts your bees conversion by the following percentage.
    Example: A Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate will convert {{Cost|Honey|160||0}} in 4 seconds instead of {{Cost|Honey|80||0}} in 4 seconds.
    It can be boosted with amulets, {{Cost|Enzymes|||0}}, {{Cost|Marshmallow Bee|||}}, {{Cost|Super Smoothie|||}}, [[Buffs & Debuffs#From NPCs|Science Enhancement]], the Conversion Buff, the [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Other|Honeyday Event buff]], the [[Badges#Honey Badge|Honey Badge]], [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]] [[Diamond Bee]], the Yellow [[Jelly Beans|Jellybean]], Jellybean Sharing Bonus, certain [[Sticker|Stickers]] in the [[Sticker Stack]], some [[Beequip|beequips]] and most [[Bags]]. This stat was formerly named 'Conversion Rate' until it was changed in the Beesmas 2019 update.

    ===Convert Rate At Hive=== Boosts your bees’ conversion by the following percentage at the [[hive]]. This stat can stack with Convert Rate.
    Example: A level 1 Basic Bee with 200% Convert Rate and 200% Hive Convert Rate will convert {{Cost|Honey|320||0}} in 4 seconds while converting at the hive, but it will only convert {{Cost|Honey|160||0}} outside of the hive.
    It can be increased with the [[Badges#Pumpkin Badge|Pumpkin Badge]], Bubble Bloat and certain items. This buff was used to be "Hive Convert Rate" until the 12/26/21 update.

    ===Bonus Convert Amount=== Increases the base conversion of the player's bees. It can be increased by certain amulets and items. ===[[Loot Luck]]=== Loot Luck is a passive attribute that increases the chances of receiving rewards from defeated [[mobs]].
    It can be increased with [[Ability Tokens#Baby Love|Baby Love]], the [[Badges#Clover Badge|Clover Badge]], [[Baby Bee|Baby Bee’s]] Gifted Hive Bonus and certain items.
    This stat was formerly named 'Luck' until it was changed in the [[Updates#2019.04.05|4/5/2019 update]].

    ===[[Critical Hits#Critical Chance|Critical Chance]]=== The chance of your bees hitting stronger or collecting more pollen. More accurately - by how big your Critical Power is.
    Example: A Basic Bee with 50% Critical Chance will hit with increased damage half of the time.
    It can be boosted with amulets, {{Cost|Tropical Drink|||}}, [[Ability Tokens#Focus|Focus]], the [[Jelly Beans|Green Jellybean]], Gifted [[Commander Bee]] and certain items. Currently, the highest critical chance a player can have at a time is 88%.

    ===[[Critical Hits#Critical Power|Critical Power]]=== This stat multiplies how strong a Critical Hit will be. It applies to fighting and pollen collecting.
    Example: Basic Bee with 300% Critical Power (on critical hit) will hit with the power of 3 Basic Bees (3 damage).
    It can be boosted with the Ant Amulet, [[Ability Tokens#Melody|Melody]], the [[Badges#Battle Badge|Battle Badge]], Gifted [[Tabby Bee]], Gifted [[Looker Bee]] and with certain items. The calculated max Critical Power is 695%.

    ===[[Critical Hits#Super-Crit Chance|Super-Crit Chance]]=== The possibility for a critical hit to Super-Crit. Super-Crits deals 33% more damage and collect twice as much pollen as a regular critical hit, and are instantly converted. When a hit is a Super-Crit, there will be a purple color indicating the damage and the '''!''' sign next to it. It can be boosted by having a Gifted Precise Bee, Tabby Blessing, Precision, a precise mark, the Bruteforce upgrade in the [[Robo Bear Challenge]], purchasing Bloxiade in the [[Retro Swarm Challenge]], or using a {{Cost|Super Smoothie|||0}}. The max possible Super-Crit Chance you can have at a time is 53% (assuming you are in the Robo Bear Challenge).

    ===[[Critical Hits#Super-Crit Power|Super-Crit Power]]=== The amount pollen is multiplied by when performing Super-Crits (after applying Critical Power). Super-crit attacks are only increased by 33% as much as Pollen Super-crits. It can only be boosted by the [[Whistle]] and Kazoo Beequips, a [[Cog Amulet]] or temporarily boosted by the [[Dark Scythe]].

    === Bee Ability Rate=== Increases the speed at which the player's bees create [[Ability Tokens]].
    Example: If it takes a bee about 20 seconds to produce a token, with 20% Bee Ability Rate, the bee is likely to take ~16 seconds to produce the token.
    Bee ability rate can be boosted by Star Amulets, Motivating Nectar, certain masks, items, bee mutations, and Beequips.

    ===[[Pollen]]=== Increases pollen from all sources (both from [[bees]] and [[:Category:Tools|tools]]).
    Example: With 100% pollen, the player can collect 1,000 pollen, and with 110% pollen, it will able to collect 1,100 pollen from the same amount of time and [[flowers]].
    It can be increased with amulets, [[Ability Tokens#Bear Morph|Bear Morph]], [[Ability Tokens#Inspire|Inspire]], Baby Love, [[Ability Tokens#Mark|Pollen Marks]], the [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Other|Honeyday Event buff]], from [[Clouds]], Gifted Basic Bee, Gifted Bear Bee, the [[Jelly Beans|Periwinkle Jellybean]] or with certain items.

    ===White Pollen=== Boosts your white pollen collection. It can be increased with amulets, {{Cost|Tropical Drink|||}}, {{Cost|Marshmallow Bee|||}}, [[Ability Tokens#Boost|White Boost]], the [[Badges#Spider Badge|Spider Badge]], the [[Badges#Coconut Badge|Coconut Badge]], Gifted [[Shocked Bee]], the [[Jelly Beans|White Jellybean]], Wind Boosts, or with certain items.

    ===Red Pollen=== Boosts your red pollen collection. It can be increased with amulets, {{Cost|Red Extract|||}}, [[Ability Tokens#Boost|Red Boost]], [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Passives|Flame Heat]], the [[Badges#Strawberry Badge|Strawberry Badge]], Gifted Rad Bee or with certain items.

    ===Blue Pollen=== Boosts your blue pollen collection. It can be increased with Amulets, {{Cost|Blue Extract|||}}, [[Ability Tokens#Boost|Blue Boost]], with the [[Badges#Bamboo Badge|Bamboo Badge]], Gifted Cool Bee or with certain items.

    ===Bomb Pollen=== Boosts your pollen collection from all bombs.
    Example: If the player collects 5,000 pollen with a Buzz Bomb token, with 200% Bomb Pollen they will collect 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.
    It can be increased with Amulets, [[Buffs & Debuffs#From Ability Tokens|Bomb Combos]], Gifted Bomber Bee, Festive Mark, Black Jelly Bean or with certain items.

    ===Buzz Bomb Pollen=== Boosts your pollen collection from Buzz Bombs and [[Ability Tokens#Fuzz Bombs|Fuzz Bombs]]. It can be increased with amulets, Gifted Demo Bee, or with certain items.

    === Red Bomb Pollen === Boosts your pollen collection from Red Bombs. It can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Rascal Bee, Festive Mark or with certain items.

    ===Blue Bomb Pollen=== Boosts your pollen collection from Blue Bombs. It can be increased with Amulets, Gifted Frosty Bee, or with certain items.

    ===[[Bubble#Bubble Pollen|Bubble Pollen]]=== Boosts your pollen collection from [[bubble]]s.
    Example: If the player collects 5,000 pollen with a bubble, with 200% Pollen from Bubbles they will collect 10,000 pollen from the same flowers.
    It can be increased with Gifted [[Bubble Bee]], [[Cog Amulet]], [[Bubble Mask]] or the [[Diamond Mask]].

    ===[[Flame#Flame Pollen|Flame Pollen]]=== Boosts your pollen collection from [[flame]]s created by bees, [[Ability Tokens|ability tokens]], and [[Passive Abilities|passive abilities]].
    Example: If the flames collect 1,000 over time, with 200% Pollen from Flames they will collect 2,000 pollen from the same flowers.
    It can be increased with Gifted [[Fire Bee]], [[Cog Amulet]], or the [[Demon Mask]].

    ===Bee Gather Pollen=== Boosts pollen collection for all bees.
    Example: With 200% Bee Gather Pollen, Basic Bee will collect 20 pollen in 4 seconds.
    It can be increased with amulets, {{Cost|Glue|||}}, Gifted [[Music Bee]], the [[Robux Shop|x2 Pollen from Bees Gamepass]] or with certain items.

    ===Tool Pollen=== Boosts your pollen collection from your tool.
    Example: With 100% (base) Pollen From Tools, the player will collect 1,000 pollen with their tool at once. With 125% Pollen From Tools player will collect 1,250 pollen with their tool at once at the same place.
    It can be increased with amulets, {{Cost|Glue|||}}, Gifted [[Carpenter Bee]], with the [[Badges#Stump Badge|Stump Badge]] or with certain items.

    ===[[Instant Conversion]]=== Instantly converts your pollen when you are collecting pollen on a field.
    Example: a Basic Bee with 50% Instant Conversion that collects 10 pollen will make half of it instantly convert into {{Cost|Honey|||0}}. You will have {{Cost|Honey|5||0}} and 5 pollen, so you can collect more pollen.
    It can be boosted with amulets, {{Cost|Enzymes|||}}, the [[Badges#Dandelion Badge|Dandelion Badge]], the [[Badges#Cactus Badge|Cactus Badge]], Gifted [[Photon Bee]], [[Ability Tokens#Festive Blessing|Festive Blessing]] buff, or with certain items.

    ===Instant White Conversion=== Instantly converts white pollen you collect, in addition to Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the [[Badges#Pineapple Badge|Pineapple Badge]], Gifted [[Windy Bee]], or with the [[Coconut Canister]].

    ===Instant Red Conversion=== Instantly converts red pollen you collect, in addition to Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, Flame Heat, the [[Badges#Mushroom Badge|Mushroom Badge]], Gifted [[Crimson Bee]], or with certain items.

    ===Instant Blue Conversion=== Instantly converts blue pollen you collect, in addition to Instant Conversion. It can be boosted with amulets, the [[Badges#Blue Flower Badge|Blue Flower Badge]], Gifted [[Cobalt Bee]], [[Diamond Mask]], or with certain items.

    ===Instant Bomb Conversion=== Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's bomb. It can be temporarily boosted by standing in a [[Ability Tokens#Festive Mark|festive mark]], or can be permanently increased by owning a Gifted [[Demon Bee]].

    ===[[Flame#Instant Flame Conversion|Instant Flame Conversion]]=== Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's summoned flames. It can be boosted by having the Demon Mask equipped, or you can temporarily get 50% Instant Flame Conversion by triggering the [[Passive Abilities#Scorching Star|Scorching Star Passive]].

    ===[[Bubble#Instant Bubble Conversion|Instant Bubble Conversion]]=== Instantly converts pollen collected by the player's popped bubbles. There are currently no ways to obtain it.

    ===[[Honey Per Pollen]]=== Allows you to make more {{Cost|Honey|||0}} from the same amount of pollen, which works on conversion, [[Instant Converter]]s, {{Cost|Micro-Converter|||}}, and Instant Conversion.
    Example: With 200% {{Cost|Honey|||0}} Per Pollen, a player will make 2,000 {{Cost|Honey|||0}} from 1,000 Pollen.
    It can be increased with the [[Moon Amulet]], the [[Wealth Clock]], the [[Badges#Goo Badge|Goo Badge]], Gifted [[Gummy Bee]] or Nectar.

    ===Honey At Hive=== Increases the {{Cost|Honey|||0}} you convert when converting at hive. You can boost this stat with Satisfying Nectar and Balloon Blessing. [[Diamond Bee]]'s Shimmering {{Cost|Honey|||0}} directly multiplies this stat.

    === Movespeed=== Movespeed of your character in studs. It can be increased with amulets, {{Cost|Oil|||0}}, [[Ability Token#Haste|Haste or Haste+]], [[Ability Token#Bear morph|Bear Morph,]] [[Passive Abilities#Coconut Haste|Coconut Haste]], Gifted [[Hasty Bee]], or with [[:Category:Boots|boots]].

    ===Movement Collection=== Allows the player to collect [[pollen]] when walking on [[flowers]]. The player can get this boost from wearing [[:Category:Boots|boots]] or from a [[Moon Amulet]]. The higher the movement collection, the greater amount of pollen the player collects from walking on flowers. This boost also collects [[goo]] if the player walks on any goo patches.

    ===Jump Power === Jump power of your character in studs. It can be increased with [[Ability Token#Bear Morph|Bear Morph,]] all boots (except for the [[Basic Boots]]), or the [[Propeller Hat]]. The minimum jump power is 55 and the maximum jump power is 97.

    ===Defense=== Reduces the damage that you take.
    Example: If a mob deals 50 damage, with 50% Defense it will deal 25 damage instead.
    It can be increased with the [[Shell Amulet]], [[:Category:Hats|hats]], or with [[:Category:Guards|guards]]. Having 100% defense means that you take no damage and can be achieved by activating the [[Passive Abilities#Emergency Coconut Shield|emergency coconut shield]] passive. The maximum defense without the emergency coconut shield is 60%. This is achieved by having the [[Demon Mask]] or [[Diamond Mask]] (+35%), both the [[Crimson Guard|Crimson]] and [[Cobalt Guard]]s (+10% each), and +5% on a Shell Amulet. Defense was originally Damage Reduction.

    ===Bond From Treats=== Gives additional [[bond]] for your bees when you give them [[treats]].
    Example: If you feed 100 normal treats (1,000 total bond) to your bee, with 125% Bond From Treats it will grant 1,250 bond instead.

    ===Bond From Battle=== Gives additional bond for your bees when you are defeating a [[mob]].
    Example: Normally, [[scorpion]]s give 15 bond for each of your bees when you defeat it. With 150% Bond From Battle, your bees will receive 22.5 Bond instead.

    ===Honey From Tokens=== Boosts {{Cost|Honey|||1}} that you receive from all {{Cost|Honey|||1}} giving tokens (Honey Gift, Honey Gift+, [[Ability Tokens#Impale|Impale]], mob loot, [[Passive Abilities#Coin Scatter|Coin Scatter]], [[Honeystorm]], and others).

    === Goo Conversion=== Instantly converts pollen equal to the goo gathered, which can convert all the pollen gathered (effectively serving as 100% Instant Conversion) with a high enough Goo Conversion % and Goo multiplyer. It can be increased by amulets, Gummy Mask, Gummy Morph, or Gummy Star.

    ===[[Capacity|Capacity Multiplier]]=== Gives additional pollen storage by the amount of the percentages.
    Example: If your backpack can hold up to 200,000 pollen, with 200% Capacity Multiplier, your backpack can hold up to 400,000 instead.

If your capacity multiplier is a decimal percentage, it doesn't show the decimal and rounds the number, but the rounding doesn't affect your capacity.

    ===[[Field Capacity|White Field Capacity]]=== Gives additional pollen storage by the number of percentages based on the percentage of white flowers in a field.

    ==Auto-Jelly Settings== [[File:3CDE71CC-E05C-4EAA-A98E-BD3E71C85BCB.jpeg|thumb|right|The Auto-Jelly settings.]] [[File:AutoJellyReminder.png|thumb|The message when an auto-jelly setting is on.]] The '''Auto-Jelly Settings''' can be found at the bottom of the System Page. There are options for {{Cost|Royal Jelly|||}} or {{Cost|Star Jelly|||}} to auto roll until the requirements are fulfilled. It can be used to help players obtain a bee they desire quickly. There are options for the requirement the jelly will roll into:

    Until Legendary: Jellies will auto roll until you get a [[Bees/Legendary|Legendary]] or [[Bees/Mythic|Mythic]] Bee. Until Mythic: Jellies will auto roll until you get a Mythic Bee. Require Gifted: Jellies will auto roll until you get a [[Gifted Bee|Gifted]] or Mythic Bee. Require Mutation: Jellies will auto roll until you get a [[mutation|Mutated]] or Mythic Bee. Multiple options can be turned on at a time, whilst also making the auto jelly stop on the combined requirement.
    Example: When the Legendary and Gifted options are turned on, the jellies will auto roll until you get a Gifted Legendary Bee. When the Legendary, Gifted, and Mutation options are turned on, the jellies will auto roll until you get a Gifted Legendary Bee with a Mutation.

    If the Until Legendary option or both the Until Legendary and Until Mythic options are turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Legendary Bee or Mythic Bee. If only the Require Gifted option is turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Bee or a Mythic Bee. If only the Require Mutation option is turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Bee or a Mutated Bee. If both the Require Gifted and Require Mutation options are turned on, it will auto roll until you get a Gifted Mutated Bee or Mythic Bee.

    If the Mutation option is turned on, but the bee you selected isn't radioactive, a pop-up after using a {{Cost|Royal Jelly|||0}} will say that you can't auto-roll {{Cost|Royal Jelly|||}} for mutation. Unless the bee is radioactive, you will have to use the {{Cost|Royal Jelly|||}} one at a time.

    When using a {{Cost|Royal Jelly|||0}} on a bee with an auto-jelly option enabled, a reminder appears after the message that the auto-jelly option is on. [[File:Extremelyrareautojelly.png|thumb|The extra warning pop-up when selecting extremely rare options.]] If you turn on the following options, an extra pop-up will warn the player that the options they selected are very rare: Mythic and Gifted. Mythic and Mutation. Mythic, Gifted, and Mutation. Legendary, Gifted, and Mutation. [[File:Autostarjelly.png|thumb|An extra warning pop-up that appears when you try to auto-jelly with star jellies.]] When using a {{Cost|Star Jelly|||1}} on a bee, another pop-up will appear, reminding the player that an auto-jelly setting is on and will use as many star jellies as it needs in order to fulfill its requirement. This pop-up will appear even if only the Until Gifted option is turned on. If you are using the Require Gifted option with {{Cost|Star Jelly|||}}, you will still receive the warning, but nothing will happen.
